## Notes

`.github/workflows/ci.yml` and `.github/actions/setup/action.yml` were written again from nothing. Its jobs are `changes` (what the change reaches, and gitleaks over the commits it brings), `rust` (fmt, clippy, obid's limits and every test compiled once, or the guards alone), `rust-test` (4 shards), `electron` and `Check`, the one required check. The same jobs run on every push to a pull request, a draft included, in the merge queue and on `dev` after each merge, the only run that saves caches.

The old workflow (984 lines, 13 jobs), `.github/workflows/cli-canary.yml`, `scripts/ci-rust-tests.sh` and its tests, and `docs/ci-architecture.md` were deleted; git history keeps them. CI-1 to 6, 14 to 20, 57 to 65, 67 and 68 left the contract with them.

The Mac's check (`scripts/oby-check.sh`) and `scripts/preflight.sh` are unchanged and still run what they ran, obid-spec, the Brain and the route guard among it.
