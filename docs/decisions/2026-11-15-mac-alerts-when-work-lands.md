## Notes

With many agents working at once, the owner wants to know when work lands and when an account stops, without watching the window. On the Mac, a task of his that ends done with nothing to decide, its pull request that merges, and an account that reaches its limit each raise a quiet banner with no buttons, not counted on the Dock's badge, each with its own switch in Settings › Notifications, all three on by default. What needs the owner alerts whatever the switches (ALERTS-16), and a job at a limit still moves or waits with no ask (ALERTS-6, LIMITS-15).

The phones keep the rule of 2026-10-28: the iPhone, Android and the Watch are told only what waits on the owner, and none of these three kinds is pushed, sealed or wakes Oby (ALERTS-17).
