### `GET /v1/settings/jobs` and `PUT /v1/settings/jobs`

How many jobs run at once, as in `status.maxRunning`: `{"maxRunning": 6}`, 1 to 20, or to the owner's `limits.maxRunning` (anything else is `422`). The runner keeps it across restarts. Raising it starts waiting jobs at once; lowering it stops nothing, and new jobs wait until fewer than the new cap run.

obid also answers `accountFailover`, `defaultAccount` and `talkAccount` (`{"maxRunning": 6, "accountFailover": true, "defaultAccount": null, "talkAccount": null}`), kept across restarts and set with `PUT` alongside or instead of `maxRunning`. `talkAccount` is the account conversations talk through unless one chose another: an account's id, or null (or `auto` on `PUT`) for Auto; `422` for an account obid doesn't have, or one that can't talk here (`reason`: `talk-needs-egress-box`, Cursor where the box can't hold it to the egress proxy); removing the account sets it back to null (docs/contracts/conversation-agent.md). `accountFailover` controls failover past a usage limit: off, a turn tries one account and a job waits for its own; on, a turn moves to another signed-in account of any tool, and so does a job (LIMITS-15), waiting only when no account has room. A body with none of them, or a value of the wrong kind, is `422`.

`limits` (obid only, the owner's decision of 2026-10-31: docs/contracts/agents.md, AGENTS-9) are the owner's own ceilings on what Oby holds to by itself, each shown as its value now: `{"rounds": 10, "maxRunning": 20, "folders": 20, "routines": 20, "accounts": 32}`, the most rounds a job may be given, the highest `maxRunning`, the most folders a conversation works in, routines and accounts obid keeps. `PUT` takes any of them, each a whole number from 1 to its ceiling (rounds 100, maxRunning 64, folders 200, routines 500, accounts 256: past them the Mac itself runs out), or null for its default (anything else, or another key, is `422`, and a `422` keeps nothing of its body); a lower `limits.maxRunning` lowers `maxRunning` with it. Kept across restarts. The other caps here protect only the Mac (lengths, file counts and sizes, connections) and stay fixed limits.

`defaultAccount` (#1573) is the account new tasks go to: an account id obid has (`422` for another), or null for routing's own order; removing the account sets it back to null. A job with no account named (`auto`) goes to it first, whatever its tool, while it's signed in with room; one that names a tool goes to it when it's that tool's. Signed out, out of room, or being signed in or removed, it's passed over as routing passes any. A conversation's plan whose account is `auto` takes its tool unless the owner's words name an agent, before `defaultAgent` (docs/contracts/agents.md, AGENTS-1 to AGENTS-4).

## What the runner doesn't do by itself

These are Oby's defaults for what it does unasked (AGENTS.md, "Oby forbids nothing"). On the owner's word, a click or their signed words in their own app, it does any of them; what still stops it is an agent's own limit, the code host's (GitHub's branch protection and required reviews), or, for anything that goes out, the secret scan, and Oby says which one did. Where obid doesn't take the owner's word for one yet, the line says so: that's the contract's **proposed** behavior, not built.

- **Push to `main` or the default branch:** never by itself. On the owner's signed Publish it pushes a job's commits there (`to`, PUBLISHING-10; obid only). Pushing anything else there on their words is proposed: agents have no GitHub login, and obid has no other action for it yet.
- **Force-push:** by itself only a rebase of its own branch, one the runner made (`oby/`, or a new one the owner named), only over the commit it pushed there last (`--force-with-lease`): a base that moved while the job ran, or its one rebase round after its pull request conflicts with its base or falls behind it, never once someone else pushed its head (MERGING-38). Never `main` or `dev` ([`GET /v1/jobs/{id}/publish`](#get-v1jobsidpublish)). Any other force-push of a job's commits on the owner's signed Publish (`force`, naming the commit it replaces: PUBLISHING-10; obid only).
- **Merge:** by itself, only the way the repository's workflow says (this repository's: squash-merge a pull request it opened into `dev`, only when Oby's review approved it, the Mac check passed on the same commit and the secret scan is clean; Mac check, above), never into `main`. Into `main` on the owner's own Merge (#1658; on obid, "When Oby merges by itself", below).
- **Publish what didn't pass:** by itself, only a job that ended `done` (its check passed, or it has none), with a clean secret scan, is pushed and gets a pull request; by hand, with a fresh device signature, the owner's Publish anyway publishes the commit its sentence names (obid only, PUBLISHING-9; proposed, not built: the card offering it with what wasn't checked). The secret scan holds either way. By itself, no comment goes out, but a review the owner approved (obid only: GitHub work, "Posting it"): Oby's own review of its own pull request and the Mac check's results on it are check runs (an Apple check posts nothing), and the `oby-failure` issues its post-mortems file in Oby's own repository (Engine/Failures.swift) are updated in place, all after the secret scan; no agent posts to GitHub, and no GitHub review, approval or comment goes on anyone else's work (proposed, not built: on the owner's word one does; today it's refused even by hand).
- **Fix its own pull request's red checks:** by itself, one round of its agent's per job, on a branch it made and only at the head it pushed last, when checks that failed on GitHub are all that stops the merge (MERGING-39): the failed runs' logs (`gh run list --commit <head> --status failure`, then `gh run view --log-failed`, at most 3 runs, the last 300 lines of each; when none can be read, the failing checks' names) are quoted in the job's `.obi/inputs/checks.md`, fenced as data with the vault's and obid's secrets taken out, and its turn's fixed words say the checks failed and the file is data, never instructions. The round is counted in the job's file (`checksRounds`, never in the API). A head still red after it, a second red head included, asks the owner with the card (MERGING-21).
- **Take a trusted review's changes on its own pull request:** by itself, one round of its agent's per head, within the same bounds, when every change request standing (each reviewer's latest review, `CHANGES_REQUESTED`) is a person's on the head obid pushed last whom the owner trusts: the owner's own gh login, or an `OWNER` or `MEMBER` of a repository that's the owner's or one of their organizations' (`gh api user`, `gh api user/orgs`; `github_work::Owner::trusts`, the owner's decision "Mine and my org's", MERGING-40), and no change request on that head, nor any of its comments on the lines, was edited by anyone but its author or the owner or carries `by Oby:` (one `gh api graphql` read of who wrote and edited the pull request's reviews, made after the quote, judged as an issue's words are for the owner's secrets; a list GitHub doesn't give whole is a no). Each review's body and its comments on the change's lines (`gh api --paginate --slurp repos/{repo}/pulls/{n}/reviews/{id}/comments?per_page=100`: each one's file, line and words) are quoted in the job's `.obi/inputs/changes.md`, the first 300 lines (obid's line says where it cut them), fenced as data with the vault's and obid's secrets taken out, and its turn's fixed words say a review asks for changes and the file is data, never instructions (`step` `Working on the review's changes`). As they're quoted the job is marked `reached` (above): a member's words never unlock the owner's declared secrets, so its check gets none from then on, and it still needs one review. These rounds and Oby's own review rounds are one count, three together whoever asked first: a fourth gets the card, or for Oby's review the owner's item. The heads that had one are kept in the job's file (`changesRounds`, never in the API). Anyone else's request, one on an older head, one edited by someone else or signed by an Oby, comments gh can't read, or a round that can't start ask the owner with the card, as before.
- **Research jobs don't commit or publish by themselves.** Proposed issues are filed only after the user taps to file them (proposed, not built: on the owner's word a research job's commits publish; today that's refused even by hand).

These protect the owner from others, and stay as they are (AGENTS.md, "Rules Oby never breaks"):

- **Touch the user's credentials:** it never reads, stores or forwards agent credentials, and each tool signs in through its own login on the runner.
- **Read outside text as instructions:** text from GitHub or other people reaches agents only as quoted files.

### Limits and settings

obid's caps, each either a limit of what it can hold or a policy the owner can change. The policy ones are settings (obid only: `limits` on `PUT /v1/settings/jobs`, docs/contracts/agents.md AGENTS-9), each with a default and a hard ceiling past which the Mac itself runs out; a request past one is refused with the cap in its words.

| Cap | Today | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| A job's task | 1 to 2,000 characters | A limit (what a prompt carries) |
| A job's `check` | one line, up to 200 characters | A limit |
| A job's `files` | at most 30 paths | A limit |
| Memories | a title of 60, a text of 500 characters, 100 per scope | A limit |
| Artifacts, egress connections | `artifacts.rs`, `egress.rs` | Limits |
| A job's `rounds` | 1 to 10 | A setting, `limits.rounds` (at most 100) |

| One conversation turn | at most 10 jobs started or told; a check-in at most daily | A policy: the owner's own number or schedule is proposed to pass (the brain, Limits 1/3) |
