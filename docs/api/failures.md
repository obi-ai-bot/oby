### Failure record

Oby studies every failure and fixes the cause itself (#139, Engine/Failures.swift). Whenever a job fails, stalls, or someone had to step in, the runner writes a record in `oby.sqlite` with the evidence, kept for good, after the job list forgets the job.

```json
{"id": "ab12cd34-limit-1791582173", "trigger": "limit", "date": "2026-10-09T21:42:53Z", "job": "ab12cd34", "repo": "deskree-inc/oby",
 "title": "Mac runner notifications", "what": "Stopped at a limit: the same failure twice in a row", "rounds": 3, "intervention": false,
 "evidence": {"summary": "…", "step": "Stage 3 of 6, Check: swift test · phase check · round 3 of 4", "check": "`swift test` printed (its end): …",
              "reviews": [], "log": ["2026-10-09 21:42:50 UTC notice [jobs] job ab12cd34 stopped at a limit: …"],
              "worktree": "git status --porcelain:\n?? Engine/Left.swift\n\ngit diff --stat HEAD:\n(nothing)", "source": "b11afca…"},
 "postMortem": {"happened": "…", "cause": "environment", "obyCouldHandle": true, "signature": "simulator-hung-before-connecting",
                "fix": {"files": ["Engine/AppleChecks.swift"], "change": "…"}, "by": "claude Sonnet 5.5", "date": "2026-10-09T21:44:10Z"},
 "tries": 1, "acted": true, "studyError": null, "issue": "https://github.com/deskree-inc/oby/issues/140", "issueError": null, "fix": null,
 "fixedBy": null, "fixedAt": null}
```

- `trigger`: what Oby saw. `failed`, `interrupted` (a restart or quit cut it off) and `limit` (case 3.1); `reviewCap` (Oby's review requested changes for the last time it may), `noVerdict` (the review ended with no verdict, failed or was interrupted), `noReviewer` (no account could review it); `checkError` (the Mac couldn't run a merge or Apple check) and `unnamedFailure` (one failed without naming a test); `publishError` and `mergeError` (not a conflict Oby rebased by itself, Mac check above); `stuck` (it stayed in one step past its own limit: work 10 minutes past the round's, a check 10 minutes past the test run's, publishing 20 minutes, getting ready 30; or a Mac check queued 2 hours, or running past its lease); `brainFallback` (the brain didn't answer and rules planned: `job` is null); and the interventions, `handMerge` (a pull request Oby opened was merged, and Oby never merged it) and `outsideCommit` (its head is a commit Oby didn't push), with `intervention` true. A review job's own end reaches its author as `noVerdict`; a job the user stopped isn't a failure, unless they stopped it while its question waited on them (`stoppedBy` ends `while its question waited`, TASKS-18): then it's `failed`. Each is recorded once, and only while it's news (within a day).
- `what`: what Oby saw, in its own words. `rounds`: the rounds the job had used. `agentVersion` (obid only, add-only): the job's `agentVersion` when the record was written, or null (a record written before obid read versions has none). `evidence`, with the vault's secrets taken out: `summary` (the job's report, or what the user said and why the brain failed), `step` (its stage and step), `check` (the end of what its check printed, and its Mac checks with what they printed, or null), `reviews` (Oby's reviews of it, oldest first), `log` (the runner's log lines of the last hour that name the job, and the last few of all), `worktree` (#221: what the job left in its worktree, `git status --porcelain` and `git diff --stat HEAD`, read at its first post-mortem, so a "nothing committed" failure shows the work left behind; null for the brain or with no worktree) and `source` (the commit of `origin/dev` the last post-mortem read, or null).
- `postMortem`: null until it's studied. A fresh, read-only turn boxed like the Lead's (`claude-sonnet-5-5`, only Read, Grep and Glob, no settings or MCP) reads the evidence as a quoted file and Oby's own source at a freshly fetched `origin/dev` (#221: a detached worktree of the runner's clone of `deskree-inc/oby`, `failures/source` in `OBY_HOME`, reset before each study; never the clone's own working tree, which is on whatever branch a job left it; the evidence names the commit), and answers in a JSON schema: `happened`; `cause`, one of `oby-bug`, `environment`, `agent-mistake`, `account-limit`, `flaky-test`, `feedback-not-converging`, `outside-service`; `obyCouldHandle`; `fix`, the `files` in Oby and the `change`; `signature`, a short kebab-case name for the cause that groups repeats (the turn sees the signatures used before); and `sameAs` (#197), `{"kind": "issue" | "pull-request", "number": N}` or null. Before the turn the runner reads the open `oby-failure` issues (number, signature, one-line cause) and the pull requests merged into `dev` in the last 7 days (with the issues their `Closes #N` lines name), and quotes them in a second file, `.oby/evidence/known.md`; `sameAs` is the one with the same root cause, and Swift drops one it wasn't offered. With an issue, the record's signature is kept as an **alias** of that issue's (in `oby.sqlite`), so later records with either name count, file and offer as one; with a merged pull request, it's an alias of the signature whose issue that pull request closes, when Oby knows it. It runs on the least busy Claude account with room, one record at a time, never holding a job up; with no account, it waits for the next minute's sweep. `tries` counts the turns (3 at most), and `studyError` says why the last one didn't answer.
- **Acting on it** (`acted`): the job's report (`summary`) gets a `What went wrong: <what happened> (cause: <cause>)` paragraph, before the review's follow-ups. When a fix merged into `dev` in the last 7 days already handles the cause (`sameAs` a merged pull request, a fix job of the signature's issue merged, or a merged pull request that closes it), the record is against that fix, `fixedBy` its pull request's URL and `fixedAt` when it merged, and nothing is filed or offered. The 7 days run from the merge, never from the records in between: a cause that still comes back after them is filed again. Otherwise an `oby-bug`, a signature seen twice in 7 days (an `environment` cause 3 times: most were a missing Mac or an old rate limit), or a record `sameAs` an open issue, becomes or updates one open GitHub issue labeled `oby-failure` in `deskree-inc/oby` (created as the GitHub app, after the secret scan of its title and text, with no assignee and no mention; a repeat, or a `sameAs`, brings that issue's body up to date instead of filing a new one, with how many times it was seen and the alias, never a comment, which would email the owner (#198); an issue Oby didn't file keeps its own body), with the post-mortem, the record and the evidence fenced; `issue` is its URL, or `issueError` why it couldn't. It also brings a `failure` inbox item offering `Fix it`, one open per signature; an `environment` cause seen twice brings only the item. When a fix of its cause is already underway (a fix job queued or running, or its pull request open, on the record's issue, its signature's or an alias's: what `Fix it` would refuse), it reaches no inbox (#215): the issue's body counts it and carries its evidence as any repeat, its item arrives `done` saying `Already being fixed by …` with no options, and the signature's open item is done by itself. A `failure` item that's open when a fix job on its cause starts or opens its pull request is done then too (Fix it would be refused), and the runner checks the open ones once after it restarts.
- **Closing** (#197): GitHub honours `Closes #N` only on merges into the default branch (`main`), and Oby merges into `dev`. So when a pull request of Oby's in `deskree-inc/oby` is merged into `dev` (Oby's merge, or one the runner sees merged), within a minute's sweep the runner closes every issue it closes (its job's `issue` and its description's `Closes`/`Fixes`/`Resolves #N`) that's open and labeled `oby-failure`: its body ends with `Fixed by #<pr>, merged into dev as <merge commit>. …` (after the secret scan), then it's closed as completed, never with a comment (#198). Feature issues keep their own lifecycle (#116). The closed issue's records get `fixedBy`, and their open `failure` inbox items are done. When GitHub fails, it's tried again 5 minutes later.
- `fixedBy`: the fix pull request merged into `dev` the record is against, or null; `fixedAt`: when it merged, or null.
- `fix`: the fix job Fix it started, or null. It's a `fix` job in `deskree-inc/oby` (on the account with the most room; the issue's when it has one, so its branch is `oby/fix-<issue>`), checked with `swift test` over up to 4 rounds, told only Swift's words; the post-mortem and the evidence are quoted in `.oby/inputs/failure-<record>.md`, as data.

### `GET /v1/failures?limit=50`

The failure records, newest first, each with its post-mortem (null until studied). `limit` goes up to 1,000.

### `GET /v1/failures/{id}`

One record. `404` for one the runner doesn't know.

### `POST /v1/failures/{id}/fix`

Fix it: starts the fix job (Failure record, above) and answers it, `201`, as `POST /v1/jobs` does; the signature's `failure` inbox items are done. `404` for a record the runner doesn't know; `409` when it isn't studied yet (`"Oby hasn't studied this failure yet."`), no account has room, or the cause is being fixed or is fixed already (#197): on the record's issue, its signature's or an alias's, a job is running (`"Already being fixed by job <id>."`), a pull request of Oby's is open (`"Already being fixed by #<pr>, its pull request open."`), or a fix merged into `dev` in the last 7 days (`"Fixed by #<pr> on dev."`). Then nothing starts.

### obid only: failures

`obid` keeps [Failure records](#failure-record) and serves [`GET /v1/failures`](#get-v1failureslimit50), [`GET /v1/failures/{id}`](#get-v1failuresid) and [`POST /v1/failures/{id}/fix`](#post-v1failuresidfix) with every key above (#445). What is particular to `obid`:

- **What it records.** Each time a job changes, `obid` looks at it as the Swift runner does (Engine/Failures.swift, `found`): a `failed` job is `limit` when it stopped at a limit, `publishError` when its summary starts `Not published:`, and `failed` otherwise; an `interrupted` one, which needs you, is `limit` when it stopped at a limit, `publishError` when its `reason` starts `Not published:`, and `failed` otherwise; a `done` job with `publishError` set is `publishError`; a `failed` job obid's own bug ended, or an `interrupted` one obid kept going down with (below, and RECOVERY-2's fourth), is `crash` (#911). Only while it is news (it ended within a day), and once: the record's id is `<job>-<trigger>-<when it ended, in seconds>`, and a record is never written again. `obid` looks only as a job changes: it has no sweep, and doesn't look at ended jobs again when it starts, so a failure is missed if `obid` stops between keeping the job and writing its record (#571). A job the owner stopped isn't a failure, unless they stopped it while its question waited on them: a `stopped` job whose `stoppedBy` ends `while its question waited` is `failed`, its `what` that `stoppedBy` (TASKS-18). A `done` one with a `reason` (nothing committed, a review that committed) isn't either. The other triggers need what `obid` doesn't run (Oby's review, merging, the minute's sweep, the brain's fallback) and are never recorded: `reviewCap`, `noVerdict`, `noReviewer`, `checkError`, `unnamedFailure`, `mergeError`, `flakyTest`, `stuck`, `brainFallback`, and the interventions `handMerge` and `outsideCommit` (a person merging is how `obid` works, never an intervention). `intervention` is always false.
- **An agent that ends without saying why (#1648).** It goes on by itself first while it has a session and tries (docs/contracts/recovery.md, RECOVERY-1). Past them, or with no session, its job's `reason` is `<Tool> stopped without finishing (<how it exited>).` and the end of what that run printed on stderr (`agent.log`, which keeps every run), at most 500 characters, masked first as a transcript entry is (every secret obid knows, then each line the secret scan flags as `[redacted: <rule>]`). The raw stderr stays in `agent.log` only, never in a job, an event, the inbox or a failure.
- **obid's own bugs (#914, #911).** A panic in a job's task, in an owner's job with a session before publishing began and with tries left (the same 3 as a restart's), doesn't end it: its slot is given back and it's `queued` again, `step` `Oby hit a bug while this ran: continuing it (<n> of 3)`, its session told in a marked message (docs/contracts/recovery.md, RECOVERY-2). Any other panic ends that job, never its slot: it's `failed`, its `reason` and `summary` `obid hit a bug while running this job, so it stopped it: <the panic's first line, at most 300 characters, every secret obid knows taken out>. Its branch and worktree are kept. Try again runs it again.`, its slot is given back first (so a queued job starts in it, whatever happens next), then its login's copy, its agent's MCP configuration, what it waited on and any claim on publishing it go; its agent's whole process group ends, and so does its check's; another Obi's review answers its exchange, failed. The other jobs keep running. Every panic's line in obid's log (its stderr) is `obid: panic at <file:line>: <message>`, the message with every secret obid knows taken out, never Rust's raw line; the hook waits on no lock and refreshes nothing, so when the secrets can't be read at once the message is withheld. Every line obid writes through its log has every secret obid knows taken out by the log's writer, with the values it learned last, and a line it can't redact at once is withheld ([logs.md](contracts/logs.md)). The owner gets one `failed` inbox item about it (one per such end, made once across restarts): its `text` the reason's first line, `options` `Try again` (runs it again as its Try again does, a fresh run when it had no session) and `Dismiss` (dismisses the job). Another Obi's review can't be run again here: its reason ends `The Obi that asked for this review was told it failed.` and its item offers only `Dismiss`. A panic after the job had ended (in what comes after its run) leaves it as it ended, the bug noted in its `changes` (`obid hit a bug after this ended: …`). A publish's claim on the job goes however it ends, a panic or a Publish by hand whose request went included.
- **`agentVersion`** (docs/contracts/cli-versions.md): copied from the job when the record is written: the version of its tool as its latest round started, or null when that couldn't be read or wasn't stamped yet (a job that failed within moments of its round's start). Its file leaves the key out when it's null, and a record written before obid read versions loads with it null.
- **Where.** One file per record, `home/failures/<id>.json` (0600, in a 0700 folder), never pruned. `GET /v1/failures/{id}` takes only ids `obid` makes (letters, digits and dashes): anything else is `404`, like an id it doesn't have (`There's no failure <id>.`). `limit` is a whole number (`422` `limit is a number.` otherwise, as `GET /v1/jobs`), below 0 is none and above 1,000 is 1,000.
- **The evidence**, with every secret `obid` knows taken out (its API token, its Obi key, the Sphere's delegation and token, and the vault's once it is ported, #457: `crate::secrets`), and so are `title` and `what` (a limit names the check's command): `summary` is the end of the job's report (4,000 characters), `step` its stage, phase, step and round, `check` the end of what its last check printed (`home/logs/<job>/check.log`, 3,000 characters) and `Publish: <publishError>`, or null; a `check.log` written before a failure of the job `obid` recorded already is an earlier run's and isn't quoted. `reviews` and `log` are always empty (`obid` runs no review of its own, and keeps no log lines in memory), `worktree` and `source` always null (both are read at the post-mortem).
- **No post-mortem yet.** `obid` runs no post-mortem turn, so `postMortem`, `studyError`, `issue`, `issueError`, `fix`, `fixedBy` and `fixedAt` are null, `tries` is 0 and `acted` false. With nothing studied, nothing is acted on: no `What went wrong:` paragraph in the job's report, no `oby-failure` issue filed, updated or closed on GitHub, no `failure` inbox item, and `POST /v1/failures/{id}/fix` answers `409` `Oby hasn't studied this failure yet.` for every record it has (`404` for one it doesn't). When the post-mortem comes, filing its issue is an outgoing action: it goes through the publish gate (the secret scan on the title, the body and the arguments; nothing goes out if the scanner can't run) with the owner's `gh`, as publishing does. The post-mortem, acting on it and the fix job are #571.
