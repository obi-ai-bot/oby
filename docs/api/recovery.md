### obid only: the brain's records and stale items

A job's, a check's or an inbox item's event starts no model turn: the event orchestrator is retired, and a job ends by obid's own rules (docs/contracts/recovery.md, "End-of-job rules"). Its escalations already in an inbox stay as they were: a `question` item whose id is `orchestrator-` and 12 hexadecimal digits, its one option `Done`, which Done or an answer settles and nothing else carries out.

**Stale items settle (obid only, TASKS-13).** When obid starts, and on every inbox change and every change of a job that isn't queued or running, obid settles (done, with no `answer`) each open item about one of its own jobs (a `question`, `permission`, `plan`, `agentQuestion`, `merge` or `limit` item, or obid's crash or review `failed` item) whose job is gone, or ended let go (`dismissed`) or with its pull request merged or closed (an Oby review's pull request is the one it reviewed, its `reviewOf`'s); and an escalation on an ended job with an open merge card, crash or review `failed` item or limit item, where obid offers that decision now (an escalation beside anything else, a confirm say, stays). A queued or running job's items stay, and so does every item about no job of obid's: an offer, GitHub's, another Obi's, a teammate approval to remove on GitHub. A confirm stays while what it does can still happen; it settles (done, no `answer`, no `options`, a plain note above its `text`) when it can't: a `merge_pr` of a pull request one of obid's jobs published that obid read merged (`Closed by Oby: that pull request was merged since.`), or, read after the card was made, closed (`… was closed since.`) or at another head than the card's (`Closed by Oby: that pull request's head moved since. Ask Oby again.`); a `message_job`, `dismiss_job`, `retry_job`, `continue_job`, `delete_job` or `change_job` whose job is gone (`Closed by Oby: its task is gone.`). A pull request no job of obid's published isn't read, so its confirm stays. The apps hear each in an `inbox` event. A `failed` or `interrupted` job whose pull request (an Oby review's: the one it reviewed) was merged or closed is let go too (`dismissed` set, as Dismiss would, with "Oby let it go: its pull request was merged or closed." added to its `changes`, so it reads as obid's, not the owner's), checked after every read of the pull requests; an Oby review still queued or running (parked on the owner's OK among them) of a pull request merged or closed since is stopped first, then let go the same way, and its items settle; not one with commits that never went out (as `stranded`, it stays the owner's), one an account limit holds (`accountLimit`, the owner's decision), or one obid is publishing. The apps show such a job as needing the owner until it's let go.

`GET /v1/brain/usage` reports the retained brain calls: `since`, total `calls`, `inputTokens`, `outputTokens`, `cacheReadInputTokens`, `cacheCreationInputTokens`, `costUsd`, and the same totals per kind in `kinds`. obid retains the most recent 500 records in `home/brain-turns.jsonl` (0600). New records contain no conversation words; older plan-loop records may retain them until they rotate out. The figures are those reported by the CLIs; fields they don't report contribute zero.
