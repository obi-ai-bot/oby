### obid only: working folders (#1572)

The message box's Select working folder… (Sangam's design, #1564): the owner points Oby at folders on this Mac, kept by the conversation. The contract is docs/contracts/folders.md; what's built so far is the boundary below. Jobs don't work in folders yet: the brain isn't told about them, and words in a conversation with folders start jobs in repositories as before (the contract's "Which kind of folder" and landing come next).

- **Who adds one.** Only the owner's Mac app, over loopback with the API token: through the gateway or the phone relay, `folders` with a path on `POST /v1/plans` is `422` (an empty list is none), and so is a `PATCH` that adds a folder (`Folders are added only on the Mac: from here you can take one off.`). A paired device's `PATCH` may take folders off. Whether adding one also needs the owner's signed words is open (#1572).
- **Every path is checked, every time it's added.** It's absolute, canonicalized (links resolved, `..` gone; the canonical path is what's kept), there, and a folder. It isn't `/`, the person's home folder itself, `/Volumes` itself, inside `~/Library`, inside `/System`, `/Library`, `/Applications`, `/usr`, `/bin`, `/sbin`, `/etc`, `/var`, `/private`, `/opt`, `/cores` or `/dev` (on Linux also `/proc`, `/sys`, `/root`, `/run` and `/boot`), and doesn't hold or sit inside obid's home, an agent's login (`~/.claude`, `~/.codex`, `~/.cursor`, every account's `configDir`) or the person's keys and logins (`~/.ssh`, `~/.config/gh`, `~/.aws`, `~/.gnupg`, `~/.docker`, `~/.kube`), each resolved through links first. A folder on another volume (`/Volumes/X/…`) is fine. A folder in the person's home is checked against that home's own guards (its `Library`, the logins and keys), never against the system folders the home may sit in. The person's homes are the one the password database names and obid's `HOME`; `OBID_PERSON_HOMES` (paths joined as `PATH` joins them) names them instead, for obid's tests, whose folders are made in a home of their own. On macOS paths are compared ignoring case (`~/LIBRARY` is `~/Library`). The `422` names the folder by its name and why (`"Library" is where your Mac keeps its own settings, so Oby doesn't work there.`; words to come from Sangam).
- **What a conversation keeps.** At most 20 folders (the owner's `limits.folders`), in the order picked, the same folder once, in its file (`home/conversations/<id>.json`); more is `422` (`A conversation works in at most 20 folders.`). `POST /v1/plans` adds; `PATCH` replaces, merged with the folders as they are when it's written, so a folder taken off meanwhile comes back only if it passes its own check.
- **What the window shows.** obid's `422`s are the error lines the Electron app shows as they are. Their words are obid's placeholders until Sangam writes his: `"<name>" isn't a folder's full path.`, `"<name>" isn't at <path>.`, `Oby can't read "<name>": macOS hasn't let it in. Allow it in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Files and Folders.`, `"<name>" is a file, not a folder.`, `"<name>" is your whole disk: pick a folder inside it.`, `"<name>" holds your Mac's disks: pick a folder on one.`, `"<name>" is your whole home folder: pick a folder inside it.`, `"<name>" is where your Mac keeps its own settings, so Oby doesn't work there.`, `"<name>" is where your Mac keeps its own files, so Oby doesn't work there.`, `"<name>" is where the system keeps its own files, so Oby doesn't work there.`, `"<name>" holds Oby's own files, so Oby doesn't work there.`, `"<name>" holds an agent's login, so Oby doesn't work there.`, `"<name>" holds your keys or logins, so Oby doesn't work there.`, `"<name>" has a name Oby can't read.`, `A conversation works in at most 20 folders.`, `Folders are added only on the Mac.` and `Folders are added only on the Mac: from here you can take one off.`
