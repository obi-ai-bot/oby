### obid only: asking another Oby from a conversation

The owner asks in a conversation, and what comes back posts there (docs/contracts/ask-an-oby.md). Three of Oby's tools; a conversation in a Sphere is told so in its orientation, which names no one.

- `oby_members` `{}`: the other active members, read afresh: `[{"name", "owner", "takesQuestions"}]`, quoted, and it marks the turn and the conversation, since names are what cards say. `name` is the person's from their card when it reads `<name>'s Oby`, is plainly a name (at most 3 words of one writing system, no `.`) and couldn't be mistaken for another member's name or owner id, or the owner's own; else the owner id.
- `oby_ask` `{to, question}`: `to` means an exact owner id, else a name one member goes by, never an Obi id, which whoever redeems an open invitation picks. On the owner's own word (their app signed the words, the turn read nothing untrusted), for an exact owner id no other card claims, it sends a `question` with the words as `text`, expiring in a day, as `POST /v1/obid/sphere/requests` does, and answers that it asked; the exchange remembers its conversation. On any other turn, it makes a `confirm` item quoting every word, `Ask <owner>'s Oby`; and on any turn for a name, `Ask <name>'s Oby (<owner>)`, or for an owner id another card claims, `Ask <owner>'s Oby, not <other>, whose card goes by that name too`: action `ask_oby`, `target` the Obi, `payload` `{question, conversation, name}`. The secret scan runs first either way: a finding sends nothing and makes no card.
- `oby_asks` `{}`: this conversation's questions, `[{"to", "question", "state", "waitingOnThem", "answeredBy", "answer"}]`, quoted. Reading any of it marks the turn and the conversation, also with no answer yet: `to` is what a member chose.

A refusal says why, in words for the owner, and keeps no exchange: for `policy_denied` from the Sphere, `I can't ask <name>'s Oby. This Obysphere's rules don't allow questions yet. An admin can change that.`; for another of the Sphere's refusals, only its code when it is `[a-z0-9_]{1,40}`; nothing else the Sphere wrote; for the secret scan, what it said; for anything else, `I can't ask <name>'s Oby: nothing was sent.` The tool's words name only what the brain gave in `to`, never who that turned out to be. A Confirm of `ask_oby` that can't send answers `409` with these words, naming the owner id, closes the item, and posts them as Oby's turn in the conversation (`relayed: true`), after any turn under way.

**What comes back** posts as Oby's turn in the conversation (`who` `oby`), once each, after any turn under way there ended, also across a restart, `<name>` being what the owner knew them as when they asked: an answer by the Obi (`by: obi`) is `> <its words>`, a blank line and `Asked <name>'s Oby. <name> wasn't bothered.`; by the person (`by: owner`), `> <their words>`, a blank line and `<name> answered this one, not its Oby.`; a `status` `waiting_on_owner`, `<name>'s Oby hasn't answered. It's waiting on <name>.`; 2 minutes with neither, `<name>'s Oby hasn't answered yet.`; a `decline` by its reason (`owner_declined`: `<name> said no to this one.`; `busy`, `out_of_scope`, `policy`, `expired`, each in its own words, the contract's table; anything else: `<name>'s Oby declined without saying why.`), its `note` quoted first when it has one; expiry, `<name>'s Oby never answered: the question expired.` Every line of the other Oby's words starts `> `, so nothing they wrote reads as obid's own. No model reads them on the way, and they start, change and approve nothing. They never reach a prompt: when a session starts over and hears the conversation's earlier turns, or a briefing does, each turn about another Oby (`relayed: true` on the turn, add-only) is only that there was one.

**A question's card** (`asks` on a turn, add-only, left out when empty; docs/contracts/ask-an-oby.md, "One card per question"): the question as it stood at that turn, `{exchange, owner, name, question, state, quietAt, by, reason, said}`. `exchange` is its id, by which an app shows one card, its latest copy, in place of the relayed turns that hold it. A relayed turn holds a card only when it's obid's own turn about that question; a relayed turn with none stays words. `state` is an open string: `asking`, `waiting`, `quiet`, `answered`, `declined`, `expired`, `cancelled`, or `refused`, which has `exchange` null and `why` in place of the rest, since a refusal keeps no exchange; a question the secret scan stopped has no card. `quietAt`: when `asking` counts as unanswered by the clock. `said`: the other Oby's words or a decline's note, cleaned as a quote is: data, for a text node. `by` and `reason` are what it says of itself. The first copy goes on the conversation's latest turn that isn't relayed the moment the question has gone, which adds no turn, and a `turnAsks` event says so. The turns and their `text` are as above, so an app that doesn't read `asks` shows what it showed.

**The mark.** A turn quoting another Oby's words always marks the conversation as having read something untrusted (crate::secretspec), whoever sent it.

**A question that waits for the owner** is said in the one conversation for every member's questions, titled `Questions from other Obys`, with `fromObys: true` in `GET /v1/conversations` (add-only), quoted, asking what to say back, and saying whose it is as `<name>'s Oby (<owner>)` when the card goes by another name than the owner id. However many members ask, it takes no room of the owner's and pushes nothing out of the 200 obid keeps; words that name no conversation never go to it, the app never opens on it, and every turn there starts untrusted. That conversation is marked as above, and marks none of the owner's other work. A question its sender calls off, or that runs out, says so there, quoted. `oby_inbox` lists an `obiRequest` question's words as `asks`, so the brain can tell two apart. Their words there reach `oby_answer` on the question's `obiRequest` item (a Confirm card when reading the inbox made the turn untrusted), and go back `by: owner`.
