### No Dispatch card (#269)

There's no draft to confirm any more: the work the brain plans starts as it's planned, like the Lead's, and `POST /v1/plans` answers the jobs it started. Everything the card let you change before the start changes after it: the account, tool or model (`POST /v1/jobs/{id}/move`), the mode (`POST /v1/jobs/{id}/mode`), a message to the agent, Stop. The outgoing gates don't move: checks, the secret scan and Oby's review still gate every push and merge. ### `POST /v1/plans`

One turn of the conversation with Oby: words become work that starts, a question, or an answer in words. Takes 3–20 seconds (the brain thinks); show Oby thinking, with a way to cancel, and let a new message replace the one under way. Cancelling only drops the answer: work the plan started goes on, and Stop ends it.

```json
{"said": "Review oby PR #33"}
```

`said` is the owner's words, not empty (`422`); on obid it may be empty when `attachments` or `jobs` go with it (Sangam's message box sends files or tasks alone, docs/contracts/attachments.md), and a conversation such words start is named after the first file. On obid there's no length of its own, as in Claude Code: the request body is at most 2 MB (`413`), and words that, with the conversation, are more than the model can read at once (Claude Code's `prompt_too_long`, Codex's `contextWindowExceeded`) first go on in a new session on the same account, the earlier turns handed over, on a turn that has only read (docs/contracts/conversation-agent.md, CONVERSATION-AGENT-16); too long there too, they are answered, with no plan loop, `That's more than Oby's model can read at once, so nothing was started. Attach long text as a file (+) instead, or send it in parts; if this conversation has grown long, start a new one.`; a turn that had already done more answers `This conversation grew too long for Oby's model partway through that, so it stopped there. What it had already done stands, and anything it started keeps going, so don't send the same words again. Start a new conversation to go on.` (proposed, Sangam's to change), with `jobs` as any answer has them. A session refused twice for a reason that doesn't pass gets a new one the same way (CONVERSATION-AGENT-15); one that doesn't answer leaves `session.json` on the session it was on. The jobs they start still take tasks of up to 2,000 characters.

`attachments`, when present, is a list of file paths in the runner's own attachments folder (the Mac app saves what's attached on it in `attachments/mac` under obid's home, `~/Library/Application Support/Oby/Runner`). The runner resolves `..` and symlinks before accepting them; a path outside that folder returns `422` naming the refused file. Accepted paths are checked again before they are copied into the job's input folder, including jobs restored after a restart: `.oby/inputs` on the Swift runner and `.obi/inputs` on `obid`. On obid at most 20 (`422`, `A message attaches at most 20 files.`); the Electron app uploads each file first (`POST /v1/attachments`, below) and sends the paths it answered. The owner's turn keeps the files' names (its `attachments`, `GET /v1/conversation`). Words sent again with their `id` are `repeated` and add no file twice.

### `GET /v1/conversation?before=&limit=`

One thread for every device (#11): the conversation the runner keeps, which every `POST /v1/plans` joins, whichever app sent it. The runner keeps the last 500 turns in `oby.sqlite`, the oldest pruned; the brain reads the last 50 of them. Answers one page, oldest first:

`obid` keeps many conversations, 500 turns each, and every turn names its `conversation`. There this endpoint takes `id`, and without it answers the latest conversation: obid only: conversations, below.

```json
[{"id": "7c1e2f30-…", "who": "user", "text": "Review oby PR #33", "at": "2026-10-11T09:12:00Z", "jobs": []},
 {"id": "9a4b5c60-…", "who": "oby", "text": "The route is in Runner/API.swift.", "at": "2026-10-11T09:12:04Z", "jobs": [], "details": "Read [Runner/API.swift](https://github.com/deskree-inc/oby/blob/<sha>/Runner/API.swift#L1)."}]
```

- `who` is `user` or `oby`. `at` is when it was said. `jobs` is always a list: the jobs the turn started, by id, for a card under Oby's words, with Stop; on the owner's turn (obid only), the jobs they attached to it (`POST /v1/plans`'s `jobs`), for cards under their words. `attachments` (obid only, #1576), on the owner's turn, is the names of the files that went with the words (`POST /v1/plans`'s `attachments`), for the tags under them; left out when there were none. `delivery` (obid only, add-only, docs/contracts/deliveries.md), on the owner's turns sent by `POST /v1/plans`, is where those words stand: `{"status", "by", "why", "error"}`, the last three left out when empty. `status` is an open string (read a value you don't know as nothing to show): `queued` (kept, waiting for a turn to take them), `starting` (a turn took them), `running` (the turn that carries them has begun), `answered` (Oby's answer for them is kept), `failed` (no agent session could answer), `interrupted` (cut by a restart; nothing writes it yet) or `cancelled` (`POST /v1/plans/cancel` dropped them). `by` is the id of the owner's turn whose turn carries the words: their own, or the latest of words answered together. `why` says why words went back to `queued`. `error` says why they `failed`, with every secret obid knows taken out. It's left out on turns from before obid kept it and on turns no plan answers (Oby's, a job's end, a routine's). Oby's reply to a plan carries the jobs it started (#269), as the Lead's does; `details`, when present on Oby's turn, is longer Markdown shown under the short reply and never spoken. `Start` on a proposed plan's inbox item adds an Oby turn carrying that job. On obid, a job that ends adds an Oby turn carrying it, with `"ended": true`, to the conversation that started it (obid only: conversations, below). Fetch one that isn't in `GET /v1/jobs` with `GET /v1/jobs/{id}`.
- `limit`: how many, 50 unless asked, 1 to 500. `before`: the `id` of the oldest turn you show, for the turns before it; leave it out for the latest. Fewer than `limit` turns means there are no older ones. `404` for an id that isn't in the thread (a turn pruned since).
- A `turn` event (`GET /v1/events`, below) comes with every turn added, and again for a turn that changed (its `jobs`): replace the turn with that `id`, or add it in `at` order.

### obid only: conversations

Many conversations with Oby instead of one thread (#493; decided 2026-10-16, D2): each thing asked on Home can start its own, and every device sees them all. The Swift runner keeps its one thread.

A conversation, as `GET /v1/conversations` lists it and the `conversation` event sends it:

```json
{"id": "5b0e2c4d-…", "title": "Review oby PR #33", "pinned": false,
 "created": "2026-10-16T09:12:00Z", "lastActivity": "2026-10-16T09:14:07Z", "cursor": "0-42",
 "tasks": [{"id": "t1", "title": "Fix the login test", "status": "in_progress", "job": "ab12cd34", "note": null}],
 "folders": ["/Users/alex/Projects/api"], "talkedOn": "personal"}
```

- `talkAccount` (obid only, docs/contracts/conversation-agent.md): the account this conversation talks through from its next turn, as the owner chose it here: an account's id or `auto`; left out, it follows the setting. `PATCH /v1/conversations/{id}` sets it (null clears it), with `PUT /v1/settings/jobs`'s `422`s. `talkedOn`: the account its last answer came from, left out before its first. A change of either goes in the `conversation` event, `talkedOn` with the answer's.
- `movedOff` (obid only, docs/contracts/conversation-agent.md): the account its turns have moved off, and said so; left out when none. The apps name where the answers come from, not the chosen one, while it's there; it goes when an answer comes from that account again or the owner chooses again.

- `folders` (#1572): the working folders its work is in, canonical paths in the order picked (obid only: working folders, below). Always a list, empty when it has none. A change sends the `conversation` event.

- `tasks`: the task list the conversation's agent keeps for itself (`oby_tasks`, obid only: the conversation manages its own work), in its order. Always a list, empty when it keeps none. A change sends the `conversation` event.

- `id`: a lower-case UUID. `title`: the first line the user said in it, as Sangam's design names conversations (`design/app/src/renderer/src/chats.ts`): at most 48 characters (the first 45 and `…` when longer), its first letter upper-case, any other control character a space. A rename replaces it. `created`: when it started. `lastActivity`: its latest turn's `at`, or `created`. Renaming or pinning isn't activity.
- `cursor`: its place in the list right now (pinned or not, and how recent its latest turn is), for paging (below). It changes with every turn and every pin. Treat it as opaque.
- Each keeps its last 500 turns, the oldest pruned, in a private file of its own (`home/conversations/<id>.json`, 0600).
- Every turn names its conversation: `GET /v1/conversation` and the `turn` event carry `"conversation": "<id>"`.
- **At most 200 are kept.** When a new one starts past 200 (and when obid starts with more), obid forgets the oldest that isn't pinned (the one whose latest turn is oldest), as `DELETE` would, with its `conversation` event. Pinned conversations and the latest one are never forgotten, so pinning more than 200 keeps them all. The jobs a forgotten conversation started stay.

**`GET /v1/conversations?after=&limit=`**: the conversations, the pinned ones first, then the others, each newest first (by their latest turn; obid orders turns exactly, not only to the second). `limit` is as for turns: 50 unless asked, 1 to 500. For the next page, send the `cursor` of the last conversation you show as `after`: the list goes on from that place. A cursor is a place, not a conversation: a conversation that stayed where it was is listed once, never skipped. One that moved since (a turn or a pin moves it up, an unpin down) can be missed or listed again; its `conversation` event already told you where it is, so keep one per `id`. `422` for an `after` that isn't a cursor. Fewer than `limit` means there are no more; `[]` before anything was said.

**`GET /v1/conversation?id=&before=&limit=`**: one page of the conversation `id`'s turns, as above; `404` for an `id` that isn't there. Without `id`, a `before` turn names the conversation (the one it's in); with neither, it's the latest conversation (the one with the latest turn), `[]` when there's none. So an app from before #493 shows the latest conversation and pages back through the one it shows.

**`GET /v1/conversations?q=watch target`** finds conversations by what was said in them (#593): a plain text match, no model. `q` is split on spaces, each word lower-cased with the punctuation around it taken off (`target?` is `target`, `off-limits` stays whole), and a conversation matches when its title, or one of its turns, holds every word, in any case. The list is in the same order as without `q`, with `after` and `limit` as above, and each conversation has one more key, `turn`: the latest turn that holds the words, `{"id", "who", "at", "text"}`, its `text` cut to 500 characters around the first word (`…` where it's cut), or `null` when only the title holds them. Only the conversations obid keeps are searched, so a deleted one never appears, and neither do turns pruned past the 500 kept. `422` for a `q` with no word in it or longer than 200 characters.

**`PATCH /v1/conversations/{id}`** with `{"title": "Vault questions"}`, `{"pinned": true}`, `{"folders": ["/Users/alex/Projects/web"]}` or more than one renames or pins it, or replaces its working folders (an empty list clears them), and answers the conversation. A folder it has already stays as kept, even one gone from disk since, so it can be taken off; any other is checked as `POST /v1/plans` checks it (`422` naming it and why). Through the gateway or the phone relay, `folders` may only leave out folders it has: one more is `422`. The title is trimmed: one line of 1 to 100 characters, with no line break (`\n`, `\r`, U+2028, U+2029) or other control character. `422` for none of the keys or a title that breaks those rules; `400` for a body that isn't that JSON (another key, a `title` that isn't a string, a `pinned` that isn't `true` or `false`, a `folders` that isn't a list of strings); `404` for an id that isn't there.

**`DELETE /v1/conversations/{id}`** forgets it and its turns: `204`, `404` for an id that isn't there. The jobs it started stay, and keep its id in their `conversation`: show such a job's card on its own.

**`POST /v1/plans`** takes `conversation`, an `id` (the words join it; `404` if it isn't there), or `"new": true` (they start a new conversation, titled from them); both is `422`. With neither, the words join the latest conversation, or start the first one: the apps from before #493 (the Mac, the iPhone, the Watch) send neither and show one thread, and the Watch and the talk key carry on the latest conversation (#493). A screen that starts a conversation, like Home, sends `"new": true`. The brain reads only that conversation's turns (or the `turns` the app sends). Every answer, whatever its `kind`, carries `"conversation": "<id>"`, the one the words joined. The body may carry `"id"`, a lower-case UUID the app gave the words (anything else is `422`): the user's turn takes it, so the app's own copy of the words gives way to obid's turn of the same id. Words sent again with the id of the user's turn of the same words, where they go (the conversation named, the latest, or with `"new": true` the one those words started; an app's Try again after an answer it lost), add nothing and start nothing: the answer is `{"kind": "none", "repeated": true, "conversation": <that one>}`. An id any other turn has (other words, or another conversation) is `409`.

- A plan obid refuses is never a `422` (#546): obid tells the brain why and lets it try once more, then Codex plans, then keyword rules, which never act on the user's first words (they reply "Oby couldn't think that through: say it again", or ask which repository; to "continue", "go on" or "keep going" they reply "Oby couldn't think that through: say “continue” again in a moment.", never asking which repository, #632). The conversation keeps the words and that answer.
- When no brain answers at all (each one asked timed out or failed, and none sent a plan obid refused), rules don't guess: the reply says which brain and how, and that nothing started, as `"Oby's brain couldn't answer: Claude Code and Codex both took too long, so nothing was started. Try again."` (`Claude Code failed, then Codex took too long`, and so on). When the Mac is very busy it says so instead of "Try again.": `The Mac is very busy (load 512): try again once it calms down.` After a step that did or looked at something, it says that first and ends `Say “continue” for the rest.` (or `… to pick up from there.`). The turn ends `brain_failed`, its record's `by` is `none`, and the conversation keeps the reply like any other.
- **One answer at a time in a conversation** (Sangam's design). Words said in a conversation while a turn is under way in it wait for that turn, which goes on to its own answer. When it ends, one turn answers every word said meanwhile, as the latest words' turn: they're one after another with a blank line between them, their attachments together, and the brain or the session reads the whole conversation as it is then, that answer included. The latest words' request carries the answer, and the jobs it starts name the latest words' `turn`. Each earlier request answers `{"kind": "none", "jobs": [], "drafts": [], "conversation": "<id>"}` once that turn takes its words, and no turn starts for them alone. Stop (`POST /v1/conversation/stop`, or a bare "stop") still interrupts the turn under way, and the words waiting then get their turn. `POST /v1/plans/cancel` drops the turn under way and the words waiting for it: their requests answer `none`, and the words stay in the conversation with `delivery.status` `cancelled` (the waiting ones at once, the turn's own once it ends, before its request answers). A turn in another conversation goes on.
- A conversation deleted while its turn runs stays deleted: the answer still comes, with its `conversation`, and Oby's words aren't kept.

**Jobs remember the conversation that started them.** A job a plan started has `conversation`, the id of the conversation whose words started it, kept when that conversation is deleted, and every `job` event of it carries it, up to the last one (how it ended): show its events in that conversation. It also has `turn`, the id of the user's turn whose words started it, from its very first `job` event, so the apps put its card under those words the moment it starts, before Oby's answer lists it in `jobs`; A follow-up the owner's words start in its parent's own conversation, and a job the Lead starts, have that turn too. `turn` is null for a job no turn started directly (a follow-up told from its page or from another conversation, a Start or Confirm in the inbox); a job Oby offered (`oby_offer`) has the turn it was offered in, whenever the owner's Start starts it, so its card goes under the answer that offered it. The apps count it only for a job of the conversation on screen. A job from `POST /v1/jobs`, or another Obi's review, has `conversation` null. An inbox item names its `job`, so it belongs where its job does. A routine's runs go to the routine's conversation ([obid only: routines](#obid-only-routines-395)): `conversation` is the routine's, `turn` is null (no user's turn started it, Run now from chat included, which starts on the owner's Confirm), and the turn that says the run started lists its job, so its card goes there. Work that comes in from elsewhere (another Oby, GitHub) doesn't yet start a conversation of its own.

**A job tells its conversation how it ended (#351).** Told once and never lost: the mark is kept in the job's file, never in the API, and an end nobody heard (obid went down, or fell behind its events) is told at the next start or then; what ended before obid first kept the mark (`home/job-reports-since`) says nothing (docs/contracts/task-conversations.md, TASKCONV-18). That session's next turn hears the first line of each report since the owner last spoke and got an answer, quoted, before their words (TASKCONV-17). A new session is handed the same lines with the earlier turns, and in a conversation marked as having read something off obid's allowlist that turn is never the owner's word (TASKCONV-20). Each time a job a conversation started goes from `queued` or `running` to an end, obid adds one Oby turn to that conversation, with the job's card (`jobs` is `[<id>]`) and `"ended": true`, a key only these turns have. No model writes it: its first line is obid's own words, from the job's fields: `<title> is done: <pull request URL>` when it was published, `<title> is done, but it wasn't published: <why>` when its publish failed, `<title> is done: Pushed, but <why>` when the push went out and only its pull request failed, `<title> is done and pushed.` for a remote with no pull requests (#627), `<title> ended with nothing done. <reason>` for a job whose `nothingDone` is true (TASKS-12), `<title> is done.` (with `. <reason>` after it when it has one), `<title> failed.`, `<title> stalled: <stalled.text>. Its branch is kept and nothing was pushed.` (a stalled job, which stays stalled with its own card), `<title> was stopped.` or `<title> was interrupted.`, the title as `title` reads, cut to 80 characters. When the job's `summary` has a line, its first one (cut to 300 characters) follows on a second line: for a failed job, why it failed (not for a stalled one, whose summary starts with the same stall). Every secret obid holds is taken out of both, as `[redacted: <name>]`. The summary may be its agent's words, so the brain's briefing reads only the first line of such a turn; what the agent wrote stays in the quoted `work.md` (obid only: the brain). A job that ends again after Try again or one more round says so again. A job from `POST /v1/jobs` or another Obi's review (no `conversation`), a dismissed job, and what ended before obid first kept the mark add nothing; a conversation deleted meanwhile isn't told.

**Events.** A `conversation` event comes with the conversation as listed whenever that changes: when it starts, with each turn (before the `turn` event, so a new conversation is known before its first turn), and when it's renamed or pinned. Replace the conversation with that `id` and sort again. When one is deleted or forgotten, it's `{"id": "<id>", "deleted": true}`: take it out. ### obid only: the conversation manages its own work

The conversation's agent manages Oby's work as Claude Code manages its own (TaskStop, TaskCreate, TaskUpdate, TaskList; Gemini CLI's `write_todos`), with Oby's tools in the `oby` MCP server above. What the owner says ("cancel that", "archive the done ones", "delete the failed job", "switch it to Codex") becomes one of these calls.

**The gate.** These tools act only on the owner's own jobs: a review another Obi asked for is refused (`That's a review another Oby asked for: it isn't yours to change.`). What can't be undone, what widens what a job may do (Bypass counts as wider only where it isn't the owner's own default: there, every job runs in it anyway), and anything done on another conversation's job (#631) or in a turn that read something untrusted (the harness's taint, above) becomes a `confirm` item, which only the owner's Confirm carries out; the tool then answers what waits, not an error (`<what> waits for your OK: confirm it in your inbox.`). Its title is obid's own words, never the agent's; words the agent added are in `payload`, and `text` shows them as the owner's words and what Oby adds for its agent, `> ` lines, never the agent's quoting fences. `payload` also holds the job's `snapshot` (Inbox item, above): a Confirm after the job changed is refused. So text that agents or other people wrote can never delete, widen or continue a job by itself. Each call lands in the turn (its answer is part of the reply) and in the job's `changes`.
