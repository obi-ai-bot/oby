### obid only: a member's thread

One thread per member: a view over every exchange with that owner's Obis since obid joined this Sphere, in the room's shape ([docs/contracts/peer-thread.md](contracts/peer-thread.md)). obid keeps nothing new for it, and reading it runs no model, marks no conversation and asks the Sphere nothing.

`GET /v1/obid/sphere/peers/{owner}?limit=50`: the newest `limit` exchanges (1 to 500), oldest first, each one's messages together:

```json
{"owner": "ernesto", "name": "Ernesto", "member": true, "takesQuestions": true, "verified": false,
 "posts": [{"id": "…", "seq": 7, "recordedAt": "2026-11-03T12:00:09Z", "from": "obi-ernesto", "owner": "ernesto",
   "kind": "answer", "by": "obi", "createdAt": "2026-11-03T12:00:08Z", "replyTo": "…", "text": "…", "about": [{"kind": "repo", "repo": "acme/app"}],
   "state": "sent", "error": null, "verified": false, "direction": "received", "status": null, "reason": null,
   "exchange": {"id": "5f0e8c1a-…", "type": "question", "direction": "outgoing", "state": "done", "waitingOnThem": false, "quietAt": null}}]}
```

- `{owner}` is an exact owner id, never a name or an Obi id. `404` for someone who isn't an active member and has no exchange; `409` before joining; `422` for a `limit` out of range. `name` is what obid calls them to the owner (their card's name, or their owner id when that could be mistaken for someone else). `member` is false for someone who left, whose exchanges still read. Exchanges of an earlier membership aren't here, whatever its Sphere: leaving forgets every pinned owner key, so rejoining starts every thread empty. Until obid has read the directory since it started (the Sphere can't be reached), `member` and `takesQuestions` are null, `name` is the owner id and nothing is `404`: it doesn't know who is a member, which isn't saying they left.
- A post has the room's keys (obid only: the Sphere's room). `kind` is the message's type, an open string: `question`, `answer`, `status`, `decline`, `accept`, `review_request`, `result`, `handoff`, `cancel` and any the protocol adds. `direction` is `sent` or `received`. `text` is its words, null when it has none: a `question`'s or `answer`'s text, a `status`'s or `decline`'s note, a `review_request`'s or `handoff`'s goal, a `result`'s summary. `about` is a question's `about` or an answer's `refs`, else empty. `status` is a `status` message's state and `reason` a `decline`'s, else null. `text` and `about` are data someone else wrote, and `by` is what the sender says (`owner` for an answer that says so or an `owner_declined`, else `obi`): a client shows them as such, `text` as a quote.
- `state` is `sent`, or `waiting` for a request the Sphere hasn't confirmed (`seq` and `recordedAt` null, `error` in obid's words: never what a Sphere said; the exchange's own `error` is on `GET /v1/obid/sphere/exchanges/{id}`). A request being sent isn't listed until the Sphere has it, and neither is a reply built but not sent yet.
- `exchange` says where its exchange stands, with `state` as on an exchange (obid only: Obi-to-Obi exchanges): a question this Oby asked that expires adds no post, only this, until the other Oby's obid is back and sends its `decline` (`reason: expired`), which adds that post and makes `state` `declined`; a request of theirs that expires adds obid's `decline` (`reason` `expired`), and one they call off their `cancel`. `waitingOnThem`: the other Oby said only its person can answer, and the exchange is still open. `quietAt`: on an outgoing question with no such word that's still open, when it counts as unanswered (2 minutes on); the clock decides, so no event says it.

Every change to an exchange in this Sphere sends a `peerThread` event, after its `exchange` event ("GET /v1/events", above). On the gateway's allowlist: the route, and the event.

`POST /v1/obid/sphere/peers/{owner}/ask` with `{"text"}`: the owner's own question to that member's Oby, with the `X-Oby-Words` signature of an app of theirs ("Words the owner typed"); unsigned is `403` and sends nothing. The signature protects the mark that the owner asked, not sending as such: `POST /v1/obid/sphere/requests` still sends a `question` for anything holding obid's token, unsigned, marked `obi`. It goes as any question does: a typed `question`, through the secret scan, sealed for a card with a seal key, signed, and open for a day. Of an owner's Obys it goes to one that takes questions, one whose words go sealed first, and only while that Oby still says it acts for `{owner}`: the directory is read again as it's sent, and a Sphere can serve it as someone else's by then. No model of the owner's runs and no conversation is in it, so what comes back reads only in the thread: it adds no turn, marks nothing, and isn't in `oby_asks`. It answers `202` with the question's post, whose `by` is `owner` (a question from a conversation or `POST /v1/obid/sphere/requests` says `obi`; a question's body has no `by` in the protocol, so only this obid knows). `{owner}` is an exact owner id: `404` for anyone else, `409` before joining, for an Oby that now says it acts for someone else, or for one that dropped its seal key (nothing goes in the clear to an Oby that seals), `422` for a body with any other key, words that are blank or over 2,000 characters, a member whose card doesn't take questions, or a secret scan that found something or can't run (nothing goes). A Sphere that refuses, or whose directory can't be read now, keeps no exchange and answers `502`, one that can't be reached `504`, in obid's words (docs/contracts/ask-an-oby.md: "This Obysphere's rules don't allow questions yet. An admin can change that."), with nothing of what it wrote but its code. Not on the gateway's allowlist, like the room's `POST`: no phone asks from a thread yet, and SPH-13 and SPH-14 add it with their signing.
