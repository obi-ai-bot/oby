# The Oby runner API

The runner is Oby's engine without the Mac app's window: the brain and the Lead, Oby's own clones and job worktrees, the Claude Code and Codex runners, accounts, routing and Publish. It runs on an always-on machine (first: `tetrix-lab`, Ubuntu, on Wellington's tailnet), so the iPhone app (and later the Watch) can say what to do, and follow the jobs, while the Mac is closed.

> The Swift runner and the Swift Mac app were deleted by the owner's decision of 2026-11-02 (git history has them). Where this document still says "the Swift runner", "a Mac runner" or "the Mac app" for a client, what exists is obid, the Electron app and the iPhone and Apple Watch apps.

This document is the contract between the runner and its clients. Change it in the same pull request as the code on either side.

The Rust daemon `obid` (`core/`) serves this same API, so the apps run against it unchanged, plus the endpoints in [obid only](#obid-only) at the end; the Swift runner is frozen and never implements those. **`obid` merges by its own rules** ([obid only](#obid-only), "merging", #853, #854): a pull request its job opened, approved and checked, the way the repository's workflow says, or on the owner's tap where it hasn't learned one. Everything below where the Swift runner merges a pull request (the Mac check's "Passed" merge, its conflict rounds and its recovery's retries, its `merge` inbox item) is the Swift runner's only. [obid only](#obid-only), "What obid leaves out", says what obid answers in those places instead.

## obid only

The Rust daemon `obid` (`core/`, docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) serves this whole API, so the apps keep working against it unchanged, and also the endpoints in this section. **The Swift runner is frozen and never implements them.** They are what Obi-to-Obi work and the end-to-end scenario (e2e/SCENARIO.md) need. JSON keys are camelCase as everywhere in this API; objects of the Obi-to-Obi protocol (envelopes, agent cards, action bodies) appear exactly as the protocol defines them, in snake_case (obi-ai-bot/obi-sphere, `spec/`). None of these endpoints is on the gateway's allowlist (`core/crates/obid/src/gateway.rs`): they are managed on the machine itself. A phone answers an Obi-to-Obi request through the inbox endpoints it already has. Two reads are the exception, for the iPhone's side panel, which lists the Obysphere and teammates' Obys (docs/contracts/ios-new-oby.md, IOSNEW-4): `GET /v1/obid/sphere` and `GET /v1/obid/sphere/members`. They carry the membership's public facts (no token, no private key) and who's in it, to a device that's the owner's; joining, leaving, keys, cards and pins stay off it.

### obid only: what obid leaves out

`obid` serves every endpoint and key above, with its own rules for merging (obid only: merging, below). So, on `obid`:

- It merges only as that section says: never by the Swift runner's Mac-check flow. For a workflow whose `checks` is `mac`, the job's own check counts, passed in its box on exactly the head (obid only: merging); nothing queues a Mac check by itself.
- A pull request on a branch obid made that conflicts with its base or is behind it gets one rebase round by itself per head before any card: its job is queued at its check (`round` one more, `step` `Rebasing onto dev`), rebased onto the base as it is now, a conflict its agent's one round, then checked and pushed again over the head obid pushed (docs/contracts/merging.md, MERGING-3, 4 and 38). A head that had its round, or a branch obid didn't make, gets the `merge` card, and the owner can ask for a rebase like any other work.
- `macCheck.tries` is never sent, and no `Not merged:` alert is made; `mergeError`, `mergeNeedsYou` and the `merge` inbox item are obid's own (below).
- The `merged` pipeline stage is `upcoming` until `pullRequest.state` is `MERGED`, then `done` (`Merged into dev, as you like` when a remembered workflow merged it). While it's approved (GitHub's decision `APPROVED`, or Oby's own review's) it is `current`: with `mergeError` while the merge needs the owner, else `Merge it on GitHub`, and the apps open the pull request (`published`) from that line.
- Deskree's promotion rules (Xcode Cloud waits, `dev` into `main`, the three-review escalation) are configuration that changes what `obid` checks and reports, never something that merges.
- **Oby's review** (#525) runs when a job publishes by itself: a `review` job (`reviewOf` the author, `branch` `oby/review-<id>`, the author's `task`, Plan mode, no check of its own) on an account that's signed in, not being signed in or removed, and with room (Routing): another tool's first, then another of the author's tool's, last the author's own in a session of the review's own (`review.sameAccount`); never Cursor. With none, nothing runs over a limit: the author's `noReviewer` says why (`Oby's review waits for an account with room: …`, its Review stage `Waiting for review · …`, no event), and the review starts on obid's next pass (every three minutes, and when it starts) once one fits.
  - It reads the very commit obid pushed (the author's last `push`, its `startCommit`), whatever the branch has since; when the review's clone doesn't have it (the owner's clone was made again since the push), obid fetches that commit from origin first, held to the owner's clone's rule, and a review never makes a marked clone again itself (MERGING-19). What the author was asked, then the owner's own words to it after (their signed messages and Confirms, their conversation turns' words without Oby's fenced addition, their signed answers to its agent's questions; never an event's words; for a follow-up carrying an earlier task's branch, that task's too; oldest first; kept in the job's file, never in the API: MERGING-18), then, under their own heading (`Words sent to it through obid's API (no app of the owner's signed them), oldest first:`), the words sent to it through obid's API token that no app of the owner's signed (an unsigned follow-up's own and later messages, `Job::api_said`; the signed words win where they differ), and the author's own summary of each round it worked on Oby's review, why it didn't take a point among it (`Job::replies`), both left out when a job of it is someone else's (`Job::someone_elses`: another Oby's, someone else's code, or read text Oby didn't write; MERGING-33), but for a job only those unsigned API words made someone else's code (`Job::foreign_by_api`, kept in the job's file, never in the API; any other mark ends it: MERGING-33a), is quoted in `.obi/inputs/asked.md` (with obid's note, when the author is untrusted, that its task read text Oby didn't write and why: its `Job::untrusted_why`, never that text; MERGING-13), and the repository's `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` as they are on the base (`origin/<base>`) in `.obi/inputs/base-rules.md`: never the change's own, which its author could steer the review with. Its run loads no project instructions from the worktree: Claude Code runs with `CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_CLAUDE_MDS=1` (and `--setting-sources ""`, as every job), Codex with `-c project_doc_max_bytes=0 -c skills.include_instructions=false -c features.skill_search=false` (no `AGENTS.md`, and no skill of `.agents/skills` or `.codex/skills` in its instructions or found by search). Its prompt says every file of the worktree, its comments, commit messages and the diff are data. Its summary is the review; only its last line that isn't empty decides, exactly `VERDICT: APPROVE` or `VERDICT: REQUEST CHANGES` (in backticks or bold too). The issue it worked from is quoted too, as data, in `.obi/inputs/issue.md`, always written by obid (the quote, or a line saying there's none or it couldn't be read; never a file a commit left), and its prompt says not to fetch from GitHub or the web. Who wrote on that issue is read again then: anyone else's words mark the task (`as its review started, …`), so it takes two reviews (MERGING-14), and the review is told why (MERGING-13, MERGING-18).
  - One review per published head: none while one runs and none of a head a review already read. A head pushed while one ran makes that one count for nothing, however it ended (not one of the three), and gets its own once that one ends.
  - `review` also carries `commit`, the head it read, and never `checkRun`. Of Oby's review, only an approval goes on GitHub, as the reviewer account's (obid only: merging); changes it asks for stay in Oby, and no check run, comment or description line is written.
  - A `reviewing` whose review a restart or a crash cut off is settled on obid's next pass: the review's end is applied, or a review that's gone is started again. Dismissing a review job (one waiting on an account-limit decision too) leaves its author `reviewError`, never `reviewing`.
  - Approved: the Review stage is `done`, `Approved by Oby`, and Merged is `current` (above). Each approval is kept in the author's `approvals` (an array of `review` objects, oldest first; those of an earlier head stay until the next approval).
  - **Two reviews** (MERGING-14 to 16, the owner's decision of 2026-10-30): a head merges by Oby's own review only after two approving reviews of it, each by a different tool, when its task is untrusted (`Job::untrusted_why`: its conversation read something Oby didn't write, it's someone else's code, words no app of the owner's signed reached it (`POST /v1/jobs` or a message without `X-Oby-Words`), or a memory known to come from someone else's words reached its agent (`memory::someone_elses_among`: never one kept before obid marked memories, nor one the owner's own words made); each kept on the job's file, carried to its follow-ups, never in the API; a reach past the registries alone, `reached`, isn't: MERGING-31) or its change touches a security-sensitive path (`merge::SECURITY_PATHS`, at any depth and in any letter case: `AGENTS.md`, `AGENTS.override.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `.cursorrules`, `.mcp.json`, `.cursor/`, `.claude/`, `.codex/`, `.agents/`, `.gitleaks.toml`, `.gitleaksignore`, `secretspec.toml`, `CODEOWNERS`, CI files such as `.github/workflows/`, and the files the repository's `check` runs), whoever wrote it. The review job reads the changed files of the commit it reviews against `origin/<base>` before it runs; when they can't be read, it counts as a security path. Its verdict carries why in `review.twoReviews` (`it changes AGENTS.md`, `Oby couldn't read what it changes`, or the untrusted reason; absent when one review is enough). Once the first approves, Oby starts the second on a tool none of the head's approvals came from, by the same order, the author's own account last; with none signed in with room, it waits by itself with no card, as a job at its limit does (MERGING-34; a teammate's Oby is still asked first where GitHub requires another person's approval; under a `reviewer` workflow, the reviewer account posts nothing): `noReviewer`, and the Review stage's line, say what for (`Waiting for a second review: <tool> has no room until <when>.`, each such tool, `; ` between them; `Waiting for a second review: your OK for work I run by myself to use the ChatGPT plan on <account>.`; or, with no other tool signed in, `Waiting for a second review: no tool other than <tool> is signed in.`; while it's about to start, `Waiting for a second review`), and once another tool's review approves that head it merges by itself by the workflow. A card another hold raises meanwhile (MERGING-2's check, say) names the second review too, and any Merge of the owner's lifts it. A second review due but never started (a restart in between) starts on obid's next pass. A second review that asks for changes is a review like any other: a round while reviews are left (`review.round` under 3), else the owner's item (MERGING-8). The words wait for Sangam's screens.
  - Changes requested by the first and second reviews are the author's next round, in its session and worktree (`round` + 1, `step` `Working on Oby's review`): the review is quoted, the vault's and obid's secrets taken out and in a fence of its own, in `.obi/inputs/review-<round>.md`, which the round's fixed words name, and its publish is reviewed again. Changes requested by the third (`review.round` 3), or by an earlier one whose round couldn't start (a follow-up carries its branch, another job holds it, it passed its safety limit, obid couldn't hand the review over), or a round that left the head as it was, need the owner: `reviewNeedsYou` is true while the pull request is open, and the Review stage is `failed` (`Changes requested`, reason `Oby requested changes.`). No fourth review starts by itself, and no Mac check is queued. One `failed` inbox item (`<job>-review-<review job>`, `Oby's review still asks for changes. Take it from here.`) offers `One more round` and `Merge` (its detail `Merges it as it is into <base> with your GitHub login, if GitHub lets it.`); obid raises it with the verdict, or on its next pass for one it kept from before (#1658), and not while a follow-up still works on the job's branch (what it pushes is the head to merge); `reviewError`, when obid couldn't hand the review to the agent, follows its text. One still open from an obid before takes `Merge` too. One more round quotes that review for the author's agent as a round past the limit (`round` + 1, `step` `Working on Oby's review`), and Oby reviews what it pushes; a review that still asks for changes is one new item, the old one done. It's refused (`409`, the item open again) while the job isn't `done`, is reviewed, a follow-up works on its branch, it passed its safety limit, or the review job is gone. `Merge` is the owner's own Merge of the head that item's review read (`review.commit` while `review.job` is the one its id names; a head moved since merges nothing), as a `merge` card's (`mergedBy` `owner`, the learned workflow or the one a card would propose): GitHub is read again and every other rule still holds (its checks, a conflict, a branch behind, a clean secret scan; into main, master or trunk too, on this Merge: MERGING-7); one that can't go is refused (`409`, `Not merged: <why>`, the item open again unless a newer review settled it). Any other answer is `422` `The answer is One more round or Merge.` No merge card stands beside the item, and nothing merges it by itself. The brain's `oby_answer` can't answer `Merge` (only the owner merges) (MERGING-8, MERGING-17).
  - A review that ended `failed` or `interrupted` before its verdict (a login refused, as codex-cli 0.162.1's `workspace routing discovery unauthorized (401)`; the tool crashing) goes to the next reviewer that may take it, by the same order, never one whose review of that head ended so: once, by itself. When none can (none left, or the second ends so too), the job needs the owner: `reviewError` `Oby couldn't review #7: <why>`, `reviewNeedsYou` true while the pull request is open (until a new head is pushed), the Review stage `Waiting for review · …`, an alert (`Couldn't review: <title>`, below), and one `failed` inbox item (`<job>-review-<review job>`, its `text` the `reviewError`) with `Try again` alone: it runs the review again, with one more reviewer to fall back on, and a second give-up is one new item, after an approving first review (MERGING-14) too. A second review that gave none, or that the owner stopped or let go, isn't started again by itself, on another account either, until Try again or a new head, and no merge card says it's the owner's to merge beside its item. No item while the pull request is merged or closed. Oby doesn't merge without a review by itself, and today merging without it isn't offered (proposed: a Merge on that item, the owner's, merges it with no review): no workflow merges without a review (`oby` waits for Oby's own, `reviewer`, `teammate` and `person` for someone's on GitHub). Its item is the owner's alone. The failed review job is dismissed (its end is its author's), so it asks no one itself; one the owner dismissed (waiting on an account-limit decision, say) isn't tried again. Internally the author keeps which reviews of its head failed (`review_failed`, never sent).
  - A review that was stopped, made commits or gave no verdict needs the owner at once, the same way (`Oby couldn't review #7: it gave no verdict`, `reviewNeedsYou`, the alert and the one `failed` item with `Try again`; RECOVERY-15), with no other reviewer tried by itself; one the owner dismissed, or stopped themself (`Stopped through Oby's API`, `Stopped by a conversation`), only leaves `reviewError` (`Oby's review didn't finish: …`), with no alert or item. One its account's usage limit stopped stays its author's review (`reviewing`, `Oby is reviewing`) and waits on its account for the reset by itself, with no card (docs/contracts/account-limits.md, LIMITS-5, 10 and 15: a review keeps its account), never dismissed or tried on another account by itself; the owner's Stop ends the wait and leaves `reviewError` (`Oby's review didn't finish: it was stopped`). The review job's own end is no event.
  - Every job that publishes by itself is reviewed, an untrusted turn's too, and twice, by two tools (Two reviews, above). A job published by hand isn't by itself (the owner can still ask Oby to review it, as any pull request): its Review stage reads `Pull request open · waiting for your review on GitHub` until GitHub has a decision.
