# obid-spec: every endpoint and trigger proven from outside

The business behavior contract for `tools/obid-spec`, from [the template](../business-contract-template.md). Scenarios are numbered `OBIDSPEC-n`; the tests that prove them are named after them (`obidspec_<n>_…` in `tools/obid-spec/src/` and `tools/obid-spec/tests/suite.rs`).

## Feature and status

- **Outcome:** Each route obid serves and each background trigger it runs is proven against the real obid binary, black-box, by a BLOK workflow whose steps name the contract scenarios they prove, so "implemented" in a contract means a workflow saw it happen.
- **Status:** OBIDSPEC-1 to 15 are **implemented** in `tools/obid-spec`. OBIDSPEC-13 to 15 drive the real Electron app (`app.*`, Playwright) and the Mac around it (`desktop.*`, agent-desktop) on macOS, as [docs/verification.md](../verification.md) asks; elsewhere their workflows are skipped and say why. Its workflows cover every route in `docs/api/routes.txt` and the eight background triggers (the Sphere long-poll and its deliveries, the Sphere directory read, restart recovery, account limits, the GitHub feed, CI autofix, cleanup and routines). `scope.json` classifies every scenario of every contract; `obid-spec coverage` lists the obid-owned ones not yet named by an assertion. The check runs it (docs/contracts/ci.md, CI-66 and 67): GitHub's merge queue the `launch` workflows (the Obysphere's and the launch flows'), the nightly and on-demand runs all of them, and a pull request all of them only when it changes `tools/obid-spec/` or carries `full-suite`; the Mac's `scripts/oby-check.sh` all of them for a change to the suite; each run gated by `obid-spec coverage --gate`.
- **Frozen until launch** (the owner's decision of 2026-11-18, "wire it in and freeze it"): no new workflow or scenario beyond the launch flows until after launch. A workflow, or a step, may still be added only when it proves a bug fix (its issue's acceptance, the `expectedRed` that comes off) or a launch flow; anything else waits, and `obid-spec coverage`'s backlog stays a list, not work.
- **Scope:** obid's HTTP API and its background triggers, from outside, on stand-in agents and a local Sphere; and, for a launch flow or a bug fix the freeze allows, the real Electron app (its built copy, on a scratch obid or its demo data) and the macOS menu bar and keys around it. Out of scope: the Electron app's unit behavior (its tests), the Obysphere server's own behavior (obi-sphere's), real agent CLIs and logins, and anything on the network past this Mac.

## Behavior

### Actors and ownership

- **The suite's host**, `obid-spec`, reads the workflows (`tools/obid-spec/workflows/`), builds obid once, starts the run's local Sphere, runs the workflows and judges each step.
- **The nodes**, `obid-spec-nodes`, one child per workflow, speak BLOK's gRPC to the host over their stdin and stdout, as `oby-check-blok-nodes` does, and do what each step says: start or stop a scratch obid, call it, wait for an event, read a file in its home, sign as the owner's device or owner key, run the local Sphere; launch and drive the real app through one helper per app (`apps/electron/scripts/drive-app.mjs`, beside the human drive's `drive-lib.mjs`, JSON lines in and out), and read and drive the Mac's menu bar through agent-desktop's CLI.
- **scope.json** says, area by area, which routes and contracts the suite covers, which scenarios are obid's (to prove here) and which another surface's, with why, and which workflow files prove each background trigger.

### Triggers and prerequisites

- `obid-spec run [--only <area>] [--tag <tag>] [--workflow <text>] [--jobs <n>] [--obid <path>] [--keep]`, from anywhere in the checkout. `cargo`, `git`, `node`, `pnpm` and `gitleaks` on `PATH`; the first run clones obi-sphere at the commit `e2e/sphere.ref` pins into `~/Library/Caches/obid-spec/` and installs its packages (`OBID_SPEC_SPHERE_CLONE` names another clone).
- `obid-spec coverage [--only <area>] [--gate]`, `obid-spec list`, `obid-spec nodes`.

### States and transitions

| A step | What the host does |
|---|---|
| Its inputs name `${state.<step>…}` | Takes those values from the earlier steps' outputs, as BLOK keeps `ctx.state[id]`; one that names nothing yet fails the step. |
| It passes | Keeps its output as `state.<id>`. |
| It fails | Fails the workflow; the rest of its steps are skipped. |
| It has `ui.expectedRed` (a known bug's issue) | Its failure is reported as expected red and the workflow goes on; its pass fails the run, so the fix is noticed and the mark removed. |
| It has `ui.flaky` (a known intermittent bug's issue) | Its failure is listed as flaky with the issue and the workflow goes on; its pass is a pass. |
| A step answers `skipped` (no macOS, no agent-desktop, no Accessibility or Screen Recording permission) | The rest of its steps are skipped and the workflow is marked skipped: neither passed nor failed. |
| The workflow ends, however | A `cleanup` call stops every process its nodes started, by PID (obids, each app's helper and the app itself); the nodes child is stopped. |

### Failures and recovery

- obid that doesn't say it listens within 60 seconds, a Sphere that doesn't answer its health within 120 seconds, a step past 300 seconds: the step fails, with what obid or the Sphere logged.
- A failed workflow's folder (its obids' homes, logs and `config.json`, the run's `report.json` with every step's output) is kept and named; a passed one's is removed.

### Invariants and boundaries

- **Nothing of the owner's.** A scratch obid has its own home, port and token under the run's folder, `HOME` a scratch folder, `PATH` only obid's stand-in agents and fake `gh` (`core/crates/obid/tests/agents`), `node`, `gitleaks` and the system's folders, a stand-in `security` for the Keychain, an empty `GH_CONFIG_DIR`, a stand-in login shell (`OBID_LOGIN_SHELL`) that answers that same `PATH`, and every tool pinned to a stand-in by absolute path, since obid also looks in the usual install folders (`/opt/homebrew/bin`). Never the owner's obid on 127.0.0.1:7777, `/Applications/Oby.app`, their logins, their CLIs or their GitHub; the HTTP client reaches only 127.0.0.1.
- **The Mac is shared.** obid is built once per run with `CARGO_INCREMENTAL=0`; at most 6 workflows at once; every process stopped by its PID and its process group, and a node stops only a process the suite started.
- **Black-box.** The suite reads none of obid's crates: what the owner's app signs (an owner key's card and delegation, a device's words and Confirm) is written again here from the Electron app's `shared/sphere.ts`, `main/words.ts` and `main/device.ts`, so a signature only obid could read fails.
- **The app and the Mac are the person's, not the suite's.** An `app.*` workflow's app is a built copy of this checkout's (or `OBID_SPEC_APP`'s), hidden (no Dock icon, every window invisible), with its own user-data folder, on the workflow's scratch obid or a port nothing listens on; port 7777 and an obid home outside the workflow's folder are refused. agent-desktop is installed into the workflow's folder, its state folder there too; no node asks for, grants or changes a macOS permission. A step that runs one of the app's menu items brings its invisible window forward for that moment and gives the front back to the app that had it.
- **Workflows are BLOK v2.** BLOK Studio can show and edit them; a step BLOK can express that this host can't run (`branch`, `switch`, `forEach`, `loop`, `wait`, `tryCatch`, `parallel`, `subworkflow`) is refused before the run, not run differently.

## Interfaces and dependencies

- obid's HTTP API (`docs/api/`), its route list `docs/api/routes.txt`, and the contracts under `docs/contracts/`.
- The local Sphere: obi-sphere's `server/scripts/local.mjs` at `e2e/sphere.ref` (Dmytro's server, read only).
- `blok-rs` at the revision oby-check-blok pins, for the node registry and its gRPC service.

## Acceptance scenarios

- **OBIDSPEC-1** Each workflow is a BLOK v2 file; one under `workflows/routes/<area>/` has an `http` trigger naming a route's method and path as `docs/api/routes.txt` lists it, one under `workflows/triggers/` a `manual` trigger. Every shipped workflow parses, and every route it names is one obid serves (`obidspec_1_…`).
- **OBIDSPEC-2** The nodes are written once and every workflow uses them: `obid-start`, `obid-stop`, `call`, `check`, `events-follow`, `events-wait`, `home-file`, `write-file`, `device-pair`, `device-sign`, `owner-key`, `sign-card`, `delegation`, `sphere-make`, `sphere-invite`, `sphere-policy`, `sphere-admin`, `sphere-owner`, `sphere-join`, `sphere-seal`, `sphere-liar`, `sphere-lie`, `next-second`, `clock`, `fixture-git`, `app.launch`, `app.click`, `app.type`, `app.press`, `app.read`, `app.measure`, `app.screenshot`, `app.close`, `desktop.snapshot`, `desktop.read`, `desktop.click`, `desktop.press`, `call-wait` (with `call`'s `background`, for a request that blocks while a later step acts, such as a turn's words); `obid-stop` with `kill` ends obid with SIGKILL, as a crash would; a step that uses anything else, or a BLOK step this host can't run, is refused before the run (`obidspec_2_…`).
- **OBIDSPEC-3** `obid-spec coverage` prints, per area, routes with a workflow, triggers with a workflow and obid's scenarios with an assertion naming them (`ui.proves`), and exits 1 when any is missing; `--only <area>` judges that area alone (`obidspec_3_…`).
- **OBIDSPEC-4** Nothing goes unread silently: a scenario of a contract in scope.json that is in neither its `obid` nor its `elsewhere` list, a scenario scope.json names that the contract doesn't list, and a step naming a scenario no contract in scope lists each fail coverage (`obidspec_4_…`).
- **OBIDSPEC-5** `obid-spec run` runs the workflows in parallel, each on its own nodes child and scratch folder, steps in order with each one's output kept for the next; it prints one line per workflow (its steps passed, its assertions passed, its expected reds) and the expected reds with their issues, and exits 0 only when no step failed (`obidspec_5_…`).
- **OBIDSPEC-6** Every assertion is proven able to fail, on every run: the answer it passed on is changed at each place it looks (the status, each leaf of the expected body, each path, the text), and each change must fail it. One that passes anyway is a vacuous assertion and fails the step; a step that names a scenario but asserts nothing fails too (`obidspec_6_…`).
- **OBIDSPEC-7** A step marked `ui.expectedRed` with a bug's issue is expected to fail: its failure doesn't fail the run and is listed with the issue; its pass fails the run (`obidspec_7_…`).
- **OBIDSPEC-8** A scratch obid gets only what's said under "Nothing of the owner's": its own config, home, token and port, a `PATH` of its stand-ins and the system's folders, a login shell that answers that `PATH`, every tool pinned to a stand-in, and no variable of the person's environment; `obid-stop` and the end of a workflow stop it by PID, and a PID the suite didn't start is never signalled (`obidspec_8_…`).
- **OBIDSPEC-9** The lying Sphere passes every call through as it is, but serves the named owner's cards in the directory re-signed with a new owner key, the key the real Sphere never changes, so a changed owner key (SPHERE-8 to 10) is proven against the real server (`obidspec_9_…`).
- **OBIDSPEC-10** A step marked `ui.flaky` with an intermittent bug's issue: its failure doesn't fail the run and is listed with the issue; its pass is a pass, so the mark comes off only when the issue is fixed (`obidspec_10_…`).
- **OBIDSPEC-11** `obid-spec coverage --gate`, the check's gate, judges what must hold now: every route and trigger has a workflow, every scenario in scope is classified, no step names an unknown scenario and no workflow names a route obid doesn't serve; it exits 1 when any fails. The scenarios of obid's that no assertion names yet are the backlog: it prints how many, and they never fail it (`obidspec_11_…`).
- **OBIDSPEC-12** A workflow may carry tags (`"tags": ["launch"]`); `obid-spec run --tag <tag>` runs only the workflows that carry it, and none is an error. The `launch` tag is on every Obysphere workflow (the `sphere` area and the `sphere-*` triggers) and on the launch flows' workflows (the app's and the Mac's among them), and on nothing else: the merge queue's set (`obidspec_12_…`).
- **OBIDSPEC-13** The real app, driven from outside: a workflow under `workflows/app/` (its trigger `manual`) launches this checkout's built Electron app with `app.launch`, through `drive-lib.mjs`'s launcher, hidden, with its own user-data folder, on the workflow's scratch obid (`OBID_PORT`, `OBID_HOME`) or its demo data (`mock`); `app.click`, `app.type` and `app.press` act on it, `app.read` reads elements by role and name, label, text or css, `app.measure` gives an element's box and whether it overlaps others, `app.screenshot` saves the window into the workflow's folder, and `app.close` quits it; each stops by PID at the workflow's end. Port 7777, an obid home outside the workflow's folder and a step on an app no launch started are refused; off macOS `app.launch` is skipped (`obidspec_13_the_app_runs_only_on_the_workflows_own_obid`).
- **OBIDSPEC-14** The Mac around it: `desktop.snapshot`, `desktop.read`, `desktop.click` and `desktop.press` read and drive the app's menu bar and the chords it takes through agent-desktop's CLI, installed into the workflow's folder with `npm install --prefix` (or named by `OBID_SPEC_AGENT_DESKTOP`), the app found by its PID. Off macOS, with agent-desktop absent, or without both the Accessibility and Screen Recording permissions, each answers "skipped: …" with why ("skipped: agent-desktop has no Accessibility/Screen Recording permission on this Mac"), and asks for, grants or changes nothing (`obidspec_14_off_macos_or_without_both_permissions_a_desktop_step_skips`, `obidspec_14_the_menu_bar_reads_as_titles_and_item_names`, `obidspec_14_a_desktop_step_without_agent_desktop_is_skipped`).
- **OBIDSPEC-15** A workflow with a step that answers `skipped` is marked skipped: its later steps don't run, the table says `skip` with why, the summary counts it apart, and it is neither passed nor failed, so a run with skips and no failure exits 0 and never reports a skipped workflow as passed (`obidspec_15_a_skipped_workflow_is_neither_passed_nor_failed`).
