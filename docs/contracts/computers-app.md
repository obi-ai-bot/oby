# Other computers, in the app

The business behavior contract for the computer picker in the Electron app, from [the template](../business-contract-template.md). Scenarios are numbered `COMPUTERSAPP-n`; the app's unit tests that prove them are named after them (`computersapp_<n>_…`, in `apps/electron/src/renderer/src/test/computers.test.tsx`). obid's side is [remote.md](remote.md) (`COMPUTERS-n`).

## Feature and status

- **Outcome:** The owner sees which computer the window works on, adds another Mac or Linux box over SSH or with one link, switches the whole window to it and back, renames or removes one, and is told plainly, and only then, when a computer can't be reached.
- **Status:** Every scenario that doesn't say it's proposed is **implemented on the demo data** (`?everything`, and `?everything&computers=<screen>` for one of the design's screens, 01 to 10): the button, its menu, the sheets and the window's states, driven by one store (`computers/store.ts`) over one interface to obid (`computers/obid.ts`, `ComputersApi`). **On obid, live** (`computers/obid.ts`, on the Mac's routes in [docs/api/remote.md](../api/remote.md), "This Mac's computers"): the list and its events, reading a link, adding with one link (or a link of version 1 and its code), this Mac's own link for another Mac, switching, retrying, renaming and removing, and every call of the window's naming the computer in use (COMPUTERSAPP-15). **Not on obid yet** (obid serves no route for them): adding over SSH and its steps, and this Mac's reach switches (obid's configuration, `direct`, says which addresses it offers); each says so in the window (`NOT_YET`, proposed words, Sangam's to change), and his controls stay where his screens have them. **Proposed:** the message box's draft kept and Send waiting while no route answers (COMPUTERSAPP-13's second half); the window's lists re-read from the computer in use after a switch (COMPUTERSAPP-14), beyond the routes obid forwards today.
- **Design:** the owner's approved design of 2026-11-20, [docs/design/computer-picker/](../design/computer-picker/README.md) (#2635): every word on its screens and in its "Every word" tables is the owner's approved words, and the app uses them as they are, but where the owner changed them since. **The owner's word of 2026-11-23** changes screens 04 and 05: pairing is one short link, random and good once, with no six-digit code and no fingerprint in it, so the switch says `With a link`, the link to the other side `Show this computer’s link`, screen 05 is `This Mac’s link` (the link and `Copy link`, no code), and screen 04 asks for a code only for a link of version 1 (COMPUTERSAPP-16, 17). Sangam's screens follow.
- **Scope:** the Electron app: `apps/electron/src/renderer/src/computers/`, the button's place in `Sidebar.tsx`, the stage and the connection line in `Home.tsx`. **Out of scope:** obid's side ([remote.md](remote.md)); choosing a computer per task (later); the iPhone; the light theme.

## Behavior

### Actors and ownership

- **obid** keeps the window's computers (which exist, which is in use, each one's state and route, every route tried), adds, switches, retries, renames and removes them, and says what went wrong in its own words (`say`). The window never parses a link or SSH's output itself.
- **Main** holds obid's token and the computer in use: it names it on every call and the stream with `Oby-Computer`, taking it only from obid's own answers about the window's computers (COMPUTERSAPP-15). It signs adding with a code, by the code the owner typed; adding with one link, by the link they pasted; this Mac's link, by `Show this computer’s link`; and removing, by `Remove computer`, as it signs their words ([signed-words.md](signed-words.md)); switching, renaming and retrying need no words (docs/api/remote.md).
- **The window** shows what obid says, in the design's words, and sends the owner's clicks.

### Where it sits

- **One new control: the computer button**, at the mini sidebar's foot, right above Settings (or the account's picture with `?everything`). Its icon is the computer in use: a laptop for This Mac, a desktop for another Mac, a server for Linux; its tooltip that computer's name; for screen readers `Computer: {name}. Change`.
- **One menu does everything**, beside the rail, its foot level with the button's, shaped like the profile menu: This Mac, then each computer by name with a second line, then `Add a computer…`. With only This Mac, the line `Work on another Mac or Linux box from this window.` sits above `Add a computer…`. A computer's ⋯, on hover, has `Rename…` and `Remove…`. There is no Settings page for computers.
- **A row's second line:** the computer in use, its kind and route (`Mac · Local network`, `· Tailscale`, `· VPN`, `· Internet`, `· SSH`; the same with `Linux`) with a green dot while connected; another computer, its kind (`Mac`, `Linux`); one no route answers, `Can’t reach it right now`.

### States

- **Switching:** a row picked (or `Back to This Mac`) closes the menu; Home's line under Oby says `Switching to {name}…` / `{other} keeps its tasks running.` and the side panel and message box dim until obid says the new computer is in use.
- **Connected:** quiet. The menu marks the computer and says how it's reached.
- **Reconnecting on a fallback route:** quiet for 2 s; past that, the window's connection line says `{name}’s {route} doesn’t answer. Reconnecting over {route}…` (`local network`, `Tailscale`, `VPN`, `internet address`, `SSH`). Moving back up shows nothing.
- **Unreachable:** the button carries the orange needs-you dot; on Home, `Oby can’t reach {name} right now.` / `Its tasks keep running there, and Oby keeps trying.`, each route tried (`Local network · {address} · no answer`, `SSH · {obid's words}`) and the chips `Try again` and `Back to This Mac`; the side panel and message box stay, dimmed; outside Home, the connection line says `Oby can’t reach {name} right now.` with its red edge.
- **Adding over SSH:** the sheet's three steps (`Reached {host} over SSH`, `Pairing this Mac with Oby on {host}…`, `Finding the fastest way to reach it`) tick off as obid reports them; added, the window switches to it. When SSH says no, obid's words show as it says them, what to type in a terminal as code, with `Cancel` and `Try again`.
- **Adding with a link:** the person pastes the link; obid reads it and the sheet shows the computer's name, its fingerprint (16 bytes, groups of four hex digits) and `Check that {name} shows the same fingerprint.`; a one link adds with `Add computer` alone, a link of version 1 asks for its code first. A link or code obid refuses shows its words, and the sheet stays.
- **This computer's link:** `This Mac’s link`, `On the Mac you’re adding {name} to, paste this link.`, the link, its fingerprint, the addresses others reach it at (each with a switch), `Good until {time}, for one computer.`, `Copy link` (`Copied` once copied) and `Done`; with none to offer, `This Mac has no address another computer can reach. Add it over SSH instead.`; when obid makes none, its words, never an empty sheet. A switch obid can't turn goes back and says why.
- **Rename:** the row's name becomes a field in place; Enter or a click elsewhere keeps it, Esc leaves it as it was.
- **Remove:** `Remove {name}?`, `This Mac stops reaching {name}, and {name} forgets this Mac’s key. Its tasks and folders stay on {name}.`, `Cancel` and `Remove computer` (the owner's word of 2026-11-21 over the design's `Remove`, so no other Remove can sign it). Removing the computer in use switches to This Mac first.

### Invariants

- Every word the picker shows is the approved design's, or obid's own (`say`), shown as obid says it.
- The window never parses a link or what SSH printed: obid does (COMPUTERS-5, 6).
- Colour appears only when no route answers: the needs-you dot and the connection line's red edge.
- Nothing of the demo data shows on obid; on obid the picker shows only once obid serves its list.

## Interfaces and dependencies

`ComputersApi` (`computers/obid.ts`), one method per route: `list` and `follow` (`GET /v1/computers`, the `computers` and `computer.route` events of this Mac's own stream), `readLink` (`POST /v1/computers/link`, its `needsCode`), `addWithCode` (`POST /v1/computers {link, code}`, or `{link}` for a one link, signed), `invite` (`POST /v1/computers/invite {}`, signed), `use` (`PUT /v1/computers/current`), `retry` (`POST /v1/computers/{id}/retry`), `rename` (`PATCH /v1/computers/{id}`), `remove` (`DELETE /v1/computers/{id}`, signed). Not served by obid yet, so answered in the window: `addOverSsh` (the design's `POST /v1/computers {ssh}` and `computer.adding` events) and `setReach`. The demo's stand-in (`computers/demo.ts`) answers as the design says obid will. The client names no route obid doesn't serve ([ci.md](ci.md), CI-10).

**Main, on another computer** (`main/obid.ts`, `main/live.ts`): every call of the window's carries `Oby-Computer: <id>` and never `Oby-Profile`; `/v1/computers` routes and main's own calls for this Mac (its device key, obid's status at launch, Help's facts, the Obysphere, pairing) carry none. The follower follows the computer in use (`GET /v1/events`, forwarded), its lists read there after each switch; this Mac's own `GET /v1/events?alerts=1` runs beside it, so obid still counts the Mac app as following (ALERTS-3), and brings `computers` and `computer.route`. Routes obid doesn't forward for a computer answer `404 not_for_a_computer`, shown as obid says it.
