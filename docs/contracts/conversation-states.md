# A conversation's state at a glance

The business behavior contract for what the owner sees of a conversation without opening it, and what stops it from going on, from [the template](../business-contract-template.md). Scenarios are numbered `CHATSTATE-n`; the tests that prove them are named after them (`chatstate_n_…`: obid's in `core/crates/obid/src/conversation/marks.rs`, the app's in `apps/electron/src/renderer/src/test/chatState.test.tsx`). The API is [conversations.md](../api/conversations.md) (`seen`, `unread`, `archived`) and [account-limits.md](../api/account-limits.md) (`POST /v1/jobs/{id}/move`).

## Feature and status

- **Outcome:** From the side panel alone the owner knows which conversations have something new, which ended well or badly, and can put old ones away; inside one, they know at once when its account can't run, and what went wrong when Oby refused a click, until they've read it.
- **Status:** **Implemented** (2026-11-17), as T3 Code shows the same things (its status pill, unread, archive, its provider banner and error banners). The words are proposed until Sangam's screen has them.
- **Scope:** the Chats column's rows and their menu, Archived at its foot and its page, the line over a conversation's or task's message box. **Out of scope:** a task's own row under its conversation (its mark is `TaskMark`, unchanged), Home's `?home` lists, the iPhone (it reads the same keys later, add-only).

## Behavior

- **Actors and ownership:** obid owns `seen`, `unread` and `archived`: it decides what is unread (a turn after the owner last looked, by its exact order, never the clock) and keeps both marks in the conversation's file. Why an account can't run is obid's (`cantRun`, ACCOUNTS-19). The app shows them, sends `seen` when the owner looks, and sends their clicks.
- **Triggers:** opening a conversation, or a turn coming into the one open; the row's menu (Mark unread, Archive, Delete…); Archived's Restore; the line's Sign in and Use another account; a refused Stop or answer.
- **States:** a row shows one mark, the first that holds: needs you (orange), working (grey, pulsing), unread (blue), its latest task failed or stopped (red), its latest task done (grey check), none. An unread row's title is bold.
- **Failures and recovery:** a refused `PATCH` is said as any refused edit is (Home's connection line); a refused Stop or answer stays as a line in the thread until dismissed. A conversation archived and then spoken in comes back by itself.
- **Invariants:** looking at a conversation, marking it or archiving it never moves it in the list (`cursor` unchanged) and is never a turn. A conversation obid kept before these marks has no `seen` and is never unread until looked at once. Deleting still keeps the tasks it started.

## Interfaces and dependencies

`PATCH /v1/conversations/{id}` with `{"seen": true|false}` and `{"archived": true|false}`, sent only to an obid whose `capabilities` lists `conversation-marks-v1` (older ones have no Mark unread or Archive in the menu); the conversation's `seen`, `unread` and `archived` (each left out until there's something to say). `GET /v1/status` accounts' `signedIn` and `cantRun`. `POST /v1/jobs/{id}/move` with `{"account"}`. The app: `chatState.ts` (pure rules), `CantRunLine.tsx`, `Refusals.tsx`, `Sidebar.tsx`, `ConvoMenu.tsx`, `ArchiveView.tsx`, `live.ts`.

## Acceptance scenarios

- **CHATSTATE-1:** In a conversation whose account can't run (the account its message box names is signed out, or obid says `cantRun`), one line over the message box says so: `<tool> · <account> can’t start: <reason>` or `<tool> · <account> is signed out`, with Sign in and Use another account. In a task's thread the same line shows while the task is queued or ended failed or stopped on such an account. With an account that can run, there is no line. (`chatstate_1_a_conversation_whose_account_cant_run_says_so_over_the_box`, `chatstate_1_a_failed_task_on_a_signed_out_account_says_so`, `chatstate_1_no_line_while_the_account_can_run`)
- **CHATSTATE-2:** Sign in opens Settings › Agents and starts that account's sign-in there, as its own Sign In does, so the tool's own sign-in page opens; for the tool's own login on this Mac, which obid doesn't sign in, it opens the page at the account's row, which says how. (`chatstate_2_sign_in_starts_the_accounts_own_sign_in_in_settings`)
- **CHATSTATE-3:** Use another account picks the first account that can run (signed in, not full, no `cantRun`), the same tool's first: a conversation talks through it from its next turn; a task moves there (`POST /v1/jobs/{id}/move` with `{"account"}`). With none that can, the button isn't there. (`chatstate_3_another_account_is_one_that_can_run_the_same_tools_first`, `chatstate_3_a_conversation_talks_through_it_and_a_task_moves`)
- **CHATSTATE-4:** A refused Stop (a conversation's or a task's) or a refused answer to what a task asked shows as a line in that conversation's or task's thread, obid's words, and stays until its × dismisses it; it isn't put in Home's connection line. (`chatstate_4_a_refused_stop_stays_in_the_thread_until_dismissed`)
- **CHATSTATE-5:** A row's mark is the first of needs you, working, unread, its latest task failed or stopped, its latest task done; the latest task is the one it started most recently. (`chatstate_5_the_rows_mark_is_its_latest_tasks_end`)
- **CHATSTATE-6:** obid: a conversation is `unread` once a turn comes after the owner last looked; it starts seen, the owner's own words see it, and `{"seen": true}` sees it. One kept before marks has no `seen` and reads as read. (`chatstate_6_a_turn_after_the_owner_looked_is_unread`, `chatstate_6_one_kept_before_marks_reads_as_read`) The app sends `{"seen": true}` when a conversation opens and when an unread one is the one showing, and shows unread as a blue mark and a bold title. (`chatstate_6_opening_an_unread_conversation_sees_it`); an obid without `conversation-marks-v1` is sent none, and the menu has no Mark unread or Archive (`chatstate_6_an_obid_without_marks_is_sent_none`)
- **CHATSTATE-7:** Mark unread (`{"seen": false}`) makes its latest turn new again until looked at; when they last looked and its place in the list stay. The row's menu offers Mark unread for a read conversation that has a turn, and Mark read for an unread one. (`chatstate_7_mark_unread_until_seen_again`, `chatstate_7_the_menu_marks_it_unread`)
- **CHATSTATE-8:** Delete conversation… asks first, naming it and saying its tasks stay; only Delete in that window deletes. (`chatstate_8_delete_asks_first`)
- **CHATSTATE-9:** Archive takes the row out of the chats into Archived at the column's foot, whose count holds archived conversations and tasks (one that needs you stays among the chats while it does, so what waits always has a row); Archived's page lists archived conversations first, each opening on a click, with Restore, which puts it back. (`chatstate_9_archive_moves_it_to_archived_and_restore_brings_it_back`)
- **CHATSTATE-10:** obid: `{"archived": true}` archives it and `false` restores it; Oby's turns leave it archived, the owner's own words bring it back. (`chatstate_10_archived_until_restored_or_spoken_in`)

**Test it when it's done (real Mac):** sign Claude Code out (`claude /logout`), open a conversation on it and see the line; Use another account, then send. Stop a conversation obid has already ended and see the refusal stay until ×. Let a task finish in a conversation you've left: its row is bold with a blue mark, then a check once opened. Mark it unread, archive it, find it under Archived, restore it. Delete one and see the window first.
