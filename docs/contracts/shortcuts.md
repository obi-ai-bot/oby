# Keyboard shortcuts: the window's keys

The business behavior contract for the Electron window's keyboard shortcuts, from [the template](../business-contract-template.md), as T3 Code keeps its own (one table of keys, one listener, every label from the table). Scenarios are numbered `SHORTCUTS-n`; the tests that prove them are named after them (`shortcuts_1_…` to `shortcuts_12_…` in `apps/electron/src/renderer/src/test/shortcuts.test.tsx`). The menu bar's Help items are [help.md](help.md)'s.

## Feature and status

- **Outcome:** The owner reaches what they use most without the mouse: a new conversation, the search over every conversation (and a Settings page or an action by its name), the words in the conversation in front of them, Settings, the side panel, the browser, and the conversations it lists, one after another or by their place. Esc closes what's open over the page. The menu bar of the app they installed has no developer's items.
- **Status:** **Implemented** in the Electron app. The keys are T3 Code's (`mod+n`, `mod+k`, `mod+f`, `mod+b`, `mod+shift+b`, `mod+shift+[`, `mod+shift+]`, `mod+1` to `mod+9`) and the Mac's own for Settings (`⌘,`). Sangam's screens have none yet: the menu bar items, the hints and ⌘K's headings and action names are proposed words, his to change.
- **Scope:** the window's page and the Mac's menu bar. **Out of scope:** keys the owner can change (T3 Code reads them from a file; Oby has one table), a command palette of its own (⌘K opens the side panel's search, Sangam's search, which also lists Settings pages and actions), and the message box's own keys.

## Behavior

- **Actors and ownership:** Only the app. obid isn't asked anything new: ⌘K's search asks obid's `GET /v1/conversations?q=` as the panel's search does (SEARCH-1 to 3, chat-first.md); its Settings pages and actions are the app's own.
- **The keys** (`shared/shortcuts.ts`, the one table; ⌘ on a Mac, Ctrl elsewhere, no other modifier):

  | Key | What it does |
  |---|---|
  | ⌘N | A new conversation: back to the empty chat, its box focused (as New conversation) |
  | ⌘K | The side panel's search: the panel opens if it's shut, on Chats, its field focused with what was typed selected |
  | ⌘F | Find in the conversation shown: a field over its top right, `2 of 5`, Return and the arrows to go through them (⇧Return back), each in view and lit; Esc closes it |
  | ⌘, | Settings, on General |
  | ⌘B | The side panel shut or open, as its toggle does (docked, or floating over a narrow window) |
  | ⇧⌘B | The browser shown beside the conversation, as the globe's click does (its tip, **Show the browser ⇧⌘B**, browser board 1), while there's a globe; already shown, it stays |
  | ⌘1 to ⌘9 | The 1st to 9th conversation the side panel lists, from anywhere: pinned first, by project when it groups them, a folded project's left out; none past the last |
  | ⇧⌘[ and ⇧⌘] | The conversation before or after the open one in that order; none open, ⇧⌘] the first and ⇧⌘[ the last; nothing past either end (T3 Code's) |
  | Esc | Closes what's open over the page: a menu, a sheet, the find bar, a page (as before) |

- **How a key is heard:** one listener on the window runs the newest screen's command for that key (T3 Code's scopes): ⌘F is the conversation's while one is shown, and nothing with a page over it. The page takes a key it answered, so its menu item doesn't run it again; a key the page leaves alone reaches the Mac's menu bar, whose item sends the same command back. A word being composed (an input method) and an alert dialog keep their keys. The keys work in the message box too, as T3 Code's do: none of them types text.
- **⌘K finds more than conversations:** with words in the side panel's search, under the conversations it finds come the Settings pages whose name holds them (under **Settings**; the word "settings" lists them all) and the actions that do (under **Actions**: New conversation, Open Settings, and Reveal obid’s Log where main is, [help.md](help.md)), as T3 Code's palette groups Threads, Settings and Actions. A click runs it and closes the search: a page opens with the panel on Settings, as the account menu's Settings does. With no words, only the conversations; "No conversations match" shows only when no group has a row.
- **Where they show:** the Mac's menu bar (Oby › Settings…, File › New Conversation, Edit › Find › Search and Find in Conversation, View › Toggle Sidebar and Show the Browser, Go › Previous Conversation, Next Conversation and Conversation 1 to 9), each with its key, from the same table; the side panel's New conversation row (its key as the pointer is on it), Settings' tip and the account menu's Settings, the Search button's tip, and the browser globe's tip.
- **The menu bar a release gets:** Electron's View menu without its developer items: a packaged build has no Reload, Force Reload or Toggle Developer Tools (nor their keys); a run from source (`npm run dev`, where main has the dev server's address) keeps them. Actual Size, Zoom In, Zoom Out and Enter Full Screen stay in both.
- **Invariants:** a menu never shows a key the window doesn't answer, and the window answers no key a menu doesn't show (but Esc, and Electron's own roles). No key sends anything to obid by itself.

## Acceptance scenarios

- **SHORTCUTS-1:** ⌘N from Settings closes it and leaves the owner on the empty chat; Ctrl+N off a Mac does the same; ⇧⌘N and a key pressed while an input method composes do nothing. (`shortcuts_1_2_4_new_conversation_search_and_settings_from_anywhere`, `shortcuts_1_ctrl_stands_for_cmd_off_a_mac_and_an_ime_word_is_left_alone`)
- **SHORTCUTS-2:** ⌘K opens the side panel's search, its field focused, from anywhere. (`shortcuts_1_2_4_new_conversation_search_and_settings_from_anywhere`)
- **SHORTCUTS-3:** In a conversation, ⌘F opens its find bar; `retry` in a thread holding it three times says `1 of 3`, Return `2 of 3`, ⇧Return twice `3 of 3`; words it doesn't hold say `No results`; Esc closes it. While it's open the thread keeps the bar's height free at its top (T3 Code's reserved height), so the bar lies on no message, the first one included, nor on the match it went to (`findBarLayout.test.ts`, every conversation of the mock at 1280px and 900px). With a page over the conversation, ⌘F isn't its. (`shortcuts_3_cmd_f_finds_the_words_in_the_conversation_and_esc_closes_it`, `shortcuts_3_with_a_page_over_the_conversation_cmd_f_is_not_its`)
- **SHORTCUTS-4:** ⌘, opens Settings, and the page takes the key. (`shortcuts_1_2_4_new_conversation_search_and_settings_from_anywhere`)
- **SHORTCUTS-5:** Esc closes an open menu. (`shortcuts_5_esc_closes_an_open_menu`)
- **SHORTCUTS-6:** The Mac's menu bar has an item for each key with that very key, and only those carry one (`CmdOrCtrl+B`, `CmdOrCtrl+Shift+[`, `CmdOrCtrl+1` …); the side panel shows ⌘N on New conversation while the pointer or the keys' focus is on it, holding no room while hidden so the row reads whole at the panel's narrowest (`sidePanelLayout.test.ts`, at 1280px and 892px), and `Settings ⌘,` and `Search ⌘K` as tips; a drawn key is no part of its control's name, which stays `New conversation` and `Settings`, the key told once by `aria-keyshortcuts` (`sidePanelLayout.test.ts`); a key with ⇧ reads `⇧⌘]` on a Mac and `Ctrl+Shift+]` elsewhere. (`shortcuts_6_the_menus_show_the_keys_the_window_answers`)
- **SHORTCUTS-7:** ⌘B shuts the side panel when it's open and opens it when it's shut, and the page takes the key; ⇧⌘B doesn't move it. (`shortcuts_7_cmd_b_shuts_and_opens_the_side_panel`)
- **SHORTCUTS-8:** With Release notes, a pinned Backoff and Flaky test, the panel lists Backoff, Release notes, Flaky test, and ⌘1, ⌘3, ⌘2 open Backoff, Flaky test, Release notes; ⌘9, ⇧⌘1 and ⌥⌘1 open nothing. Grouped by project, the order is each project's rows then No project's, a folded project's left out. (`shortcuts_8_cmd_1_to_9_open_the_conversations_in_the_panels_order`)
- **SHORTCUTS-9:** With Backoff open between Release notes and Flaky test, ⇧⌘] opens Flaky test and ⇧⌘[ Release notes, whether the key reads `}` or `]`; ⌘] without ⇧ isn't it. At an end nothing opens; none open, ⇧⌘] opens the first and ⇧⌘[ the last. (`shortcuts_9_cmd_shift_brackets_go_to_the_previous_and_next_conversation`)
- **SHORTCUTS-10:** The menu bar a packaged build gets has no Reload, Force Reload, Toggle Developer Tools nor Electron's `viewMenu` role, and keeps Actual Size, Zoom In, Zoom Out and Enter Full Screen; a run from source has the three; main builds it with `dev` from the dev server's address. (`shortcuts_10_a_packaged_build_has_no_reload_nor_developer_tools`)
- **SHORTCUTS-11:** In the side panel's search, `gener` lists General under Settings and no "No conversations match"; `settings` lists the conversation Settings sync, then General, Agents, Obysphere, Connectors, Repositories, Routines and About under Settings, then Open Settings under Actions; `log` lists Reveal obid’s Log, whose click asks main for it; `new conv`'s New conversation starts one; `connectors`' Connectors opens that page. (`shortcuts_11_cmd_k_finds_settings_pages_and_actions_under_their_own_headings`)
- **SHORTCUTS-12:** At browser board 1 (the panel closed), the globe's tip says `Show the browser⇧⌘B`; ⌘B without ⇧ leaves the panel closed; ⇧⌘B opens it and the page takes the key, the globe now **Hide the browser**; pressed again it stays open; hidden by the globe, View › Show the Browser shows it again. With no browser offered (no globe), ⇧⌘B is left alone. (`shortcuts_12_cmd_shift_b_shows_the_browser_as_the_globe_does`)

**Test it when it's done (real Mac):** open Oby; press ⌘, (Settings), ⌘N (the empty chat), ⌘K (the search field, focused), type a word from an old conversation and see it listed; open a conversation, ⌘F, type a word, Return through the matches, Esc. Open the menu bar's Oby, File, Edit › Find, View and Go menus and see the same keys; press ⌘B twice, ⌘1 and ⌘2, then ⇧⌘[ and ⇧⌘]. With the browser's globe in the title bar, point at it and see `Show the browser ⇧⌘B`, press ⇧⌘B and see the panel open; hide it with the globe, then open it with View › Show the Browser. In ⌘K type `about` and open About. In the installed Oby (not `npm run dev`), View has no Reload nor Toggle Developer Tools, and ⌥⌘I does nothing.
