# Your Tetrix: the address of the person's own

The business behavior contract for how a person tells Oby which Tetrix to connect to, on Sangam's first run (the Knowledge step) and in Settings › Connectors, from [the template](../business-contract-template.md). Scenarios are numbered `TETRIX-URL-n`; the tests that prove them are named after them (`tetrix_url_1_…` to `tetrix_url_4_…` in `apps/electron/src/renderer/src/test/tetrixUrl.test.tsx`). The API is `docs/api/tetrix.md`; why a sign-in didn't take is [sign-in.md](sign-in.md).

## Feature and status

- **Outcome:** A person connects the Tetrix their own team runs by typing its address, on first run or later in Settings, and Oby never suggests one of its own.
- **Status:** **Implemented.** The social contract's first rule is that nothing in Oby is hard-coded to one provider or instance ([why](../decisions/2026-10-28-the-social-contract.md)).
- **Scope:** the first run's Knowledge step, the first conversation's setting up (the same row), Settings › Connectors' Connect Tetrix and Edit Tetrix, and an Obysphere's Knowledge page when it adds Tetrix. **Out of scope:** what obid does with the address (HTTPS, the sign-in server it finds, the login it keeps: [obid only: Tetrix](../api/tetrix.md), [sign-in.md](sign-in.md)); Sangam's words and layout, which stay as his screen has them.

## Behavior

- **Actors and ownership:** The person types the address. obid decides whether it is one it can sign in to (`POST /v1/tetrix/sign-in`, `reason` `badAddress` or `noSignIn`) and holds the login. The Electron app checks the address as the person types, so Connect waits for one obid would take, and shows obid's reason as its own sentence.
- **Triggers and prerequisites:** The Knowledge step, Settings › Connectors' Connect (or Edit, Reconnect) and an Obysphere's Add knowledge source › Tetrix. Nothing is signed in, or the person is changing which Tetrix.
- **States and transitions:** The Knowledge step's Tetrix row has a field under it (`Your Tetrix URL`, placeholder `https://tetrix.example.com/mcp`) and a Connect that is off until the field holds an address. Connect opens Tetrix's sign-in page in the browser (the row shows a spinner), then a check. **Skip for now** stays the way past. Settings' window has the same field, prefilled with the address Oby uses once there is one, and Continue in Browser; connecting to another address signs Oby in there in place of the one before.
- **Failures and recovery:** An address that isn't one obid would take (not HTTPS, no host, a login, a `#` part, a query but `?org=<uuid>`; plain HTTP only on 127.0.0.1 or localhost) says why under the field once the person has left it or pressed Connect, and Connect stays off. A refused or unfinished sign-in puts Connect back with obid's `reason` as its sentence under the field (SIGN-IN-10 to 12), and the field keeps what was typed.
- **Invariants and boundaries:** Oby names no Tetrix: no preset, no default address, no organization list, in the app's code, its sample data or its words. What obid still answers as `preset` and `presets` (the API only grows) is never read by the app, so an old `tetrixPresets` in someone's `config.json` offers nothing. The address is sent as the person typed it, trimmed and written the way obid reads it (`https://host` as `https://host/`), nothing else with it. No address is stored by the app; obid keeps it in `home/tetrix.json`.

## Interfaces and dependencies

`POST /v1/tetrix/sign-in` with `{"url": …}` only (the app never sends `preset`); `GET /v1/tetrix`'s `url`, `connected`, `signIn` and `error` (its `preset` and `presets` stay in the answer, unread). `tetrixUrl.ts` checks the address; `TetrixAddress.tsx` is the field, shared by the first run's row (`TetrixRow`, `SetupCard.tsx`) and Settings' `TetrixWindow` (`Connectors.tsx`).

## Acceptance scenarios

- **TETRIX-URL-1:** The first run's Knowledge step, Settings' Connect Tetrix and the Edit window offer no server by name: each has one field, `Your Tetrix URL`, and no list, whatever `GET /v1/tetrix` says in `presets` or `preset`; Edit starts from the address Oby uses. (`tetrix_url_1_first_run_asks_for_your_tetrix_url_and_offers_no_server`, `tetrix_url_1_settings_connect_tetrix_asks_for_the_url_too_and_never_lists_obids_presets`, `tetrix_url_1_edit_starts_from_the_address_oby_uses_even_when_obid_says_a_preset_named_it`); an Obysphere's Add knowledge source › Tetrix has the same field and no list of organizations (`tetrix_url_1_add_knowledge_source_asks_for_the_url_and_offers_no_org`)
- **TETRIX-URL-2:** Connect is off until the field holds an HTTPS address with a host (or HTTP on 127.0.0.1 or localhost), no login, no `#`, and no query but `?org=<uuid>`; what isn't one says why under the field once it's been left, and the sentence goes when the address is right. (`tetrix_url_2_https_with_a_host_is_the_way_in_and_the_rest_says_why`, `tetrix_url_2_the_problem_shows_once_the_field_is_left_and_connect_stays_off`)
- **TETRIX-URL-3:** Connect (or Enter) sends `POST /v1/tetrix/sign-in` with `{"url": <the address typed, trimmed>}` and nothing else; when obid refuses, the row's or window's field shows the sentence for obid's `reason` (SIGN-IN-10), never its `error`, and Connect is on again. (`tetrix_url_3_connect_sends_the_typed_address_and_nothing_else`, `tetrix_url_3_enter_connects_too`, `tetrix_url_3_obids_refusal_is_its_reasons_sentence_under_the_field_never_its_error`, `tetrix_url_3_settings_shows_the_same_sentence_under_its_field`)
- **TETRIX-URL-4:** No file of the app (renderer, main, shared) names a preset Tetrix, by a name such as "Dogfood" or by the host of a real instance, and none reads `preset` or `presets` from obid's Tetrix answer. (`tetrix_url_4_no_renderer_main_or_shared_file_names_a_preset_instance`, `tetrix_url_4_the_app_reads_no_preset_from_obids_answer`)

**Test it when it's done (real Mac):** on a first run, reach Knowledge and see a field, with Connect off; type `http://x.example/mcp` and leave it (`Use a secure address, one that starts with https://.`); type your Tetrix's address, Connect, finish in the browser and see the check. In Settings › Connectors, Edit Tetrix shows the address Oby uses; there is no list of servers anywhere.
