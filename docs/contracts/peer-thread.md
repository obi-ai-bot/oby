# A member's thread

The business behavior contract for one thread per member of your Obysphere, from [the template](../business-contract-template.md): a view over every exchange with that member's Oby, in the shape of the room ([rooms.md](rooms.md)). Scenarios are numbered `PEER-n`, and the tests that prove them are named after them (`peer_n_…` in `core/crates/obid/tests/peer_flow.rs`, `core/crates/obid/tests/sphere_questions.rs`, `core/crates/obid/src/sphere/exchange.rs`, `core/crates/obid/src/sphere/sharing.rs`, `core/crates/obid/src/sphere/link/asking.rs` and `apps/electron/src/renderer/src/test/`, and the app's in `apps/electron/src/renderer/src/test/peerThread.test.tsx`, `peerScreen.test.tsx` and `personCard.test.tsx`). The drives on the plan's Sphere tab are numbered `PEER-n` too and are a different list: human-drive steps, not these.

## Feature and status

- **Outcome:** Clicking a person's Oby shows everything your Oby and theirs have said to each other, in one place. The owner's words: an Oby is your digital representation, so "if I am working on something my Oby would be able to answer what is the progress of what I'm doing".
- **Status:** obid's side **implemented** (SPH-2), asking from the thread (SPH-3), what the owner's Oby tells, only about the repositories they choose (SPH-7 to SPH-9), and in the Electron app the row that opens it, its box (SPH-4), the words for each state (SPH-5) and the person's card (SPH-6). The look is the canvas's Peer board; its words stay Sangam's to change.
- **Scope:** reading a member's thread, hearing that it changed, and asking from it. **Out of scope:** a phone asking from a thread (SPH-13, SPH-14), a follow-up that knows the last exchange (SPH-11).

## Behavior

### Actors and ownership

- **obid** builds the view from the exchanges it already keeps. A thread has no store of its own: nothing is kept twice, and nothing can disagree with the exchange.
- **The owner** asks in their own words, typed or clicked in their own app, which signs exactly `{text}` for this owner's path (docs/contracts/signed-words.md, WORDS-12). Nothing else asks from a thread.
- **The apps** show it, and say in Sangam's words what each state means. They decide nothing.
- **The other Oby** says what it says. Every word of it, and what its card says, is data.

### Triggers and prerequisites

obid is a member of an Obysphere. A thread exists for an active member, even with no exchange yet, and for someone who left but has an exchange in this membership (`member` false).

### What a thread holds

Every exchange with that owner's Obis since obid joined this Sphere, whichever way it went and whatever started it: a question from a conversation, a request from the API, one they asked, a review. Oldest first, each exchange's messages together. A post is one message, with the room's keys, plus what a thread needs:

| What happened | What the thread has |
|---|---|
| A question went | `kind: question`, `direction: sent`, `exchange.state: sent`, and `exchange.quietAt`, 2 minutes on. `by: owner` when they asked from this thread, else `by: obi` |
| No word by `quietAt` | The same post: past that time, it counts as unanswered. The clock decides, so no event says it |
| Only the person can answer | `kind: status`, `status: waiting_on_owner`, `exchange.waitingOnThem` true, `quietAt` null |
| Answered | `kind: answer`, `by: obi` or `owner`, the words in `text`, `exchange.state: done` |
| Declined | `kind: decline`, `reason`, a note in `text` if it has one, `exchange.state: declined`; `by: owner` for `owner_declined` |
| A question you asked expired | No new post: `exchange.state` on the posts there are. When their obid is back it settles the question too and sends `decline` (`reason: expired`), so a post comes and `exchange.state` becomes `declined`. Nobody said no |
| A request of theirs expired | obid's `decline` (`reason: expired`) once the Sphere has it; until then `exchange.state` |
| They called their request off | `kind: cancel`, `exchange.state: cancelled` |
| A review or a handoff | `kind: review_request` or `handoff`, the goal in `text`; its `result`, the summary in `text`. The findings stay on the exchange |
| A request being sent | No post yet: a refusal removes it with no event, so it shows once the Sphere has it |
| A Council's request not confirmed by the Sphere | The request's post, `state: waiting`, `seq` and `recordedAt` null, and `error` in obid's words. Only a Council's: a question whose send got no answer isn't kept, so it has no post |
| The Sphere refused a question | No exchange is kept, so no post (docs/runner-api.md, "obid only: Obi-to-Obi exchanges"). The refusal answers whoever asked |

### Asking from it

The owner's words go straight to that member's Oby as a typed `question`, as `oby_ask`'s do: through the same secret scan, sealed for a card with a seal key, signed, and open for a day. It differs from asking in a conversation in what's left out: no model of the owner's runs, and no conversation is in it. So they speak as themselves, and the thread marks it as theirs (`by: owner`, kept with the exchange: a question's body has no `by` in the protocol, so the other Oby isn't told). Everything comes back in the thread, and only there.

It's the owner's own act, signed, so it needs no Confirm, and none of what a turn's trust decides applies. The signature protects that mark: it doesn't stop what holds obid's token sending a question, which `POST /v1/obid/sphere/requests` still does, unsigned and marked `obi`. It goes by the exact owner id of the thread, and a request to anyone else is `404`, never a guess at who was meant: nothing here reads a name or an Obi id. With no Confirm, the app is the only place the owner sees who: **SPH-4 must show the owner id beside a name that differs from it, as `whose_oby` does.** When an owner has more than one Oby, it goes to one that takes questions, one whose words go sealed first.

obid reads the directory again as the question goes, and a Sphere's operator could serve that Oby by then as someone else's, under an owner key pinned at first sight and a seal key of its own. So it goes only while the Oby still says it acts for that owner id, and otherwise nothing goes. `oby_ask` holds to the same, on the owner's word and on their Confirm.

| What stops it | What the owner reads (the route's answer) |
|---|---|
| Unsigned | `403`, nothing goes |
| A possible secret, or a scan that can't run | What the scan said, without the words: nothing was sent |
| Not a member, or before joining | `404`; `409` |
| The card doesn't take questions | "Dima's Oby doesn't take questions." |
| The Oby now says it acts for someone else, or dropped its seal key | "I can't ask Dima's Oby: nothing was sent." `409` |
| The Sphere's rules (`policy_denied`) | "This Obysphere's rules don't allow questions yet. An admin can change that." The thread already says who, and no model of the owner's is there to say "I". No exchange is kept |
| The Sphere refused otherwise, or can't be reached | The words ask-an-oby.md gives: of what the Sphere wrote, only its code |

### In the Electron app

A member's row in a live Obysphere is a button that opens their thread, by exact owner id; the row goes by that id, which is all the directory gives. The tour's sample and the mock's Obyspheres keep Sangam's made-up conversations, and a live row never shows them, whatever the member's name (SPHERE-7).

- **Who.** With no Confirm, the app is where the owner sees whom they're asking. The title and the box say `<name>’s Oby (<owner id>)` when obid's `name` differs from the owner id, else `<owner id>’s Oby`, as `whose_oby` does. A post's byline is its `owner`, never a name. An owner id with anything but plain ASCII shows those characters as their codes (`d\u{456}ma`), so a letter from another alphabet shows for what it is. An ASCII look-alike (`dirna` for `dima`) still passes: the id is exact, but only the owner's eye tells them apart.
- **Who speaks.** It's in the room's parts ([room-app.md](room-app.md)). Yours is only what came from this Mac's Oby: your bubble when its `by` is `owner`, under `Your Oby` otherwise. Everything else is under their Oby's byline, whatever its `by` says, as a text node, `Not verified` until the owner compared their key.
- **Their words are a quote.** In a thread every word from someone else's Oby is a quote (`msg-quote`, as another Oby's answer in a conversation) under its byline, as a text node: lines it writes stay inside the quote, so nothing it says reads as Oby's, and it can't write a state line. Yours and your Oby's aren't quoted, and the room doesn't quote.
- **Where it stands.** A post with `text` null has no bubble. Under each exchange's last post with words, and only there, so it's told once, is one quiet line outside the quote, in ask-an-oby.md's words. `Dima` is the owner id's name as **Who** gives it: `Dima (dmytro)` and `Dima’s Oby (dmytro)` for any other name, `Dana` and `Dana’s Oby` when the name is only the id's capitals. Of a question you asked:

  | What happened | The line |
  |---|---|
  | Sent, until `quietAt` | `Asking…` |
  | `quietAt` passed, by the clock: the app's timer for the next one, stopped when the view goes, with no event | `Dima’s Oby hasn’t answered yet.` It goes when anything else comes |
  | `waitingOnThem` | `It’s waiting on Dima.` |
  | Answered, `by: obi` | `Dima wasn’t bothered.` |
  | Answered, `by: owner` | `Dima answered this one, not its Oby.` |
  | Declined, `owner_declined` | `Dima said no to this one.` |
  | Declined, `busy` | `Dima’s Oby is busy with other questions and didn’t take this one. Ask again later.` |
  | Declined, `out_of_scope` | `Dima’s Oby doesn’t take questions like this one.` |
  | Declined, `policy` | `This Obysphere’s rules stopped Dima’s Oby answering. An admin can change them.` |
  | Declined, anything else | `Dima’s Oby declined without saying why.` |
  | Expired, or declined `expired` once their obid is back | `Dima’s Oby never answered: the question expired.` |

  `by` and `reason` are the sender's own claims: they pick these words, and never show. A review or a question of theirs says `It’s waiting on Dima.` while `waitingOnThem` is true, and past that has only its plain state: `Declined`, `No answer came`, `Called off`. The Sphere's rules keep no exchange, so that one is the box's refusal, above.
- **The box** takes words alone, signed by main (WORDS-12), and keeps them until obid takes them: any refusal leaves them there with obid's words over it. It stays while `member` and `takesQuestions` are null, since that's obid not knowing. Only `false` shuts it, saying so. One send per owner: words on their way hold the box of a view opened meanwhile, the owner having clicked away and back, so nothing asks twice, and only what went leaves the draft. A refusal stays with the thread until the next words, so one that came while the owner was elsewhere still shows. A draft is the Sphere's own, as owner ids are.
- **Reading.** Each `peerThread` event for a thread it has opened, and the stream coming back, read it again, and an older answer never replaces a newer one. obid away leaves what shows. A `404` or `409` shuts the box with obid's words, until the view next opens and it's read again; leaving the Sphere closes the thread. What was typed stays the box's draft.

### Failures and recovery

- The Sphere can't be reached: the thread still reads, from what obid keeps. It asks the Sphere nothing, and goes by the directory as last read. Before obid has read it since it started, it doesn't know who is a member: `member` and `takesQuestions` are null, `name` is the owner id, and no one is `404`. It's read in the next poll that reaches the Sphere.
- obid restarted: exchanges are kept, so the thread is whole. An app that missed events reads it again.
- A reply obid built but hasn't sent isn't a post until the Sphere has it: it may be sealed by then. `exchange.state` already says where it stands.

### Invariants and boundaries

- **It's only that member.** By exact owner id, never a name or an Obi id: a card that goes by someone else's name moves nothing between threads. Only exchanges of this membership: they outlive one, but leaving forgets every pinned owner key, so nothing says the next `sam` is the same person, whether on another server with the same Sphere id or on this one. Rejoining starts every thread empty. Within a membership a member's owner key is pinned at first sight (identity.md 2.2), so a second key can't claim the id.
- **What comes back is data.** `text` and `about` are what someone else wrote, and `by` is what the sender says of itself, as in the room. An app shows `text` as a quote, as a text node, never as HTML or a link it chose. Nothing in a thread starts, changes or approves anything.
- **Nothing a member chose reaches a prompt.** Reading a thread, or hearing of one, runs no model, marks no conversation and adds nothing to a briefing. Its `name` is for the owner's eyes.
- **Never what a Sphere said:** a post's `error` is obid's own words. `GET /v1/obid/sphere/exchanges/{id}` still has the exchange's own `error`.
- **Nothing goes out unscanned:** reading sends nothing. A question passes the secret scan before anything leaves: a finding, or a scan that can't run, sends nothing and keeps nothing.
- **A question from the thread reaches no prompt:** it adds no turn, marks no conversation, starts nothing, and `oby_asks` and the telling of what came back (crate::brain::ask) never read it: they go by the conversation that asked, and none did.
- **Only the person answers for the person:** a post marked `by: owner` from this Oby is signed by one of their devices, a paired phone's, or a Confirm of theirs (PEER-33).
- **The API stays add-only.** Two new routes and one new event, which every app ignores until it knows it (`default` in apps/shared/Runner.swift's and Runner.kt's event parsers, and in obid-wire.ts's). `kind` is the protocol's message type, an open string, on a list no existing app decodes. No enum is touched.

## The person's card

The other end of "It's waiting on Dana". A question only its owner can answer is the `obiRequest` obid already keeps (docs/contracts/ask-an-oby.md, "Only the person can answer"): there is no second inbox, and every app that shows that item goes on showing it. The words below are proposals: this design leads for now, by the owner's decision, and Sangam can change them.

- **Where.** One card per question. On Home's stack, like everything else waiting on the owner, under the title `Question from <owner id>`, and in that member's thread, over the box: the same item and the same state, so what's typed or refused shows in both. It alerts on the Mac like any item that needs the owner (`triage: needs_you`), with the asker's words as plain text in place of obid's "quoted below"; a click opens Home, and the alert has no buttons, since an answer takes the click in the window. "Questions from other Obys" still says it, once: its words there answer it too, through a Confirm (ask-an-oby.md).
- **What it shows.** `Question`, "Only you can answer this one.", the asker's words as a quote and a text node, their choices and the repositories it's about, then **Answer**, **Decline** and **Later**. Answer opens a box for the person's own words, one line, up to 2,000 characters. Words that are exactly `Decline` count as a decline, as they always have (`QUESTION_OPTIONS`).
- **What goes back.** An answer is `answer` with `by: owner` and their words, so the asker reads "Dana answered this one, not its Oby." A decline is `decline` with `reason: owner_declined` and no words, so they read "Dana said no to this one." In the thread on the person's own side, an answer is their own bubble.
- **Only their hand.** `POST /v1/inbox/{id}/answer` with an answer or a decline to an open question takes `X-Oby-Words`, the device's signature (WORDS-2: main signs only on a click or keystroke it saw itself, in exactly this shape, its words held by the box or the button's label), or a paired phone through the gateway. Anything else is `403`, the card stays open and nothing goes. **Later** takes no signature, since it says nothing to anyone. Inside obid, a conversation's turn can't either: `oby_answer` carries it out at once only on the owner's own signed word, and otherwise makes a Confirm showing every word, a Decline too, which until this was one-off. **What this stops, and no more:** obid's token alone, so a job, an agent, another Oby or a script with no input of the owner's. A script that took the whole page over keeps the ceiling the owner accepted on 2026-10-30 ([signed-words.md](signed-words.md)): one request of its choosing per real input of the owner's in the window, and that holds here. An agent has no token, and another Oby only sends typed actions. A device key that can't be read leaves the words in the box, with these words over them: "Only you answer this one, from your own app: this answer wasn't signed, so nothing was sent."
- **Their words are data.** The asker's words reach a prompt only as `oby_inbox` quotes them, which makes that turn untrusted, and they are text nodes everywhere. They choose no button.
- **Nothing unscanned leaves.** The person's words pass the secret scan before they go. A finding, or a scan that can't run, is `422`: nothing is sent, the words aren't repeated, the card stays open, and the app keeps them in the box with obid's words over them. So does any other refusal.
- **How it ends.** Settled anywhere, the item is `done` with an `inbox` event, so it closes on every device and its alert goes. A second answer is `409`. The conversation says how it ended: "You answered this question from Dana's Oby:" or "You said no to this question from Dana's Oby:", the question quoted, never the answer. A question the asker no longer waits for closes as the inbox already closes one: they call it off, and it says so there; a day passes with no answer (`expires_at`), and obid declines it `expired`, with nobody's words, and it says so there. A card closing under someone typing takes their words with it, and what they read is that line. The asker's thread reads `Called off` or `No answer came`.
- **Phones.** Nothing they read changed: the `kind`, `options`, `text` and `obi` are as they were, so a phone without this shows the card it always did and answers in words or with Decline. Both phones sign when their key is registered, and a paired phone that doesn't still goes, through the gateway. Swift's `ObiAsk` and Kotlin's item decode these keys as open strings, and no value is new.

## Being asked to share, and what your Oby tells

The other side of a thread: for a member's Oby to answer by itself, its owner must have said it may. Joining doesn't say so (below), so every question waits on its owner and the asker reads "It's waiting on Dana" until they do. The words below are proposals: this design leads for now, by the owner's decision, and Sangam can change them.

- **Asked once, where the owner already is.** The first question that waits on an owner who has no rule, and was never asked, brings one Confirm card on the stack, over your conversation and on the empty chat (docs/contracts/chat-first.md), with the orange dot on Chats and Oby's row (and on the phones, and as a Mac notification, like any Confirm): "Which repositories can I tell people in your Obysphere about?", with the owner's repositories to tick ("Only the repositories the owner chooses", below), that it starts with the questions waiting now, what it tells and what it never tells, all of it in the three lines the stack's card shows. obid marks it asked when it makes the card, so no other question, member or restart asks again. Nothing else asks: not obid's start, not a join.
- **The card holds obid's own words only**, and the owner's own repositories, from their configuration and their jobs. Neither who asked nor their words are in it: nothing a member chose shapes what the owner's device signs. The question itself is on its own card, as ever.
- **Yes.** One rule, `{"for": "*", "repos": [what's ticked], "tells": ["jobStatus", "jobProgress"]}`, answering who asks and opening no room, since the card promises nothing about the room. Every question waiting on the owner then goes by it, as if it had come now: so the asker who read "It's waiting on Dana" gets their answer, and a member isn't stuck behind a question they already asked (`busy`). The card says so. Four stay with the owner: one about a repository they didn't tick; one the material can't answer, which comes back; one whose answer they already wrote, waiting on its Confirm, since a model's words mustn't replace theirs; and one from someone no longer an active member, who gets nothing (by the directory as obid last read it, up to about 60 seconds old: a member removed within that minute may still get this answer). "Questions from other Obys" says how it ended: "I answered this question from Dana's Oby by myself:", or that it said no because what it wrote didn't pass the secret scan, quoted.
- **No (Cancel).** Nothing changes, and it doesn't ask again. Questions keep waiting on the owner.
- **Listed.** The Obysphere's settings list "What your Oby tells": each rule's what, to whom, in which repositories and how many times it answered, with Remove, and on the rule their yes made, "Change repositories". With nothing shared, "Nothing yet" and a button that opens the same card, whole, so no and Remove aren't one-way. What Confirm signs is on screen.
- **Removed stays removed.** Removing any rule also marks the owner asked: no start or question brings the rule or the card back, and only their own click does. Leaving, below, is what starts over: the next Sphere asks again. A `sharing.json` from before this that a person emptied by hand through the API can't be told from an upgrade's, so it gets the one card; a card only asks. A file that doesn't read shares nothing and stays as it is: no card, and `409` for anything the owner does that would write over it (Share, a Confirm, a rule, the limits).
- **Leaving ends it.** A rule obid made (the owner's yes, the room's) names no Sphere, and what they agreed to was that Sphere's. Leaving takes it, any open card, and that they were asked, so the next Sphere asks again instead of sharing with no click. Rules they wrote themselves stay.
- **How the click is signed.** It's a Confirm (docs/runner-api.md, "Confirm takes the owner's tap"): the device signs `oby-confirm\n<item id>\n<title>\n<signedAt>`, within two minutes, once, and obid refuses `Confirm` without it (`403`, the item open, no rule). In the Electron app, main signs it for this action only on a press of a button labelled Confirm: the preload saw the owner's own event hold that label, and main saw a real click, Enter or Space itself. That stops a script acting on an input it didn't arrange, no more: [signed-words.md](signed-words.md) states what a script in the page can still do. A phone signs on the owner's tap. A rule made any other way, `POST /v1/obid/sharing/rules`, takes the device's signature too, and no app sends it: obid's token, which the page reaches through main, isn't enough to widen what's told. Removing a rule takes only the token, as Settings' Remove does: it shares less, and Share brings it back. `POST /v1/obid/sphere/join` carries no device signature, so it can't be what shares: since SPH-8 no join makes a rule, a new member's or anyone's (PEER-40). The token alone doesn't change how much answers may spend either: `PUT /v1/obid/sharing/limits` takes the device's signature, and no app sends it (PEER-41). **No agent, other Oby or job can grant it:** agents can't reach these routes, another Oby sends only typed actions and a question's words are data, and inside obid nothing confirms without a device.

## What "What's the progress?" gets

The owner's goal, in their words: "if I am working on something my Oby would be able to answer what is the progress of what I'm doing". An answer's material was only each job's title, repository, stage, state and pull request. Under a rule with `jobProgress` (the owner's decision 28) it also holds each job's current step and latest report line.

- **What's told, exactly.** While a job runs: what it's doing, from its latest tool call, and its agent's latest words (`context`), else the first line of its report. Once it ended: no step, and the first line of its report. Each at most 200 characters, and where one is cut, no piece of a token stays at the end. That's all: never a log, a diff, a prompt, a file's contents, a secret, a login, a conversation or its transcript.
- **A step isn't its arguments.** The owner agreed to a step, not to what a command was given, and the scan misses common credentials in one (`mysql -u root -pS3cretPassw0rd9`). So a member hears a command as its program ("Running mysql") or the description its agent gave it, and a search as "Searching the code". A file's name stays ("Editing retry.rs"). Only tool calls make this line, never a refusal's words or an error. The owner's own screen shows the step as it always did.
- **No conversation.** Once the owner has spoken to a job (words to it, or an answer to its question or permission), what its agent says can be its reply to them, so it's no longer told: only its step, and a report written before they spoke, kept apart by `STEERED`. A report that can't be told from a reply isn't told, which is true of a job they spoke to before its first report. It stays that way for the rest of the job.
- **The line is the agent's own.** An agent repeating the first words of what it was asked has that line left out. An agent putting its prompt in its own words isn't caught: that's the limit of this, and the owner's yes covers it, which the card says ("an agent's own words").
- **Through the scan, whole and alone, before the run.** Each line goes through the secret scan by itself, before any cut, so a secret across the cut or across two lines is seen. One it stops goes, whole, while the others stay. A scan that can't run tells no line. So a secret in a line never reaches the run, which can't retell what it never read. The answer is scanned again as it goes, as every answer is. It costs one scan per line, 40 at most an answer.
- **Quoted data under the same fence.** A line can hold text from outside (an agent read it somewhere). It reaches the model only in `material.json`, under `agentWrote`, which the fixed prompt calls quoted data and never instructions. The run still has Read alone, in its own folder, inside the sandbox: a line that says "ignore your instructions" can at worst change the words of an answer, which holds only what the run read, is scanned, and stays a quote on the asker's side.
- **A question only.** The room's `found` goes by the room's card, which lists a task's title, repository and pull request, and it hears none of this, whatever its rule holds.

Run for real on 2026-11-03, on the rig with Dana's real job (PEER-3's steps below), before a command was cut to its program. Asked with the job editing its 4th file: "Dana has one Oby task running: Billing retries, in the work stage. It is currently editing step-04 tests, and its latest report says step 02 is committed and step 03 is not yet written, with work continuing." Asked again with 26 commits made: "Dana's Billing retries task is running at the work stage. It is editing step-26.test.js, and steps 01 through 19 are committed. It is committing step 20 now, with 10 more to go." An agent's words lag its step, and the answer says both as written. Neither ask added anything to Dana's inbox.

**A yes isn't widened silently.** An owner who said yes to a list hasn't agreed to more, so `jobProgress` is its own value of `tells`, and only what follows adds it:

- **The card lists it from now on.** One card, one latch (`offered`), one `share_status` Confirm: a yes to it means every line it lists, so a member from before is asked once, not once for status and again for progress.
- **A rule the owner wrote keeps telling what it listed**, across upgrades and restarts. A rule's `tells` changes only by the owner's signed click.
- **An owner whose own rule tells everyone status in every repository, and was never asked,** gets the same one card the first time a question is answered by itself, in words that ask only what it adds: "Can I also tell people in your Obysphere each task's current step and latest report line?" The full card's title names them too, since that's what the device signs. That question and any until a yes are answered with status. Yes adds `jobProgress` to the rule they have, and to any other that tells status, since those people are among everyone. No changes nothing, and it doesn't ask again. This is the one time an answer by itself also brings a card, which is why ASK-11 says no question rather than an empty inbox.
- **A rule for some people or repositories asks nothing:** it's the owner's own list. They can name `jobProgress` themselves (`POST /v1/obid/sharing/rules`, signed), always with `jobStatus`.
- **Leaving takes the yes back.** A rule obid made goes (below), and a rule the owner wrote loses `jobProgress` if only their yes put it there: the next Sphere asks again. A rule that lists it in their own words keeps it.
- **Remove closes an open card.** Its yes would share more than its words say, so it goes with the rule, and asking again from Settings opens a card that says all of it.
- **No or Remove aren't one-way.** The Obysphere's settings show "Also tell what each task is doing" while a rule tells everyone status and none tells progress, and it opens the same card, whole.

## Only the repositories the owner chooses

The owner's decision 28: an Oby tells what the owner chooses, asked once at the first question. Before this, a yes covered every repository and a first join made that rule with no click, so a teammate could read a personal job's title and a private repository's name. There is one question, not two: one latch (`offered`) and one card (one `share_status` Confirm), which this changes to ask which repositories. The words are proposals, as above.

- **Nothing is ticked, and nothing ticks them all.** A click mustn't share private work unread, so the owner ticks each repository they mean, and there's no "all". The list is fair however long it is: every repository of theirs, each name whole, the ones they most recently worked on first, then the rest of their configuration, scrolling in place, never a repository they only hold a teammate's job in. It reads only those two: never all their code host can list. A rule holds at most 50 (as any), and past that the card says so. A repository added later isn't told until they tick it. A question while the card is open lists what's new on it, ticks as they are.
- **With nothing to list, nothing asks.** A new user reaches their Obysphere before any task. A question that finds no repository to list waits on them as any does, makes no card and doesn't spend that they were asked: the next question brings it, once there's one to list. Settings' Share says so too (`409`, "run a task first").
- **Confirm signs what's ticked.** Each tick goes to `POST /v1/obid/sharing/offer` with `{"repos": […]}`, which shares nothing and answers the same card, its title naming exactly them: "Can I tell people in your Obysphere how your tasks are going, with each one's current step and latest report line, in acme/app and acme/web?". The device signs that title, as for any Confirm, so a signature for other ticks doesn't pass. With nothing ticked Confirm does nothing (`409`, the card still open) and the app's button is off, as it is while a tick is on its way or one was refused; the boxes follow what obid has. A repository that isn't the owner's, `*` or a bad name is `422`. A tick never opens a card (`409` with none open), so one on its way when they say no doesn't ask them again. As with every Confirm, a page script could change the ticks just before a real click: [signed-words.md](signed-words.md) states that limit.
- **A phone shows the card and can't tick yet** (SPH-13, SPH-14): its Confirm on an empty card reads "Choose the repositories first, in Oby on your Mac: nothing was shared." With ticks made on the Mac, the title names them and its Confirm goes.
- **A job outside the rule is never told.** obid decides, before any model runs: an answer's material holds no job or result of a repository outside the rule, so the run can't name it, count it or hint at it, and its fixed prompt says the items are all it knows ("never say or guess that they have other tasks"). A question that names an outside repository, or a job there, waits on the owner like any they didn't share, also when that repository or job doesn't exist, and tells the asker nothing else: a job names its repository whether or not obid knows it, so what happens to a question says nothing of which jobs are real. A question naming no repository ("What's the progress?") is answered from the repositories they chose.
- **Changing them reaches a run already going.** A run reads the material when it starts, and can take 90 seconds. What it wrote goes only if the rule still covers every repository it was told of, whatever the question named; otherwise the question goes to the owner, and the room's `found` is refused, also from its queue. A Change that takes away nothing it read lets the answer go. An answer already in the outbox, while the Sphere can't be reached, still goes: it's the moment of the check that counts, as for a rule removed.
- **A job with no repository is never told,** under any rule, a rule for every repository too: nobody could tick it. (Every job names one today; this holds if that changes.)
- **A rule the owner wrote keeps what it says.** Its `repos: "*"` stays and answers: it's their word, signed. One for some repositories keeps sending a question that names none to them, as it did: a yes isn't what they gave it. Change never touches it.
- **What said "every repository" is asked again, not kept.** A first join's rule was never clicked. A yes to "in every repository" did say so, but it was days old and never released (SPH-7, in no published build), and decision 28 is what the owner wants told. So at its next start obid removes those rules (`starter` or the room's, with `repos: "*"`), closes a card still open with those words, and clears that they were asked. They tell nothing from then on, a teammate reads "It's waiting on you", and the first question brings the card. No is still no. obid doesn't narrow them by a guess: only the owner knows which repositories are private. Nothing is ever widened by this.
- **Settings changes them the same way.** "Change repositories" opens the same card with what's chosen ticked, and a Confirm changes that one rule, under its id. Questions waiting go by it as after a first yes. Remove stays what it was.
- **The room goes no further than the yes.** Its card names the repositories it would post about, and its rule covers those. Change narrows a room's rule to what's now chosen, and takes it away with none left; it never grows back by itself: Remove and "Also post in the room" do that, with a card that names them. A room's card still open when they change closes.
- **The first join makes no rule.** So a new member and an old one reach the same card, the first question asks, and a join, which no device signs, shares nothing.

## Interfaces and dependencies

- `GET /v1/obid/sphere/peers/{owner}?limit=50`, `POST /v1/obid/sphere/peers/{owner}/ask` and the `peerThread` event: [runner-api.md](../runner-api.md), "obid only: a member's thread". On the gateway's allowlist, the read and the event only: the room's `POST` isn't on it either, and no phone asks from a thread until SPH-13 and SPH-14 add the route with their signing.
- `GET /v1/obid/sharing`, `POST /v1/obid/sharing/offer`, `DELETE /v1/obid/sharing/rules/{id}`, `PUT /v1/obid/sharing/limits` and the `share_status` Confirm: [runner-api.md](../runner-api.md), "obid only: sharing rules".
- `Exchange::posts` builds a post, and `QUIET` and `waiting_on_them` are the same rule `oby_ask`'s report goes by (docs/contracts/ask-an-oby.md), imported rather than copied.
