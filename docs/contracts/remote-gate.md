# The factory's remote gate: Linux checks on the gate VM, macOS checks on a second Mac

The business behavior contract for running the factory's gate on the Azure VM `oby-gate`, and its macOS part on the owner's second Mac, the Mac worker (`oby-mac-pro`), from [the template](../business-contract-template.md). Scenarios are numbered `REMOTE-n`. The design and the hosts as they are: [factory-remote-gate.md](../factory-remote-gate.md). Task A00-03 built the VM's side; A00-10 built the gate's; A00-18 built the Mac worker; A00-19 made the SSH rule follow the Mac's IP and the idle stop count only the VM's uses; A00-31 sent builders' heavy Rust runs (`factory/bin/test-run`) to the VM, in builder slots of their own; A00-33 closed three holes its security review found in them: a builder run could hold a gate's lock, keep a login, or leave something behind that steers a later run.

## Feature and status

- **Outcome:** a factory gate's Linux check groups (rust, electron, scan) run on a 32-core VM, up to four at once, instead of one at a time on the owner's Mac, which keeps only the macOS-only suites, the factory's own tests, and the whole check for a change to the check's own files. With a Mac worker set, those macOS groups (rust for macOS-only code, swift, factory) run on the second Mac when it is free, so two gates' macOS parts run at once, one on each Mac. With builder slots set, a builder's heavy Rust run that doesn't need macOS (`factory/bin/test-run -- cargo test …`) runs on the VM too, in a builder slot of its own, several at once, instead of one at a time on the owner's Mac behind its lock.
- **Status:** all thirty-six scenarios are **implemented** (REMOTE-1 to 36). REMOTE-2, 7, 8 and 9 by `factory/remote/oby-remote-gate`, tested by `factory/tests/test_remote_gate.py` (A00-03, A00-10); REMOTE-11 by `factory/remote/setup.sh` and `factory/remote/tighten.sh`, tested by `factory/tests/test_remote_gate.py` (A00-12); REMOTE-1, 3, 4, 5 and 6 by `factory/bin/gate` and `factory/bin/job queue`, tested by `factory/tests/test_gate_remote.py` (A00-10; REMOTE-4's factory group, A00-13); REMOTE-10 by `factory/bin/gate`, tested by `factory/tests/test_gate_remote.py` (A00-13); REMOTE-12 by `factory/remote/setup.sh`, tested by `factory/tests/test_remote_gate.py` (A00-15); REMOTE-13 to 16 by `factory/bin/gate`, tested by `factory/tests/test_gate_remote.py`, REMOTE-17 by `factory/remote/oby-remote-gate --mac` and REMOTE-18 by `factory/remote/setup-mac.sh` and `factory/remote/tighten.sh`, tested by `factory/tests/test_remote_gate.py` (A00-18); REMOTE-19, 20 and 21, and REMOTE-3 and 5 as they are now, by `factory/bin/gate`, tested by `factory/tests/test_gate_remote.py` (A00-19); REMOTE-22 to 29 and 31 by `factory/bin/test-run`, `factory/bin/gate` and `factory/remote/oby-remote-gate --run`, tested by `factory/tests/test_test_run_remote.py` (REMOTE-28 also by `test_gate_remote.py` and `test_remote_gate.py`), and REMOTE-30 by `factory/remote/setup.sh`, tested by `factory/tests/test_remote_gate.py` (A00-31); REMOTE-32 to 34 by `factory/remote/setup.sh` and `factory/remote/oby-remote-gate`, tested by `factory/tests/test_remote_gate.py` (A00-33); REMOTE-35 by `factory/remote/setup.sh`, tested by `factory/tests/test_remote_gate.py`, and REMOTE-36 by `factory/bin/test-run`, tested by `factory/tests/test_test_run_remote.py` (A00-35). The remote path is **off** until the owner adds a `remote_gate` key to `factory/config.json` (protected, so the owner's): until then every gate runs on the Mac as before. The Mac worker is **off** until the owner also adds a `mac_worker` key: until then the macOS part runs on the owner's Mac as before. Builders' runs stay on the owner's Mac until the owner runs `setup.sh` again (it adds the user `oby-builder`) and sets `builder_slots` in `remote_gate`; until then test-run behaves as before and says nothing new.
- **Scope:** the VM's setup (`factory/remote/setup.sh`, and `factory/remote/tighten.sh`, which it runs last on the host), the script that runs a commit's Linux groups there (`factory/remote/oby-remote-gate`), and the gate's side: where each part runs, starting the VM, moving the SSH rule's source to the Mac's current IP, the fallback to the Mac, and deallocating the idle VM from the Mac. Builders' runs: `factory/bin/test-run` deciding where a heavy run goes by the gate's own macOS rule, what it sends and how it falls back; `oby-remote-gate --run` on the VM; the no-sudo user `oby-builder` that `setup.sh` makes for it; and the gate and builder slot counts in `remote_gate`. The Mac worker: its setup (`factory/remote/setup-mac.sh`, which runs the same `tighten.sh`), the same script's Mac mode (`oby-remote-gate --mac`), and the gate sending its macOS part there once the VM's part passed, with the fallback to the owner's Mac. **Out of scope:** records in `~/.oby-flow/pipelines/runs.jsonl`, the VM's state and slots in the dashboard's header, and creating or changing any other Azure resource (the owner's). For builders' runs: sending them to the Mac worker, sending the whole check (`oby-check.sh`, `oby-check-blok`) or a Swift or Xcode run to the VM, forwarding any environment variable or login, installing `cargo-nextest`, Node or gitleaks for `oby-builder`, builder slots in the dashboard, and pruning a finished job's `core/target` (`factory/bin/job` already removes its whole worktree). For the Mac worker: builders' `factory/bin/test-run` and the nightly check (`factory/bin/nightly`) on it, whole-check runs on it (a change to the check's own files, a gate with no Linux group planned, and any fallback from the VM stay on the owner's Mac), Linux groups on it, its slots in the dashboard, and installing anything on it but `oby-check-blok`.

## Behavior

### Actors and ownership

- **The gate** (`factory/bin/gate`): reads the check's plan, starts the VM when it is stopped, runs the Linux groups there over SSH and the Mac's part on the Mac, moves the SSH rule's source to the Mac's current IP when SSH doesn't answer, falls back to the Mac, and writes `checks.md`. Every use of the VM is recorded in `factory/jobs/remote-gate/state.json`: open while the gate uses it, then ended.
- **The queue** (`factory/bin/job queue`, every few minutes): runs `factory/bin/gate --idle`, which deallocates the idle VM. The VM has no managed identity, so it can't deallocate itself: the Mac does.
- **The remote gate** (`oby-remote-gate`, on the VM): takes a slot, fetches the commit from origin, runs the commit's own `scripts/oby-check.sh` for its Linux groups, offline, and exits 0, 1 or 2.
- **The Mac worker** (`oby-mac-pro`, the owner's MacBook Pro, reached over SSH): runs `~/oby-worker/bin/oby-remote-gate --mac` for the macOS groups the gate gives it, in one of two slots, and exits 0, 1 or 2. It holds no factory state, and the factory never starts, wakes or stops it.
- **test-run** (`factory/bin/test-run`, the copy in a builder's worktree): for a heavy run, decides by the gate's own rule whether it needs macOS, writes the worktree's tree, scans what it would send, reaches the VM as the gate does and runs the command there over SSH as `oby-builder`, or on this Mac as before. Its use of the VM is recorded in the main checkout's `state.json`, as a gate's is.
- **The builder run** (`oby-remote-gate --run`, on the VM as `oby-builder`, which has no sudo and can write neither its home, its logins nor its toolchain, all root's): takes a builder slot, empties it and the builder root of what an earlier run left, resets its clone's settings, rebuilds the worktree's tree from the base and the payload, and runs the builder's command there, offline, at nice 15, with a Cargo home of the slot's own.
- **The owner:** owns the VM, its budget and its network rule, and the `remote_gate` key that turns the remote path on (the gate may move the rule `ssh-from-owner`'s source to this Mac's current `/32`, REMOTE-19, and change nothing else about it or any other rule); owns the Mac worker, its sleep and network, and the `mac_worker` key that turns it on.

### Triggers and prerequisites

- `setup.sh` has run against the host: the pinned tools, `/srv/oby/repo.git` and four slots; for builders' runs, the user `oby-builder`, with a toolchain root installed in its root-owned home, and `/srv/oby-builder/repo.git`.
- After A00-33 merges, the owner runs `factory/remote/setup.sh` again, with no builder run going, so its fixes reach the VM (`/srv/oby` `0700`, `oby-builder`'s home, keys and toolchain made root's); until then the VM keeps A00-31's modes, and the gate locks become `0600` only once the new `oby-remote-gate` opens them.
- `remote_gate` in `factory/config.json` names the VM, or `FACTORY_REMOTE_GATE` does for one run.
- The commit to check is on origin (`obi-ai-bot/oby` is public, so no token is needed).
- The caller keeps the remote gate's standard input open, as `ssh` without `-n` does.
- For the Mac worker: `setup-mac.sh` has run against it (`~/oby-worker/repo.git`, two slots, the runner, `oby-check-blok`), `remote_gate` is on, and `mac_worker` in `factory/config.json` names it, or `FACTORY_MAC_WORKER` does for one run.

### States and transitions

- **Waiting for a slot:** all four (or `--slots`') held; it says so once and tries again every second.
- **In slot N:** fetch and checkout under one repository lock, then the plan, then each Linux group in order (rust, electron, scan), every one to its end. swift is never run there.
- **Ended:** 1 when a group failed, else 2 when one couldn't judge, else 0, with a last line naming the verdict, the slot and the seconds.
- **A builder's run** (`--run`): in a builder slot (`builder-1` to `builder-<m>`, never a gate's), waiting at most `--wait` seconds for one; the base checked out, the payload applied, the tree checked, every file under `core/` and `tools/` touched, `cargo fetch`; then `running in builder slot <N>`, the command, and `ended <code> in builder slot <N> after <s>s`.
- **On the Mac worker** (`--mac`): the first of two free slots, or, with both held, `both slots are busy` and exit 2 at once, with no wait; then fetch and checkout as above, and exactly the groups it was given, in that order, every one to its end.

### Failures and recovery

- A commit origin doesn't have: nothing runs, exit 2.
- A dropped connection (standard input's end), a failed write or SIGHUP, SIGTERM or SIGINT: the check's whole session is stopped and the slot is free.
- A plan that can't run, a missing clone, or a group whose setup (`cargo fetch`, `npm ci`) fails: exit 2, couldn't judge.
- SSH that doesn't answer within 180 seconds: the gate reads this Mac's public IP and, when it changed, moves the SSH rule's source to it and gives SSH 60 seconds more (REMOTE-19, 20).
- On the Mac, the gate runs the whole check there, as without a VM, and says why in `checks.md`, when the settings are invalid, the plan can't be read, the commit isn't on origin, the VM can't be started or reached within 180 seconds (and 60 more after moving the rule), or the remote gate couldn't judge (2) or was cut off (ssh's 255, a signal). A remote failure (1) is a verdict and isn't run again on the Mac.
- A builder's run runs on the owner's Mac exactly as before (under the lock, at nice 15 and half the cores), with one line `factory/bin/test-run: fell back to this Mac: <why>`, when the VM can't be started or reached within 180 seconds (and 60 more after moving the rule), the builder user can't log in (ssh's 255), every builder slot stays busy for 600 seconds, or anything else fails before the VM says `running in builder slot <N>`. After that line, the VM's exit code is the verdict, a failure included, and the command doesn't run again here. A stop (SIGTERM or SIGINT to test-run) or a dropped connection stops the run's session on the VM and frees its slot.
- The Mac worker's part runs on the owner's Mac, as without a Mac worker (each group through `checks.sh`, rust under its lock), and `checks.md` says `Fell back from <host> to this Mac: <why>`, when the Mac worker doesn't answer `ssh <host> true` within 60 seconds (asleep, off the network), says both its slots are busy, couldn't judge (2) or was cut off (any other code but 0 or 1), or when its settings are invalid. A failure there (1) is a verdict and isn't run again.

### Invariants and boundaries

- No two gates ever share a slot's worktree or build folder, and no builder's run shares a slot with a gate or another builder's run: gates never wait for a builder, a hostile builder run included. `/srv/oby` is the SSH user's alone (`0700`) and every lock a gate takes (`slot-N/lock`, `repo.lock`) is `0600`, so no lock a gate takes can be opened by `oby-builder`.
- A builder's slot gives every file under `core/` and `tools/` the current time before each run, so a crate another tree built in its `target` never stands in for this one's (A00-23).
- The VM holds no secret, token, key or login; it fetches from the public repository with no credential.
- `oby-check-blok` on the VM is built from `origin/dev`, never from a commit being checked.
- Nothing on the check's path that setup installs or owns is writable by group or others.
- The only Azure actions the factory takes are starting and deallocating `oby-gate`, reading its state, reading the rule `ssh-from-owner`, and setting that rule's source to one `/32` address, this Mac's public IPv4.
- The factory never writes a range, tag or list, nor touches another rule; it creates nothing.
- A gate sends the VM nothing but the commit and the base (and the slot counts when set), validated on the Mac and quoted. A builder's run sends the base's SHA (on origin), its tree's SHA, its folder and its command, validated and quoted, and on standard input the binary diff from the base to its worktree's tree, which holds no ignored file and has passed `gitleaks` with the main checkout's `.gitleaks.toml`; a finding, or a scanner that can't run, keeps the run on the Mac. No environment variable, `CARGO_TARGET_DIR`, token, login or other file goes.
- `oby-builder` has no sudo (setup stops when `sudo -n -l -U oby-builder` shows it may run anything) and can't write the gates' `/srv/oby`.
- `oby-builder`'s logins and toolchain are root's: its home (`0755`, holding only `.ssh`, `.cargo`, `.rustup` and `.kani`), `.ssh` (`0755`, only `authorized_keys`), `authorized_keys` (`0644`, a copy of the SSH user's public keys) and everything under `.cargo`, `.rustup` and `.kani` are owned by root and writable by neither it, group nor others; setup checks that as `oby-builder` and stops naming any path it can still write. A builder run can't add a key, keep a login or change a later run's tools.
- Each builder run starts clean: its slot holds only its lock and its `target` folder, the builder root only `repo.git`, `repo.lock` and the `builder-N` slots, its worktree is added afresh, its Cargo home is the slot's own and emptied with it, the clone's `config` is written afresh (a bare clone, `gc.auto 0`) with no `info/attributes`, and every git call runs with hooks off. Nothing an earlier run left there but `target` reaches the next.
- The VM's groups build and test offline (`CARGO_NET_OFFLINE`, `npm_config_offline`), as on the Mac; only `cargo fetch` and `npm ci` before them have the network.
- The Mac worker holds no secret, token, key or login of the factory's, and setup runs nothing on it as root (no `sudo`).
- `oby-check-blok` on the Mac worker is built from `origin/dev`, never from a commit being checked.
- Nothing on the Mac worker's check path that setup installs or owns (`~/.cargo`, `~/.rustup`, `~/oby-worker` and its slots, the runner) is writable by group or others.
- The factory never starts, wakes or stops the Mac worker: no `az`, no Wake-on-LAN, no `jobs/remote-gate/state.json` stamp for it.
- The Mac worker's part never holds the owner's Mac's check lock; only a fallback to the owner's Mac takes it.
- Nothing goes to the Mac worker but the runner's home-relative path, `--mac`, the commit, the base and the group names, all validated, so the command reads the same in its fish and in bash.

### Known limits of builders' runs

Left as they are (A00-33). No merge depends on a builder's result: the gate runs everything again in its own slot, as the SSH user.

- Two builder runs at once share one user, `oby-builder`, so one can reach the other's slot while both run.
- The kept `target` folder can carry something an earlier run built or left there.
- A process a run starts in a new session of its own, or a `crontab` or `at` job, outlives the run: the script stops only its run's own session.
- The builder clone's object store, and shared system folders such as `/tmp`, are `oby-builder`'s or everyone's.
- Builder runs reach the network during `cargo fetch`; the offline settings are environment variables only.
- `HOME` is the real home, read-only to the run: a test that writes into `$HOME` fails there where it passes at the gate.
- Kani builds into each slot's kept `target/kani`, about 2 to 3 GB per slot.

## Interfaces and dependencies

- `oby-remote-gate [--slots <n> --builder-slots <m>] <commit> <base>`: a full 40-character SHA, and `origin/<branch>` or a full SHA. Output streamed; exit 0, 1 or 2 (128 + a signal when stopped). The counts, both or neither, set the gate slots (1 to 64, by default 4) and the core share, OBY_BUILD_JOBS = the cores / (slots + builder slots).
- `oby-remote-gate --run --slots <n> --builder-slots <m> --wait <seconds> <base> <tree> <folder> -- <command>...`: as `oby-builder`, root `/srv/oby-builder`. `<base>` and `<tree>` full SHAs, `<folder>` relative to the worktree (`[A-Za-z0-9._/-]+`, no `..`). Standard input: a line with the payload's size (at most 64 MiB), the payload (`git diff --binary --full-index <base> <tree>`), then held open. Before its git steps it empties the slot of all but `lock` and `target` and the root of all but `repo.git`, `repo.lock` and the `builder-<n>` folders, and, under `repo.lock`, writes `repo.git/config` afresh and removes `repo.git/info/attributes`; every git call has `-c core.hooksPath=/dev/null`. It runs the command's words with no shell, from `<slot>/worktree/<folder>`, under `nice -n 15`, offline, with `CARGO_TARGET_DIR=<slot>/target`, `CARGO_HOME=<slot>/cargo` (for its `cargo fetch` too) and `OBY_BUILD_JOBS`, `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS`, `RUST_TEST_THREADS` and `NEXTEST_TEST_THREADS` at max(1, the cores / (slots + builder slots)). Exit: the command's (128 + a signal when stopped), or 2 before `running in builder slot <N>`.
- `factory/remote/setup.sh [ssh-host]` and `setup.sh --pins [ci.yml]`: the pins are `.github/workflows/ci.yml`'s (`RUSTUP_TOOLCHAIN`, `GITLEAKS_VERSION` and `GITLEAKS_SHA256`, `node-version`), and it also installs every package ci.yml installs with `apt-get install`.
- `remote_gate` in `factory/config.json`: `{"ssh_host": "oby-gate", "resource_group": "oby-factory", "vm": "oby-gate"}`, each value matching `[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9._-]*`. Missing or `null`: the remote path is off. Optional `nsg` and `nsg_rule` name the SSH rule (by default `<vm>NSG` and `ssh-from-owner`, so `oby-gateNSG/ssh-from-owner`), matching the same pattern; an invalid one makes the settings invalid. Optional `slots` (an integer from 1 to 64, by default 4), `builder_slots` (0 to 64, by default 0, which keeps builders' runs on the Mac) and `builder_user` (the same pattern, by default `oby-builder`); a wrong type or a value out of range makes the settings invalid. With neither count set, the gate's call stays `oby-remote-gate <commit> origin/<base>`; with either set, it passes `--slots <n> --builder-slots <m>` first. On the 64-core VM, for example, `"slots": 4, "builder_slots": 8`.
- `FACTORY_REMOTE_GATE`: the same JSON, in the environment, in the key's place for one run (the tests, a trial gate).
- `factory/bin/test-run -- <command>`: a heavy run goes to the VM when the settings are valid with `builder_slots` 1 or more, no word's name is `swift`, `xcodebuild`, `oby-check.sh` or `oby-check-blok`, the worktree's changes against `git merge-base HEAD origin/<base>` (committed, uncommitted and new files that aren't ignored) touch no macOS-only file by the gate's rule (`MACOS_ONLY`, or a copy in the working tree or on the base naming `target_os = "macos"`), and no `--test <name>` names such a `core/crates/*/tests/<name>.rs`. Else `factory/bin/test-run: on this Mac: <why>`, once the builder path is on. The call: `ssh <the gate's ssh options> -l <builder_user> <ssh_host> <the --run words, quoted>`.
- `FACTORY_BUILDER_WAIT_SECONDS`: lowers the 600 seconds test-run gives `--wait`. A test hook only.
- `FACTORY_REMOTE_REACH_SECONDS`: shortens the 180 seconds the gate gives the VM to start and answer SSH, and the 60 after moving the rule (the smaller of 60 and it). A test hook only, as `OBY_REMOTE_ROOT` is.
- The IP service: one HTTPS GET to `https://checkip.amazonaws.com` (AWS's, no key, one IPv4 address in plain text), with a 10-second timeout and no body, extra header or credential. The answer counts only when, stripped, it is exactly one global IPv4 address (not private, loopback, link-local, multicast, reserved or unspecified). `FACTORY_REMOTE_IP_URL` replaces the URL, a `file://` one in the tests: a test hook only.
- `az network nsg rule show --resource-group <rg> --nsg-name <nsg> --name <rule> --output json` (the source is `sourceAddressPrefix`, else the one entry of `sourceAddressPrefixes`) and `az network nsg rule update … --source-address-prefixes <ip>/32`, each with a 60-second timeout, on the Mac's own `az` login.
- `factory/bin/gate --idle`: first drops each open use whose gate's `pid` is gone, as ended at its `started` (moving `used` to it when later); then, with no use open, deallocates the running VM (`az vm deallocate --no-wait`) once `used` is 30 or more minutes old and isn't `settled`, whatever stage any job is in; `factory/bin/job queue` runs it and passes what it says to stderr.
- `factory/jobs/remote-gate/state.json`, always the main checkout's (from git's common folder, whichever copy of `gate` or `test-run` runs; in a worktree of a bare clone, such as a slot on the VM or the Mac worker, which has no main checkout, the copy's own): `open`, the uses of the VM under way (`{"<uuid>": {"pid": <the gate's or test-run's pid>, "started": "<UTC>"}}`, opened before the gate or test-run reaches for the VM and closed when its part ends, whatever the outcome); `used`, the UTC time the last use ended; and `settled`, the `used` the idle rule last acted on. A file with only `used` has no use open. Every read and write is under an exclusive `flock` on `state.lock` beside it, with an atomic replace.
- `checks.md` with the remote path on: `HOST: oby-gate`, `oby-gate, mac` or `mac` after `CHECKS`, and a `## Where it ran` section (host, groups, slot, seconds, exit code, the moved rule, and why it fell back).
- `oby-remote-gate --mac <commit> <base> <group>...`: the same commit and base, then one or more of `rust`, `swift` and `factory`, run in that order; anything else, or no group, is a usage error (2). Two slots under `~/oby-worker` (`OBY_REMOTE_ROOT` for the tests); `both slots are busy` and 2 at once when both are held; OBY_BUILD_JOBS at the cores / 2; `~/.cargo/bin`, `/opt/homebrew/bin` and `/usr/local/bin` ahead of the inherited PATH.
- `factory/remote/setup-mac.sh [ssh-host]` (default `oby-mac-pro`): run from the owner's Mac, safe to repeat; everything on the host through `bash -s`. It stops before writing anything when a tool (git, python3, xcodebuild, rustup, cargo, rustc, node, gitleaks) or the `iPhone 17` simulator is missing, or the host's `rustc --version` or Xcode differs from the owner's Mac's; a Node or gitleaks difference is only said.
- `mac_worker` in `factory/config.json`: `{"ssh_host": "oby-mac-pro"}`, matching `[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9._-]*`. Missing or `null`: no Mac worker. It takes effect only with `remote_gate` on.
- `FACTORY_MAC_WORKER`: the same JSON, in the environment, in the key's place for one run.
- `checks.md` with a Mac worker: `HOST: oby-gate, oby-mac-pro` when it ran the macOS part, and in `## Where it ran` `- oby-mac-pro: <groups, rust with its macOS-only reason> in slot <N>: passed|failed with exit <code> in <s>s`; after a fallback, `HOST: oby-gate, mac` and the line `Fell back from <host> to this Mac: <why>` in `## Summary` and `## Where it ran`. The log has `==== <host>: <groups> ====` with its output, or `==== <host>: its output before the fallback, not part of the verdict ====`.
- `factory/remote/setup.sh` makes `/srv/oby` the SSH user's with mode `0700`, set even when it exists (`gate_root`). It also makes `oby-builder`: `/bin/bash`, no sudo or admin group, no sudoers entry, a home that is `root:root` `0755` and holds only `.ssh`, `.cargo`, `.rustup` and `.kani` (anything else, `useradd`'s skeleton files included, is removed), `~/.ssh` made afresh by root (`0755`) with `authorized_keys` a root-owned copy of the SSH user's (`0644`), `/srv/oby-builder` (its own, with a bare clone, `gc.auto 0`; builder slots are made on demand), and rustup with the pinned toolchain, rustfmt and clippy installed by root into `~oby-builder/.cargo` and `~oby-builder/.rustup` (`--no-modify-path`; when anything in its home isn't root's, as on A00-31's VM, these and `.kani` are removed and installed afresh), and Kani at `setup.sh`'s own pin (not ci.yml's), installed by root: `cargo install --locked kani-verifier` into `~oby-builder/.cargo`, then `cargo kani setup` with `KANI_HOME` at `~oby-builder/.kani` (its bundle there, its nightly in `.rustup`), and `.kani` given to root; it is left as it is when `kani-verifier` is installed at the pin, `.kani/kani-<pin>` exists and `.kani` holds no `*.tar.gz`. The toolchain and the root are tightened with `tighten.sh`'s rule; `cargo --version`, `rustc --version` and `cargo kani --version` are run as `oby-builder` with a Cargo home in a scratch folder, and `cargo kani` proves a one-harness scratch crate there offline; and setup stops when `oby-builder` can write its home, `.ssh`, `authorized_keys`, `.cargo`, `.cargo/bin`, `.rustup`, `.kani`, `.kani/kani-<pin>` or its `bin`. It changes no sshd setting.
- Depends on `scripts/oby-check.sh`'s `OBY_CHECK_PLAN` and `OBY_CHECK_ONLY`, on SSH hosts `oby-gate` and (with a Mac worker) `oby-mac-pro`, and on `az` signed in on the Mac.

## Acceptance scenarios

- **REMOTE-1** (implemented).
