# The app's Obysphere, for launch

The business behavior contract for what the Electron app does in an Obysphere at launch, from [the template](../business-contract-template.md): joining from a link pasted in the app, the members rule and a membership that grow to take handoffs, a revoked membership that says so, handing a member's Oby a task, and what is hidden for launch. obid's side is [obysphere.md](obysphere.md), [handoff.md](handoff.md) and [peer-thread.md](peer-thread.md); the rules' engine is [sphere-policy.md](sphere-policy.md). Scenarios are numbered `SPHEREAPP-n`, each tested as `sphereapp_n_…` in `apps/electron/src/renderer/src/test/` (`sphere.test.ts`, `sphereApp.test.tsx`, `inviteMember.test.tsx`, `ipc.test.ts`).

## Feature and status

- **Outcome:** A stranger joins their team's Obysphere from its invite link inside Oby, on first run or in Settings, seeing who they will join as before anything is made. Every member may hand every other a task, including members who joined before handoffs. An owner hands a member's Oby a task from that member's thread and reads what comes back there. An Oby whose admin took it out says so instead of vanishing.
- **Status:** **Implemented** in the Electron app (`apps/electron`). Changing the owner id waits on obid's add-only `PUT /v1/obid/sphere/owner` (SPHEREAPP-9): until obid serves it, the id shows read-only. A revoke's words follow obid's add-only `revokedReason` code once obid sends it.
- **Scope:** the app only. **Out of scope:** making an Obysphere and an admin removing a member (the Obysphere's own site does those); handing over a task by talking to Oby (`oby_handoff`, #2428's conversation tool); comparing keys.

## Behavior

### Actors and ownership

- **The owner** pastes a link and clicks Join, answers main's Join dialog, fills and sends a task, and leaves.
- **Main** holds the owner key, joins through its one join path (the `oby://` link's), signs the card and delegation, renews them, and sends a handoff through obid. It acts on the page's request only right after a click or key it saw itself (`takeInput`, as POLICY-4).
- **obid** keeps the membership, sends the handoff (`POST /v1/obid/sphere/requests`), and keeps every exchange.
- **The page** holds the link and the form's fields, and shows what main and obid answer. It decides nothing and signs nothing.

### Joining from a link

- First run's Obysphere step, where Oby can't make an Obysphere, has a field for the team's invite link under its words, with Join; Let's go goes on whether or not it joined. Settings has an Obysphere page with the same field.
- A text that isn't an invite link says so under the field, and Join stays off. A link shows its Obysphere's name, its host and the owner id obid would join as (`GET /v1/obid/sphere`'s `owner`) before any key is made.
- Join hands main the link. Main reads it as `oby://` links are read, opens the same native Join dialog (its detail now also says `You’ll join as <owner>.`), and only the owner's Join there joins, signing with the app's owner key. The page shows `You’re in <name>.`, or why it failed in main's words; the dialog's Cancel shows nothing.
- In an Obysphere, the Settings page shows its name and the owner id instead of the field.
- **Changing the owner id.** Two strangers whose Macs share a login name, with no `gh` signed in, would join as the same owner, so the id is the owner's to change before their first join. While obid's membership is `none`, the page asks obid once, with `PUT /v1/obid/sphere/owner` and an empty body (which changes nothing: `422`); where obid answers `404` the id shows read-only. Where it's served, the owner line has Change: a field and Save. An id that isn't lowercase letters, digits and single hyphens (1 to 63, the spec's slug) says so and sends nothing; one obid keeps shows on the line, and main's Join dialog says it. obid's `409` (a membership exists) says `It can’t change once you’ve joined an Obysphere.`

### The members rule and a membership grow

- The members rule (`members-ask`) lets every member ask a question or a review and hand over a task: `question`, `review_request` and `handoff`, in every repository.
- An admin's "Let members ask" adds the rule when no rule has its id; when one has, it adds only the actions that rule lacks, at its end, keeping everything else in it and every other rule as it was. Nothing is written when nothing is missing.
- At every keep (launch, after a join, every 5 minutes), a membership whose card accepts less than the app's (`review_request`, `question`, `handoff`) gets a new card accepting both what it had and what it lacks; one whose delegation's scope lacks one of the app's scopes gets a new 30-day delegation with both. Signed once: the next keep finds nothing missing and signs nothing. Owner clicks aren't asked for: each handoff still waits for its owner's Accept on Home (HANDOFF-2).

### A revoked membership

- When obid's membership is `revoked`, the Obysphere stays in the side panel with no members, its settings open with a line from obid's `revokedReason` code (an open set): `removed`, or no code, says `<name>’s admin took your Oby out.`; a code this app doesn't know says `Your Oby isn’t in <name> any more.`, and a code is never shown as it is. Then the field for a new link, and Leave. Its rules and What your Oby tells are not shown. Settings › Obysphere shows the same line over its field. The side panel's Obysphere says it too, under Members where `Just you so far` would be, and lists no member, so no thread opens and `@` in the message box offers none of them (SPHEREAPP-14). Joining again leaves the revoked membership first (SPHERE-1's join).

### Handing a member's Oby a task

- A member's thread, while they're in the live Obysphere, has `Hand <name>’s Oby a task` over its box. It opens a form: what to do (one line, at most 200 characters), the repository (`owner/name`, offered from obid's repositories; one picked starts from its own default branch) and the branch to start from (`main` unless changed), with `Done means its tests pass.`
- A field that can't go says why on Hand it over, and nothing is sent. Hand it over goes to main, which, right after that click, picks the member's Oby that takes `handoff` (a sealed one first) and sends one `handoff` through obid: the goal, repository and base as typed (trimmed), the one condition `tests_pass`, open for 7 days. The form closes and the thread reads again.
- Refusals stay on the form: a member whose Oby doesn't take tasks (`<owner>’s Oby doesn’t take tasks yet.`), someone not in the Obysphere, the Obysphere's rules (`This Obysphere’s rules don’t allow handing over tasks yet. An admin can change that.`), or obid's own words.
- What comes back reads in the thread under the task: `Handed over. It’s waiting on <name>.`, `<name> said yes. Their Oby is on it.`, `<name>’s Oby sent back its result.` (the result's summary is its own post), or why it was declined: `<name> said no to this task.`, `<name>’s Oby couldn’t start it.`, `<name>’s Oby doesn’t take tasks.`, the rules, busy, expired, or `declined without saying why`.

### Getting back in with a sign-in (a lost Mac)

- Beside the invite-link field (first run's Obysphere step and Settings › Obysphere), once the field holds an Obysphere's address (an invite link, used up or not, or `<host>/<sphere>`), and only when that Obysphere lists `owner_rebind` in `GET /v0.1/features`: the button **Sign in with <provider>** on its own, and under it `Already have an account? Sign in to restore your Obyspheres and your data.` The provider is the first of the Obysphere's `GET /v0.1/signin` (Google for one from before that list). An address whose Obysphere doesn't offer it reads as `This isn’t an Obysphere invite link.`, as any other text that isn't one. Main reads those two public answers with no click.
- **Sign in**, only right after a click main saw, opens main's own dialog: `Get back into <sphere> as <owner>?` (`You’ll sign in in your browser. Your Obys there under your old key stop working, and your teammates’ Obys ask them to compare your new key. Do this only if you lost the Mac you joined from.`), **Sign in** or **Cancel**. Its owner is obid's owner id, which the owner line's Change sets before (SPHEREAPP-9).
- Then main, all in main (obi-sphere `sphere-api.md` sections 3.1 and 3.2): makes a new owner key; listens on `127.0.0.1` on a port the system picks for the browser's way back (one GET of `/oby-signin`, 10 minutes at most, RFC 8252 section 7.3); starts the sign-in for the new key (`POST /v0.1/signin/{provider}` `{owner_key, return_to}`); opens the provider's https page in the person's browser; takes `state` and `code` back only for the state it started; has obid make its Oby key and card and signs the card with the new key; sends `POST /v0.1/spheres/{sphere}/owners/{owner}/rebind` `{owner_key, signin: {state, code}, card}` with no `Authorization`. On `200` it keeps the new key in place of the old (before anything else can fail) and hands obid the membership the rebind made: `POST /v1/obid/sphere/join` with `rebound: true` in place of `invitation`, the card and a delegation signed by the new key (obid's side is B1's, agreed and not merged yet; until it is, the line says the owner is back in but this Oby couldn't take its place).
- The page shows `You’re back in <sphere>.`, or why not, in words, never the Obysphere's own: `404` `This Obysphere has no owner by that id. Check its address and the owner id you’re getting back in as.`; `403` `That sign-in can’t get you back in. Use the account this name added, or ask an admin to reset it.`, and for an owner who never added one (the Sphere's `account_not_linked`, assumed) `This name never added a sign-in. Ask an admin to reset it.`; `409` `That account is linked to someone else in this Obysphere, or this Mac already holds your key there.`; `card_invalid` `The Obysphere didn’t take this Oby’s card. Update Oby, then try again.`; `429` `Too many sign-ins at once right now. Try again in a minute.`; a sign-in that failed or came back for another state, `The sign-in didn’t finish. Try again.`; 10 minutes with none, `The sign-in took too long. Try again.`. Each keeps the old key, and a sign-in that didn't come back sends no rebind.
- An Oby already in an Obysphere starts nothing: `This Oby is already in an Obysphere. Leave it first (in its settings), then open this link again.`

### Add sign-in (link first)

- Only an owner who added a sign-in, with that same account, gets back in by themselves after losing their Mac (the owner's decision: link first). So Settings › Obysphere, while in a live Obysphere that offers getting back in (`owner_rebind`), has a **Sign-in** group right under its name: **Add sign-in**, `Without it, you can’t get back in by yourself if you lose this Mac.`, with **Sign in with <provider>**; once linked, `Signed in as you`, `If you lose this Mac, sign in with <provider> to get back in.` No email or account shows, ever.
- Main reads whether it's linked with the owner's own `directory:read` token (`GET /v0.1/spheres/{sphere}/owners/{owner}`'s `account_linked`, **assumed** until the Sphere's shape is fixed), with no click. **Sign in with <provider>**, only right after a click main saw: a sign-in at the Sphere's provider for the owner key this app holds, in the person's browser, back to main's `127.0.0.1` listener (as for getting back in), then `PUT /v0.1/spheres/{sphere}/owners/{owner}/account` `{signin: {state, code}}` with the owner's own token (**assumed** too). `409`: `That account already belongs to someone else in this Obysphere. Sign in with your own.`; `403`: `That sign-in didn’t take. Use the account you want to get back in with, then try again.`; a sign-in that didn't finish, as for getting back in.
- The sole-admin notice adds `Add sign-in too, so you can get back in yourself.` while this owner hasn't.

### Inviting teammates (the admin)

- In Settings › Obysphere, while in one whose admin this owner is (the Sphere's `admins`, as the rules' read brings it), an **Invite teammates** group: New link, `For 5 people, 14 days`, with **Make a link**; the link it makes, whole and selectable, with **Copy** (`Copied` for a moment); then each open link as the Sphere lists it, `<left> of <max> uses left` and `Until <date>`, with **Withdraw**. Its footer: `Anyone with a link can join until it runs out.`, and `Members can ask, request reviews and hand off tasks.` once the members rule has all three.
- Main does it with the owner's own token (`admin`), which only the owner key mints and which never leaves main, as for the rules: `POST /v0.1/spheres/{sphere}/invitations` `{expires_in: 1209600, max_uses: 5}` (an open invitation, sphere-api.md section 4), `GET` the same to list (never with a secret), `DELETE …/invitations/{id}` to withdraw (one the Sphere no longer has counts as withdrawn). Making and withdrawing only right after a click or key main saw itself, one each; the list takes none.
- The link is `oby://join/<host>#sphere=<sphere>&by=<owner>&id=<invitation_id>&secret=<secret>`, handed out only when it reads back as the join field and an `oby://` link read it. The secret comes once and goes only into that link, on this page while it's open.
- On the same click, main adds what the members rule lacks (SPHEREAPP-1), so a new member can hand off tasks.
- A member who isn't admin sees no group, and main asks the Sphere for no list; one who isn't in an Obysphere makes nothing. The Sphere's refusal says `Only the Obysphere’s admins can invite.`; a click main didn't see, `Making a link takes your own click.`. Making an Obysphere stays hidden (`CAN_CREATE`).

### Invite member, from the side panel (the admin)

- The side panel's Obysphere has Sangam's **Invite member** under Knowledge. On the live Obysphere it shows only to its admins (the Sphere's word, as the rules' read brings it, as for Invite teammates); a member who isn't admin, or an Oby taken out, sees none. The mock's Obyspheres (`?everything`) and the tour's sample keep it as he made it, with his modal's own pretend link, its domain toggle and its Invited list.
- On the live Obysphere, the click on Invite member opens his modal (`Invite to <name>’s Obysphere`, Email, Invite link, Send invitation) and is the click main sees. On it, main hands back a link it made in this run that the Sphere still lists as open (matched by its invitation id), with the joins it has left; only when there's none does it make one, exactly as Make a link does (SPHEREAPP-10: `{expires_in: 1209600, max_uses: 5}`, the members rule's handoff added). Every list the Sphere answers drops from main's links those it no longer lists (withdrawn, out of date, used up), and leaving drops them all. Settings' Make a link always makes a new one. The link field shows it whole (`Making a link…` until then), and its copy icon copies it as it is; it is in Settings › Invite teammates' list, where it can be withdrawn.
- **Send invitation**: the Obysphere sends no mail and keeps no address (sphere-api.md section 4), so, only right after a click or Enter main saw itself, one each, main opens the person's own mail app on a new email (a `mailto:` URL, never another scheme) to the addresses typed, subject `Join <sphere>’s Obysphere`, its body carrying the link, how to use it and how many it lets join. Only for a link main itself made in this run that the Sphere, asked again then, still lists as open; and to no more addresses than the joins it has left (more, and under the field `This link lets <n> people in. Take some out to send it.`, Send invitation off, and main refuses the same words). Nothing is sent until the person sends it there.
- **The secret scan** reads the draft first, with main's own scan (gitleaks with its canary, as Copy Diagnostics): the recipients, the subject and the body with the link, and the `mailto:` URL as it is encoded. One value is left out of what it reads, replaced by a fixed stand-in: the secret of the invitation main made itself, taken from main's own record of that link, never from the page (gitleaks reads it as `generic-api-key`; the owner's decision of 2026-11-23, "Yes, skip that one"). Nothing is allow-listed in gitleaks' own settings. Any other finding says `The secret scan found what looks like a secret (<rules>), so no draft was opened.`, a scan that can't run `The secret scan couldn’t run (<why>), so no draft was opened.`, and neither opens anything. The button then reads `Draft opened` and the modal closes; nobody is added to Invited, since Oby can't know who was sent the link.
- "Only allow @<domain> emails to join" doesn't show on the live Obysphere: a link held to a domain needs a sign-in to redeem (sphere-api.md section 4), and this app's join refuses such a link (`Oby can’t sign you in with Google yet`), so one would let nobody in.
- Refusals show in the link field, in words, with Copy and Send invitation off: the Sphere's `Only the Obysphere’s admins can invite.`, `This Oby isn’t in an Obysphere.`, no click main saw (`Making a link takes your own click.`), and an Obysphere Oby couldn't reach (`Oby couldn’t reach your Obysphere.`). Send invitation's own: `Sending an invitation takes your own click.`, a link main didn't make (`That link isn’t one Oby made. Close this and invite again.`), and a mail app that wouldn't open (`Oby couldn’t open your mail app. Copy the link and send it yourself.`).

### Admins (Make admin, Remove admin, the sole-admin notice)

- In a live Obysphere's settings, the Members list says who is `Admin` and who is `Member`, as the Sphere's `admins` say (the rules' read brings them, with this Oby's owner id). To its admins, each other member's row has **Make admin**, or **Remove admin** for one who is. Nobody's row is shown to a member who isn't admin with either.
- Main changes it with the owner's own `admin` token, only right after a click or key main saw itself: `PUT` or `DELETE /v0.1/spheres/{sphere}/admins/{owner}` (sphere-api.md section 2). The Sphere's answer lists its admins, and the list shows them. The Sphere keeps at least one admin (`409`: `An Obysphere keeps at least one admin.`) and makes admin only an owner who has joined (`409`: `<owner> hasn’t joined with their own key yet.`); a member who isn't admin: `Only the Obysphere’s admins can change who’s admin.`; no click main saw: `Changing who’s admin takes your own click.`
- **The sole-admin notice.** While this owner is its only admin, the Members list and Settings › Obysphere (over Invite teammates) say `You’re the only admin of <name>. Make someone else an admin so it never hangs on one Mac.`, and `Add sign-in too, so you can get back in yourself.` while they haven't (SPHEREAPP-13). A notice, never a block: nothing waits on it.

### Hidden for launch

- The room: the app never looks for one, so its tile click, Settings' "Also post in the room" and its posts never show (`ROOM_SHOWS` in `spheres.ts`).
- Other Obys in the message box are no longer hidden: on obid, "Add other Obys" and `@` list the live members that take questions, and a mention asks them (ask-an-oby.md, "Asking by @ in the message box", ASK-37 to 41). Out of a Sphere they list no one and + has no "Add other Obys".

### Failures and recovery

- obid not answering when the page reads the owner id: no owner line shows; main's dialog still says it when it can.
- A join or a handoff that fails changes nothing the page holds but the problem line; the owner can try again.
- A page script that took the window over can't join or hand over by itself: main refuses without a click or key it saw (`Joining takes your own click.`, `Handing a task over takes your own click.`), and a join still needs the owner's Join in the native dialog.

### Invariants and boundaries

- The owner key never leaves main; the page never holds anything to sign (Approvals, AGENTS.md).
- Handoffs are typed: the page sends four strings, main builds the body from them; another Oby's words come back only as quoted posts.
- obid's API is unchanged by this app: it reads `revokedReason` add-only and sends only what obid already takes.

## Interfaces and dependencies

- IPC: `sphere-join` (a string: the link), `sphere-handoff` (`{to, goal, repo, base}`), `sphere-invitations`, `sphere-invite`, `sphere-withdraw` (a string: the invitation's id), `sphere-invite-mail` (the addresses and the link), `sphere-admin` (an owner id and a boolean), `sphere-rebind-offer` (a string: an address), `sphere-rebind` (an address and a provider id), `sphere-signin-status` and `sphere-add-signin` (a provider id), in [ipc.md](ipc.md).
- The Obysphere: `GET`, `POST` and `DELETE /v0.1/spheres/{sphere}/invitations` (section 4), and `PUT` and `DELETE /v0.1/spheres/{sphere}/admins/{owner}` (section 2), with the owner's own `admin` token; `GET /v0.1/features`, `GET` and `POST /v0.1/signin/{provider}` (section 3.1) and `POST /v0.1/spheres/{sphere}/owners/{owner}/rebind` (section 3.2, obi-sphere#102), with no token.
- obid: `GET /v1/obid/sphere` (with `revokedReason`, B1's), `GET /v1/obid/sphere/members`, `POST /v1/obid/sphere/requests`, `PUT /v1/obid/sphere/membership`, `GET /v1/repos`.
- obid's `PUT /v1/obid/sphere/owner` `{owner}` (add-only, B1's): `2xx` with the owner kept, `409` once any membership exists, `422` for a body without a valid owner; `404` from an obid without it.
