# A task at work, live

The business behavior contract for a dispatched agent's work in the Electron app: its card in the conversation, which is its progress only, what it did shown under that card as part of the conversation, and its row nested under that conversation in the side panel, from [the template](../business-contract-template.md). A task has no view of its own: every way to it opens the conversation that started it, at its card.

## Feature and status

- **Outcome:** The owner sees what a dispatched agent is doing the way Claude Code shows it, and can follow it and talk to it, all in the conversation that started it, as simple as T3 Code, whose thread is the session's work itself. In their words: "I just saw a chat that dispatches, but what happens with the agent that were dispatched? Where are the thinking, the information on it?", and on 2026-11-17, confirming the plan in their words "Yes exactly": the old full-screen task view goes; every route leads to the conversation that started the task, scrolled to its card; the card is only progress (its head and its stages bar); the details (what the agent did, what changed, the test run, the result, the decision) live in the conversation ([why](../decisions/2026-11-17-tasks-live-in-the-conversation.md)).
- **Status:** **Implemented** in the Electron app. obid already recorded and streamed all of this (`GET /v1/jobs/{id}/transcript`, the `transcript` event, `docs/runner-api.md`). One obid change, with nothing new in its API: a job's Claude Code now starts with `CLAUDE_CODE_ENABLE_TODO_TOOLS=1`, because headless 2.1.302 otherwise has no to-do tool at all, so no Claude Code job could ever make a list. The design is Wellington's canvas "Oby: the Sphere is the chat", boards "Oby at work" and "The agent's own conversation" (https://claude.ai/artifact/StYU4XCqZAnQ3t9z9yBDsd), and screens 03 and 04 of `docs/design/oby-home/`. The nested rows, the card's head and the task's own thread follow page 2 of the canvas "Oby Factory On and Off" (https://claude.ai/artifact/WVGbVtqZBEsVWH6p3Nxvxm), screens 03 and 04; where that page draws the card opened in place or a session of its own, the owner's word of 2026-11-17 holds: the card stays progress only and what the task did shows under it in the conversation, never inside it and never on a page of its own. Where screen 04 draws the decision under the card's head, the owner's word of 2026-11-17 holds: the card stays its head alone and the decision waits in a drawer over the message box, as T3 Code shows its approvals and questions ([why](../decisions/2026-11-17-decisions-like-t3-code.md)). **Proposed, Sangam's to change:** the drawer's words "Details", "1 of 3", "Next decision" and the answered line ("Permission · Allow once"). **Proposed, Sangam's to change:** the card's ⋯ and its menu's "Tell Claude Code…" (ended: "Ask for a follow-up…"), the chip over the message box ("To Claude Code · <task>", its × back to Oby), "Show N more", "Show more", "Show less", the moment the card is lit when opened at it, "Show N earlier", "Thinking", "Thought", "Declined" and the note when a call's details weren't kept.
- **Streaming (LIVETASK-32 to 36): Implemented** in obid and the app, adding two keys to the `transcript` event (`tail`, `output`), nothing changed. The agent's words show about every tenth of a second instead of a whole line at a time, and a running command's last lines show while it runs instead of only once it ends, as T3 Code shows them (gap analysis of 2026-11-17, item 8). A conversation's own words already stream a word at a time (the brain's turn, `docs/api/conversations.md`); its agent's commands show no output, so nothing changes there.
- **Scope:** The task's card in a conversation (`ThreadTask`, `TaskEntry`, `TaskHead` in `ChatView.tsx`), what it did under the card (`TaskLog.tsx`), opening a conversation at a task (`Home.tsx`, `openAt`; `ChatView.tsx`, `flash`), the drawer of decisions over a message box (`DecisionBar.tsx`, `AskBlock`'s `bar` in `TaskCard.tsx`), the card's menu and the box's chip (`TaskMenu.tsx`, `ChatView.tsx`), its row nested under the conversation in the side panel (`Sidebar.tsx`, `nested.ts`), where typed words go (`replyTarget.ts`, modelled in `docs/models/reply_target.qnt`), and reading transcript events (`obid-wire.ts`, `live.ts`). **Out of scope:** a helper agent's own conversation (it shows as one line); Home's stage; Sangam's earlier Home (`?home`), whose task page (`TaskView.tsx`) stays its own.

## Behavior

### Actors and ownership

- **obid** owns the transcript: what's recorded, what's masked, each entry's number and state. It decides nothing new here.
- **The app** shows what obid sent and invents nothing. It sends the owner's words and Stop as it does everywhere else.

### What each tool gives

| | To-dos | Thinking |
|---|---|---|
| Claude Code | Only if it made a list (`TodoWrite`, `TaskCreate`) | "Thought for 8s", no words: nothing to open |
| Codex | Only if it made a plan (`turn/plan/updated`) | A summary at most, maybe none |
| Cursor | Only if it made a list (`plan`, `cursor/update_todos`) | Its words, after the thought ends |

### States

| The task | Its card in the conversation | What it did, under the card | Its row under the conversation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Queued or running | "Running · tool · repository"; its stages as a bar | Its newest to-do list, if it made one, the one it's on lit; its last 4 lines as they happen (a tool's verb and target, its thinking, its words), earlier ones behind "Show N more"; obid's own line last when its session doesn't hold it (a stage past the agent's work, a wait, "Trying again…") | Its agent, **its title** · what it's on, a working dot |
| Needs the owner | Its head alone: "Needs you · Claude Code · oby-scratch", the stage it's on orange; the decision waits in the drawer over the conversation's message box, "from" its title | As above, never the decision | **Its title** · what it asks ("asks to run a command"), an orange dot; the conversation's line says it too |
| Ended | One line: "Done", "Nothing done", "Stopped" or "Failed", and its commits, branch, pull request and time | Its last words whole (what changed, the test run, the commit, the result), folded past a few lines behind "Show more"; its lines before them behind "Show N more"; a decision answered, one line | **Its title** · Done (a check) or Failed (a red dot) |

A card never opens anything, in place or elsewhere: every way to a task (an alert's click, Waiting for you's Open, its nested row, Home's stack) opens the conversation that started it, scrolled to its card and lighting it a moment; a task in no conversation (from GitHub, another Oby) opens its own thread, the same card with what it did under it. A decision never shows under a card: it waits in the drawer over the message box until it's answered, Later taking it off that drawer only until the conversation is opened at a task again (LIVETASK-24 to 27). The message box at the foot of a conversation with Oby talks to Oby, or to one task's agent while that task's chip is on it (its card's menu, "Tell Claude Code…"), and to Oby again once those words are sent or the chip's × is pressed; in a task's own thread it talks to that task's agent.

### Failures and recovery

- An event that didn't reach the app, or a lost connection: the session is fetched again at each of the job's events, from the last entry known with none missing before it, so a hole fills by itself.
- A fetch answered late never undoes an event: an ended call stays ended and words stay as far as they got.
- A `tail` or `output` event that didn't reach the app: the next one, a tenth of a second later, replaces it whole; a fetch keeps what streamed until the entry comes again or the call ends.
- Telling the agent is refused: obid's own words show, as for any tell (`say`).
- The job is deleted while its conversation is open: its card, what's under it, its row and its chip on the box go with it.

### Invariants and boundaries

- Only a job something shows is kept in memory, let go when the last thing showing it goes, and an agent's every line redraws only the lines that changed. A long session shows its last 300 lines, with earlier ones on asking.
- Everything shown is the transcript's own, masked by obid. A call's output and a file's contents come only when its line is opened (LIVETASK-11), but a command's last 40 lines stream while it runs (LIVETASK-34). Nothing streams that obid couldn't mask whole: a word or a line not ended waits (LIVETASK-33, 35).
- `Job`, the phones' API and its enums are untouched.

## Interfaces and dependencies

Reads `GET /v1/jobs/{id}/transcript`, `GET /v1/jobs/{id}/transcript/{n}` when a line opens, a job's `reason`, and the `transcript` event; sends `POST /v1/jobs/{id}/messages` and `POST /v1/jobs/{id}/stop`. No new route; the `transcript` event gains `tail` and `output` (LIVETASK-32, 34; `docs/api/jobs.md`), add-only: an older app drops an event it can't read.

## Test it when it's done (real Mac)

Dispatch a short task on Claude Code and on Codex from a conversation. The card shows how it stands and its stages; under it, its to-dos with the current one lit and its last lines as they happen, earlier ones behind Show N more. Open a tool line to see its output. Leave the conversation, then click the task's nested row: the conversation opens at its card, lit. A task that needs you stays its card's head ("Needs you"), and its decision waits over the message box on one line; Later takes it off. With the window behind another app, click the task's alert, and open Waiting for you and its row's Open: each lands in the conversation at the card, the decision back over the box. From the card's ⋯, Tell Claude Code…: the chip shows over the box, the words reach the task, and the box is Oby's again. When it ends, the card folds to one line with its branch or pull request, and its last words (what changed, the test run, the commit) show under it. Ask it to run `for i in $(seq 1 10); do echo line $i; sleep 1; done`: its words come in a few at a time, and its command's line, opened while it runs, gains a line a second.
