Contract: docs/contracts/teammate-review.md, TEAMREVIEW-7 (proposed there, with its words; Sangam's to change).

What. Oby asks one teammate's Oby at a time for the approval a pull request needs. A teammate who sends nothing back (no `accept`, `decline`, `status` or `result`) holds the pull request for the request's whole 2 hours (`approval::REQUEST_LIFETIME`) before Oby asks anyone else, and the task shows nothing about the wait.

Why. A teammate's Mac asleep or offline stops the owner's merge for two hours without a word. The contract already decided the fix.

How. As TEAMREVIEW-7 says: after 15 silent minutes Oby asks the next teammate, and the silent request keeps its 2 hours (either approval merges it: the contract's assumption; the owner's question on calling it off with `cancel` stays open). All the while the task's line shows the wait, in the contract's words ("Waiting for <teammate>'s Oby to review it and approve it on GitHub (asked 6 minutes ago)." then "<teammate>'s Oby didn't answer in 15 minutes…"). When every teammate has been asked and none answered, the task's line says so. The 15 minutes is a constant beside `REQUEST_LIFETIME`. Move TEAMREVIEW-7 to Implemented.
