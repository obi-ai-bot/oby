## What
Two settings per project. `factory: false`: conversations don't turn words into tasks; they answer and work where the conversation works (B00-11). `askBeforeMerge: true`: even the owner's own work gets a Ready-to-merge card instead of merging by itself (MERGING-45).

## Why
- **Some people want conversations only** (the plan, §4: "conversations only, no factory").
- **Some want to approve every merge**: a manager or CTO who mostly reviews. Today own work merges with no card, and nothing turns that off.

## How
1. Both live in the project's settings beside `factory.json`, the owner's word overriding a default (AGENTS.md, "Defaults").
2. The brain's planner reads `factory`: off, it starts no job unless the owner asks for one by name.
3. Merging reads `askBeforeMerge`: on, own work that passed its reviews gets the merge card (MERGING-21's words) and waits for the owner's signed Merge.
4. Defaults keep today's behaviour: factory on, own work merges by itself.
