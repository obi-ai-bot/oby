### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

The owner decides whether Android leaves the launch scope. On yes, nobody ports Electron or iPhone changes to Android by hand until generated wire models exist, and the code stays in place.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R03-08, milestone R03 Delete what has no caller).

**Why:** Android stopped at the 2026-11-02 parity pass (last feature commit ee6fbe61) and missed the iPhone's chat-first change (#1887): it still shows Home with Running and Recent (apps/android/.../Home.kt:124-129), has no talkAccount, and is an internal-testing build (versionCode 3 in apps/android/app/build.gradle.kts). Parity fixes came in pairs (#1718/#1739, #1762/#1771, #1772/#1774, #1773/#1776, #1791/#1793). AGENTS.md:121 already says apps/android comes later, but the parity work went on.

**In:**
- Step 1: ask the owner on an issue: freeze Android out of launch and parity work? Record the answer in docs/trim.md, with what ends the freeze (generated wire models, R07).
- On yes: apps/android/README.md opens with 'Frozen' and the date; AGENTS.md:121 says Android is frozen and out of parity work; Android issues move to Later on the board with a link to the decision.

**Out:**
- Deleting apps/android: not asked; the code stays.
- obid's FCM path (push.rs, POST /v1/notifications with platform fcm): it stays, add-only.
- The iPhone and Watch rule at AGENTS.md:39: unchanged.
