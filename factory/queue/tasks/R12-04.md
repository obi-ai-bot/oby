# feat(obid): a routine run ends quiet on `NOTHING TO REPORT`

<!-- Roadmap R12-04 · Sprint 9 · R12 Developer workflows · effort S · risk low -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

A check-type routine that finds nothing does not ping the owner. Owners stop pausing useful routines because of noise.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R12-04, milestone R12 Developer workflows).

**Why:** Every routine run ends in a turn and, when it needs nobody, still alerts. obid already reads fixed last lines (`GOAL MET`, `GOAL NOT MET`, jobs.rs 14766-14779, TASKS-19).

**In:**
- For a scheduled routine job only: a last summary line exactly `NOTHING TO REPORT`, with no commits, no outgoing actions and no open ask, ends `done` with `quiet: true` on the job and on the routine's turn.
- obid sends no alert or push for a quiet job.

**Out:**
- Run now and payload runs: never quiet.
- Any model call to decide quiet.

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- The quiet rule and the `quiet` field.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- Clients may fold a quiet turn; until Sangam designs it they show it as any routine turn.

### Interfaces and dependencies

- Job and routine turn: `quiet: true` (add-only, absent when false).

### Steps

- Add the line parser next to the GOAL MET parser (one rule, one place).
- Apply it only when the job's `routine` mark says scheduled.
- Skip the alert and push for `quiet`.
- Ask routine prompts to end with `NOTHING TO REPORT` when they found nothing.
- Test ROUTINES-7 and 8.

### Acceptance scenarios

- ROUTINES-7 (proposed): Given a scheduled routine job whose last line is `NOTHING TO REPORT` with no commits, no outgoing and no ask, when it ends, then it is `done`, `quiet: true`, and no alert or push goes out.
- ROUTINES-8 (proposed): Given the same last line on a job with a commit, an open ask, a failure or a Run now start, when it ends, then it is not quiet and alerts as before.
