The simple factory, in three tasks: this switch (B00-50), `/factory` making a project's factory the first time it's used (B00-51), and the app's side of both (B00-52). The owner chose this flow on 2026-11-16 over a three-step setup sheet. Sangam owns what the user sees: the canvas is a proposal, and his words lead.

## What
Think of the factory as a room in the house that's locked until you open it. Nothing in obid says today whether a person wants the factory at all, so there's nothing for the app to show as off, and nothing for `/factory` (B00-05) to check before it starts work. This task adds one setting, `factory`, true or false and false unless the owner set it. It's the switch on Settings › Factory, and it's the only setting the factory needs before it's used.

## Why
- **The owner, 2026-11-16:** "I think it should just be a toggle to turn it on or off and then we can just use "/" to send stuff to the factory and then it will create a factory for that project … Simplify it as much as possible!"
- **The same day, the owner's direction:** Oby installs as a blank canvas. The factory ships with Oby, but it's off until a person turns it on, and someone who never does uses Oby as a plain dispatcher.
- **Lane: dispatcher.** Whether the factory takes work is the dispatcher's state. Nothing here reads, edits or runs anything for an agent, and obid makes no model call.

## How
1. **The setting.** `Settings.factory: Option<bool>` in `core/crates/obid/src/settings.rs`, read through `Settings::factory()` (none is false), as `github_every_repo` is (`settings.rs:144`, `settings.rs:260`). It's kept in `settings.json` across restarts.
2. **The route.** `GET /v1/settings/jobs` answers `factory`. `PUT /v1/settings/jobs` takes it alongside or instead of the other keys (`routes.rs:1039`, `routes.rs:1198`). Anything but `true` or `false` is `422` and keeps nothing of the body, as `githubEveryRepo` is. This adds a key and removes nothing (add-only API).
3. **Status.** `GET /v1/status` answers `factory` (a bool), and `capabilities` gains `factory-switch-v1`, still sorted (`api.rs:651`).
4. **What it reads, and what it doesn't.** Only the factory's own routes and its `/factory` command read the switch (B00-51). `POST /v1/jobs`, conversations and their dispatch, routines, follow-ups and the jobs `factory/bin/oby-feed` starts never read it: Oby as a dispatcher works the same with the factory off. Turning it off stops nothing that's running.
5. **Docs.** `docs/api/settings.md` describes `factory` beside `githubEveryRepo`. `docs/contracts/factory-switch.md` (new) holds the contract: what the switch is, the owner's words, FACTORYON-1 to 5, and the line that only the factory's own routes read it.
