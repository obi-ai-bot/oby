What the owner sees. One icon in a conversation's title bar shows or hides its project's factory beside the conversation: a line per task of that project, each with the tool's mark, its title, what stage it is at and what it is doing, and Sangam's stage bar under it. A second icon opens the factory in full: a row per task and a station per stage (Prepare, Work, Check, Publish, Review, Merge), the stage bar running under the stations, the lit station saying which tool is working. Opening a row shows the agent of that station, with the small box to tell it something. The same icon brings the conversation back.

How. Read the project's jobs (the task before this one) and each job's pipeline. Hold nothing but whether the panel is open.
