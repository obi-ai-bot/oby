Step 1 of the Agents plan (docs/decisions/2026-11-16-agents-follow-openbot.md).

## What
Oby's memory is about the owner or a repository, saved only when the owner says Remember, and shared by every conversation (`docs/api/memory.md`). An agent can't keep what it learned for itself. In OpenBot an agent writes its own memories, and the person sees and controls them.

## Why
- **The owner, 2026-11-16:** "Let's follow what OpenBot does!", answering who writes an agent's memory.
- **Lane: dispatcher**, by that decision.

## How
1. **The store.** Per agent, in obid's home (`home/helpers/<id>/memories.json`, 0600): each memory has text (at most 500 characters), `essential` true or false, `pinned`, who wrote it (`agent` or `owner`) and when.
2. **The agent's tools.** `oby_remember(text, essential)`, `oby_forget(id)`, `oby_search_memories(query)`, for its own memories only. A write or forget is staged and kept only when the turn completes; a stopped or failed turn keeps none. The agent can't change or forget a pinned memory.
3. **Each turn.** Its essential memories, newest first, up to 8 KiB, go into its room as a quoted file (`agent-memories.md`), marked as data, never instructions. The rest are reached by search.
4. **Secrets.** A memory holding a vault secret, or one the secret scan finds, isn't kept, and the turn is told why.
5. **The owner.** `GET`, `POST`, `PATCH`, `DELETE /v1/helpers/{id}/memories`: view, add, edit, pin, delete, clear. The app shows them in the agent's sheet.
6. **Oby's own memory** (owner and repository, Remember) stays as it is.
