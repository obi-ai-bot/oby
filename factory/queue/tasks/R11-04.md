# refactor(obid): one OAuth client module, Tetrix its first caller

<!-- Roadmap R11-04 · Sprint 8 · R11 Connections: MCP, sign-in and the vault · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

Discovery (RFC 9728 and 8414), optional dynamic registration, PKCE, the loopback listener, token exchange, refresh, sign-out and revocation live in one oauth.rs with tokens as vault entries per address. Tetrix keeps its routes and words; the registry (R11-09) can sign in to any server with it.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R11-04, milestone R11 Connections: MCP, sign-in and the vault).

**Why:** tetrix.rs (2,667 lines) holds its own OAuth client: start:503, challenged:780, register:806, token_request:859, keep:904, finish:922, access:984, forget:1054, revoke:344, callback:1646. chatgpt.rs has a second copy (begin:469, token_request:571, refresh:864, revoke:1041, callback:1460). Their refresh rules already differ (ChatGPT REFRESH_BEFORE 5 min; Tetrix 'less than a minute', #1133).

**In:**
- new: core/crates/obid/src/oauth.rs: `Client` with discovery, `resource_fits`, registration kept per authorization server, PKCE, one loopback listener (a preferred port, then any free one), the refresh-outcome table (only invalid_grant, invalid_client, unauthorized_client drop tokens; network errors keep them), sign-out deleting locally first then RFC 7009 revoke with a short limit, a body and time limit on every request, and private addresses refused for a public server.
- Each caller passes its scopes, its lead time before expiry and its extra check (Tetrix: `resource` match and `?org=`).
- Tetrix moves onto it.

**Out:**
- ChatGPT (R11-05).
- New sign-in routes.
- Changing Tetrix's lead time or words: SIGN-IN-8 to 14 and tetrix_api.rs stay green.

### Acceptance scenarios

- OAUTH-1 (proposed): Given a signed-in address, when the token server refuses a refresh with invalid_grant, invalid_client or unauthorized_client, then obid deletes that address's tokens and says sign in again; on a network error or 5xx, then it keeps them and tries again later.
- OAUTH-2 (proposed): Given a sign-out, when the revocation endpoint doesn't answer within its limit, then the tokens are already gone from the vault and the sign-out answers done.
- OAUTH-3 (proposed): Given a public server's metadata that names a token or authorization endpoint on a private or loopback address, when obid signs in, then it refuses with a reason and sends nothing there.
- OAUTH-4 (proposed): Given the preferred loopback port in use, when a sign-in starts, then the listener takes a free port and the redirect URI names it.
- OAUTH-5 (proposed): Given an answer from any OAuth endpoint larger than its limit or slower than its deadline, when obid reads it, then the sign-in or refresh fails with a reason and no token is kept.
