# feat(obid): take back one waiting message

<!-- Roadmap R05-08 · Sprint 4 · R05 Durable words · effort S · risk low -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

The owner can take back one message that still waits, without cancelling the turn under way.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R05-08, milestone R05 Durable words).

**Why:** Today only `POST /v1/plans/cancel` exists, and it drops the turn under way and every waiting message together (brain/mod.rs `cancel`, turn.rs `Turns::cancel`). #929 asks for take-back.

**In:**
- Add-only `turn` on POST /v1/plans/cancel's body: only that delivery, only while `queued`, becomes `cancelled`; the turn under way goes on.
- A delivery not `queued` answers 409 with words why, and nothing changes.

**Out:**
- The app's take-back control (R05-09).

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Which deliveries can be taken back.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- Nothing changes for clients until R05-09.

### Interfaces and dependencies

- Add-only: optional `turn` in POST /v1/plans/cancel's body (`{conversation, turn}`); 409 for a delivery not `queued`.

### Steps

- Extend `underway`'s body type with `turn` (keep `deny_unknown_fields` and the old bodies working).
- Apply `cancel` to that delivery only; answer 409 on a refused transition.
- Write DELIVERY-19 and 20; document it.

### Acceptance scenarios

- DELIVERY-19 (proposed): Given a turn under way and a message waiting, when POST /v1/plans/cancel names that message's `turn`, then it reads `cancelled`, the turn under way goes on, and the next turn does not hear it.
- DELIVERY-20 (proposed): Given a message already `running` or `answered`, when it is named in POST /v1/plans/cancel, then obid answers 409 and nothing changes.
