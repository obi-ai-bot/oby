# refactor(obid): Tetrix becomes one registry row

<!-- Roadmap R11-08 · Sprint 10 · R11 Connections: MCP, sign-in and the vault · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

Tetrix is served to agents as a built-in registry row (auth oauth, tools = TOOLS) through the same broker, scan and use log. Its special forwarding path goes; its Settings routes and words stay.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R11-08, milestone R11 Connections: MCP, sign-in and the vault).

**Why:** tetrix.rs keeps its own agent server (agent:2185, handle:1178, tools:1102, call_tool:1142, upstream:1086) beside the broker, with its own route /v1/agent/tetrix/{job} and its own Claude, Codex and Cursor wiring in jobs.rs (tetrix_mcp:1473, :10527-10537) and codex.rs.

**In:**
- Tetrix's agent tools come from a row named `tetrix` whose token is the OAuth access token (R11-04), whose allowlist is TOOLS, and whose extras (save_session stamps, gaps, digests, Use counts) stay in tetrix.rs as hooks on the row.
- Keep /v1/agent/tetrix/{job} as a thin alias to the broker for one release (warm sessions hold its URL).
- Keep GET/DELETE /v1/tetrix, POST/DELETE /v1/tetrix/sign-in unchanged.

**Out:**
- Tetrix's context queries and gaps (job_ended, context, digests): unchanged.
- The conversation's TETRIX server name in prompts: unchanged.

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Tetrix as one row; its login; its allowlist.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- Nothing changes for clients.

### Steps

- Add the built-in row; route Tetrix's tools/list and tools/call through the broker entries.
- Point jobs.rs, codex.rs and acp.rs at the one broker; keep the `tetrix` server name for prompts with the alias.
- Delete Tetrix::handle, tools, call_tool and upstream once tetrix_api.rs passes.
- Add CONNECT-19 and 20; run tetrix_api.rs, conversation_harness.rs, adapters.rs.

### Acceptance scenarios

- CONNECT-19 (proposed): Given Oby signed in to Tetrix, when an owner's job lists its tools, then Tetrix's TOOLS are listed and resume_session, admin, write and diagnostics tools are not, as today.
- CONNECT-20 (proposed): Given Oby signed out of Tetrix, when a running job calls a Tetrix tool, then it answers Oby isn't signed in to Tetrix and sends nothing.
