# fix(obid): the owner's words to a job stay queued until its agent turn runs

<!-- Roadmap R05-06 · Sprint 4 · R05 Durable words · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

Words told to a job are never lost by a launch or prepare failure: they stay `queued` in the job file and the next run hears them. TASKCONV-15 reads one status in place of four fields.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R05-06, milestone R05 Durable words).

**Why:** `take_heard`/`take_words` (jobs.rs:4549-4562) empty `job.heard` and `job.owners` before steps that can fail (claude login, memory read, MCP config write, safety stall, launch); after that the words live only in `notes` (#1649, part 2). `last_turn` (jobs.rs:4565) takes them the same way.

**In:**
- The job's owner's words become a list of deliveries in the job file using `delivery.rs` (queued, starting, running, answered, cancelled).
- A run marks its words `starting` before prepare, `running` once the agent is launched (or the steer acknowledged), and `answered` when the turn that carried them ends.
- A prepare or launch failure, or a parked or restarted run, puts `starting` words back to `queued`.
- Job files with `heard`/`owners` load as queued deliveries (no data loss); the old fields are read, not written.
- TASKCONV-14/15 read the delivery status.

**Out:**
- The Stop/Try again/worker race of #1649 part 1 (R04).
- Retrying prepare and launch failures (#1943 `retry-prepare`).
- Steer for jobs (R05-07).

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Where a job's words are and when they move.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- Nothing changes for clients; the job JSON may carry an add-only `deliveries` list.

### Interfaces and dependencies

- Add-only: `deliveries` on a job in GET /v1/jobs/{id} and `job` events: `[{turn?, status, why?}]`, words left out (they stay in `notes` as today).

### Steps

- List every reader and writer of `job.heard`, `job.owners`, `notes` and `left_words` and say which delivery status each means.
- Add the deliveries list to the job (serde default empty) and the upgrade from `heard`/`owners` in `job.upgrade()`.
- Replace `take_heard`, `take_words` and the taking in `last_turn` with `start` before prepare and `confirm` after launch; `restore` on every failure path before launch.
- Move `left_words` and TASKCONV-15's follow-up rule to read the status.
- Write TASKCONV-21 to 23 and run the existing taskconv_14 and taskconv_15 tests.

### Acceptance scenarios

- TASKCONV-21 (proposed): Given the owner's words told to a running job, when its next run fails before its agent launches (a login copy not ready), then the words stay `queued` in the job file and the run after hears them.
- TASKCONV-22 (proposed): Given words `queued` on a job, when obid is killed and starts again, then the words are still `queued` and the job's next run hears them once.
- TASKCONV-23 (proposed): Given a job file written before this change with `heard` and `owners`, when obid loads it, then those words are queued deliveries, the owner's own still marked so.
