## What
- In 0.2.26 the window's Over SSH is a stub: apps/electron/src/renderer/src/computers/obid.ts `addOverSsh` throws NOT_YET ("Adding a computer over SSH isn't in Oby yet"). obid already has `obid pair-computer` (the Mac's name and device key on stdin) and docs/contracts/remote.md describes the flow (COMPUTERS-3 to 6, 12).
## Why
The owner's SSH to the other Mac already works (an alias); pairing should be one click from it, with no link to carry.
## How
- Main (not the page) runs the person's own `/usr/bin/ssh <alias>` to the other computer's `obid pair-computer`, the Mac's name and device key on stdin, never on a command line; redeems the invitation through the ssh tunnel (`ssh -N -L`, COMPUTERS-12); signs the owner's words with its device key.
- Every ssh error in the words COMPUTERS-5 gives; a host key is the person's call (never accept one, never turn strict checking off); nothing written to ~/.ssh.
- The sheet's steps tick off as Sangam's screen shows; a refusal says why.
Acceptance: a fake ssh in tests drives each step and each error; real Mac: Add a computer › Over SSH › an alias pairs a second Mac and switches to it.
