# refactor(obid): one limit hold table keyed by account and scope

<!-- Roadmap R08-07 · Sprint 3 · R08 One interface for every agent CLI · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

One `limits::Holds` map says which account (or which model family on it) is held until when. Routing, a job's wait, a move and the conversation's account choice all ask it, so the limit rules live in one place and a wait survives a restart.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R08-07, milestone R08 One interface for every agent CLI).

**Why:** Limits are spread over `Recovery.scoped_holds` (job.rs:91), `Job.resumes_at` (job.rs:635), `Jobs::mark_full` (jobs.rs:3104), jobs/account_limits.rs `limit_reached` / `by_room`, and two `UNKNOWN_RESET` constants (usage.rs:26 and jobs/account_limits.rs:26). account-limits.md went through three designs in 9 days (LIMITS-1 to 4, 5 to 14, 15 to 20); jobs/account_limits.rs has 8 fixes in 19 commits.

**In:**
- new: core/crates/obid/src/limits/holds.rs: `Holds` keyed by (account, `Scope::Account | Scope::Family(String)`), `apply(refusal)`, `release_at(reset + 90 s)`, `held(account, family, now)`.
- Persist holds in home/usage.json beside the readings (LIMITS-18 needs a wait to survive restarts).
- `mark_full`, `limit_reached`, `by_room`, the wait and the conversation account choice ask `Holds`; one `UNKNOWN_RESET`.
- Load-time conversion: a job file with `scopedHolds` or `resumesAt` becomes holds, then the fields stop being written.

**Out:**
- Deleting the decision store and its API answer: R08-08.
- New reset sources: R08-10.
- Any change to who moves where (LIMITS-15, 19, 20).

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Every limit hold, its reset and its release.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

### Acceptance scenarios

- LIMITS-21 (proposed): Given an account a model's limit refused (USAGE-13), when a job of another model family routes, then the account takes it, and a job of that family passes it by until the reset plus 90 seconds.
- LIMITS-22 (proposed): Given a job waiting for room with a hold until 14:00, when obid restarts at 13:00, then the job still waits and resumes at 14:01:30 at the latest, with no card.
- LIMITS-23 (proposed): Given a job file written by dev 952bc03d with `scopedHolds` and `resumesAt`, when obid loads it, then the same holds exist and the job waits until the same time.
- LIMITS-24 (proposed): Given a conversation turn refused for its account's limit, when the next turn picks an account, then it reads the same hold a job would and does not pick that account until the hold ends (CONVERSATION-AGENT-3 kept).
