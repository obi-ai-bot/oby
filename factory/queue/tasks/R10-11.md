# refactor(obid): one inbox owner, so the Sphere link holds only Obi-to-Obi traffic

<!-- Roadmap R10-11 · Sprint 8 · R10 Finish the Obysphere client · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

Merge, review, limit, crash and GitHub-work cards no longer go through the Sphere client. An obid with no Sphere and a change to the Sphere client cannot break the owner's inbox.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R10-11, milestone R10 Finish the Obysphere client).

**Why:** sphere/link.rs is 4,410 lines; `Link` (link.rs:507) holds the inbox, decisions, sharing, pins, triage, the room and the relay handle. 18 calls outside sphere/ use it for inbox cards: jobs.rs (3968, 7027, 11694, 11899, 12770, 12817, 13132), review.rs (1438-1468), github_work.rs:1139, brain/actions.rs:904. 24 of 92 commits to link.rs are fixes.

**In:**
- Move `inbox`, `triage`, `saving` and add_items, limit_items, review_items, merge_items, github_work (link.rs 1092-1240) into one inbox owner in core/crates/obid/src/sphere/inbox.rs, made by obid at start and given to Jobs and Link.
- Point the 18 outside callers at it; the Link calls it for obiRequest items.
- No behaviour change: same files under home, same events, same API answers.

**Out:**
- Moving exchanges, sharing, pins or the room.
- A new crate.
- Changing what any card says.

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns
