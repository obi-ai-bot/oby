# feat(electron): Take over and Hand back on a task

<!-- Roadmap R12-09 · Sprint 10 · R12 Developer workflows · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

needs Sangam's design first

### Outcome

From a task, the owner opens it in Terminal with its session resumed, and later hands it back with one click.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R12-09, milestone R12 Developer workflows).

**Why:** #986's done-when needs buttons; Sangam's task menu (apps/electron/src/renderer/src/TaskMenu.tsx) has no Open in, Take over or Hand back.

**In:**
- Take over: call take-over, then run the handoff command in Terminal from main.
- Show in Finder and Open in editor on the worktree path.
- Hand back on a held task.
- Gate the actions on obid's capability list.

**Out:**
- Any word or layout before Sangam designs it.
- The iPhone.

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- The hold and the command.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- The menu items, the held state, and Hand back.

### Files

- apps/electron/src/renderer/src/TaskMenu.tsx

### Steps

- Ask Sangam for the menu items and the held state.
- Run the command in Terminal from main only; the renderer never builds a shell command.
- Wire Hand back; send both as the owner's signed words.
- Test TAKEOVER-11 and 12.

### Acceptance scenarios

- TAKEOVER-11 (proposed): Given a finished own-lane job, when the owner clicks Take over, then main opens Terminal in the worktree with the handoff command and the task shows as held.
- TAKEOVER-12 (proposed): Given an obid without the take-over capability, when a task menu opens, then Take over and Hand back are not shown.
