## What
`factory.json` (B00-06) says who runs each step; nothing honours it yet. A factory job for a step should run on that step's tool, account, model and effort, and Oby's second review on `steps.review.second`. Today the reviewer rule never picks Cursor (`core/crates/obid/src/jobs.rs`, `reviewer()`; `docs/cursor.md:19`, with no reason given).

## Why
- **Choosing has no effect until the jobs follow it.**
- **The owner's answer (2026-11-15):** every connected account may review, Cursor included, "cause we will be adding other accounts in the future". A Cursor-only owner's own work may otherwise never merge by itself.

## How
1. A factory job started for a step takes `agent`, `account`, `model` and `effort` from the step (`POST /v1/jobs` already takes the first three; add `effort` if it doesn't, add-only). `byRisk` applies with intake's risk.
2. `reviewer()` takes `steps.review.second` first when it's signed in and has room. Otherwise, with `whenFull: "wait"`, the review waits and says why; with `"any"`, today's rule picks.
3. Drop "never Cursor" from the reviewer rule: any connected tool may review, and one added later does from the day it's connected. **Spike first:** Cursor gives a review verdict reliably (10 reviews of real pull requests through obid). A review that gives no verdict counts as no review, as it does today for any tool.
4. The rule for a security path is unchanged: two approving reviews by different tools, or the owner's click (MERGING-13 to 16).
