## What
Settings › Factory (B00-09) shows, beside each step, how long it takes and how much of the account it uses. Those numbers should be true: this project's own history, not a guess.

## Why
- **Choosing a model without its cost in time and room is a guess.** No product checked shows more than a "Free" badge or a usage-limit mark (research in the plan, §1).
- **The owner's answer on hints (2026-11-15):** "% of the week" for plan accounts, dollars only for accounts billed by the token.

## How
1. For each step, over the project's last 20 jobs of that step: the median minutes, and the median share of the account's week (`sevenDay` before and after, `docs/api/accounts.md`).
2. An account billed by the token shows dollars from `prices_usd_per_mtok` and the job's tokens instead of a share of the week.
3. Fewer than 5 samples: `null` ("no history yet"). Gate and prove have minutes and no account.
4. Served in `hints` of `GET /v1/projects/{id}/factory/settings`; presets' totals are the sums.
