Step 2 of the Agents plan (docs/decisions/2026-11-16-agents-follow-openbot.md).

## What
An owner's agents can't reach each other. A "Researcher" can't ask the "Release notes" agent for a draft. Other people's Obys can be asked (`oby_ask`), but not your own agents. OpenBot's agents send messages to teammates, and the answer goes back to the sender.

## Why
- **The owner, 2026-11-16:** Agents follow OpenBot's agents.
- **Lane: dispatcher.** Carrying words between the owner's own agents is the dispatcher's.

## How
1. **The tool.** `oby_tell_agent(agent, words, reply)`: words to another of the owner's agents in the same profile. They wait for its next turn, as the owner's words do, marked as from that agent.
2. **The answer back.** When `reply` is true, the recipient's final answer for that turn goes to the sender's conversation as a message from it.
3. **Loop guards, as OpenBot's.** No message to itself and no duplicate recipient. No automatic reply to a reply. A chain stops at 4 hops from the owner's words, and a request past that is refused, saying why. At most 10 messages per agent per hour.
4. **Trust.** Words from another of the owner's agents are not outside text. A conversation that was untrusted passes that mark on with its words.
5. **What shows.** Both conversations show the message and the answer.

### Formal verification
A Quint model `docs/models/agent_messages.qnt`: invariants `noSelfMessage`, `chainBounded` (never more than 4 hops), `untrustedPassesOn`; witness `askAndAnswer`.

Acceptance (each a test named after it):
1. AGENTMSG-1: agent A's message reaches B's next turn, and B's answer shows in A's conversation.
2. AGENTMSG-2: a message to itself, or to an agent in another profile, is refused.
3. AGENTMSG-3: a chain A→B→C→D→E is refused at the fifth hop, with the reason in the sender's conversation.
4. AGENTMSG-4: an untrusted conversation's message marks the recipient's turn untrusted.
5. AGENTMSG-5: the 11th message in an hour from one agent is refused.
