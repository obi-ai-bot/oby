## What
- **Finding of V01-03:** this Mac's APFS takes `ſ` (U+017F) for `s` and `K` (U+212A, the Kelvin sign) for `k`, so they name the same file. `caught_by` folds only ASCII letters, so such a path is not caught. In `merge::tests`, `merging_15_a_unicode_case_fold_of_an_entry_is_caught` fails, for example:
  - `"AGENTſ.md"` on entry `"AGENTS.md"`;
  - `"JenKinsfile"` (with `K`) on entry `"Jenkinsfile"`;
  - `".buildKite/f"` (with `K`) on entry `".buildkite/"`;
  - `"docs/contracts/publiſhing.md"` on entry `"docs/contracts/publishing.md"`.
- **APFS's answer** (V01-03's build): `touch Jenkinsfile "$(printf 'Jen\xe2\x84\xaainsfile')" "$(printf 'Jenkin\xc5\xbffile')" && ls | wc -l` in a scratch folder prints `1`.
- **Harm:** a change to `AGENTſ.md`, `.agentſ/f` or `.buildKite/f` (with `K`) is a change to `AGENTS.md`, `.agents/` or `.buildkite/` on macOS, yet it merges after one review, where two approving reviews by different tools were needed (MERGING-15).

## Why
MERGING-15 holds every entry caught "in any letter case" because macOS reads a name as its case-folded form (`SECURITY_PATHS`'s doc). APFS folds more than ASCII, so `caught_by` misses names the Mac treats as an entry.

## How
- Make `caught_by` compare paths in the case folding APFS uses, at least for `ſ` and `K`, which fold to ASCII letters; decide with a test whether other non-ASCII folds reach an entry.
- Remove the `#[ignore = "finding V01-40"]` line from `merging_15_a_unicode_case_fold_of_an_entry_is_caught`.
- Keep `SECURITY_PATHS`, `fits` and `mod proofs` as they are.
