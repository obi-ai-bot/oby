# feat(ios): one recovery state machine for the phone relay

<!-- Roadmap R07-11 · Sprint 5 · R07 One wire contract · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

uses an existing screen: apps/shared/ReachNotice.swift (new state words need Sangam)

### Outcome

The iPhone reconnects by one written rule: short retries with jitter, a cooldown, and no retry when no retry can help. A hung connect step can no longer leave the app deaf.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R07-11, milestone R07 One wire contract).

**Why:** Reconnect is spread across Phone.swift (1,541 lines) and apps/shared/PhoneRelay.swift. Backoff exists only for 429/4008 (PhoneRelay.swift:479-481: 30 s doubling to 300 s, no jitter); other drops retry with no plan. Relay fixes were repeated (map: 4 of 6 relay commits are fixes; epic #1942).

**In:**
- new: docs/contracts/reconnect.md with states online, waiting (1, 2, 4, 8, 16 s plus up to 20% jitter), connecting, cooldown (30 s after 5 failures), suspended (version refusal, 4001 forgotten, 4003 replaced; cleared by the owner's action or a return to the app)
- A table of test cases in new: docs/contracts/wire/reconnect-cases.json that every client runs
- A small Swift type holding the machine; PhoneRelay and Phone.swift call it
- Each connect step has its own deadline
- Side effects (clearing alerts, reading the Sphere) run after online, never inside connect
- Catch-up after online uses R06's event seq when it exists, else a full reload

**Out:**
- Electron (R07-12)
- Android (R07-15)
- obid changes

### Steps

- Real-iPhone check: kill the Sphere connection and watch timing.

### Acceptance scenarios

- RECONNECT-1 (proposed): Given the phone is online, when the socket drops, then it waits 1 s plus up to 20% and connects again, doubling to 16 s.
- RECONNECT-2 (proposed): Given five failed connects in a row, when the fifth fails, then it waits 30 s in cooldown and starts a new series.
- RECONNECT-3 (proposed): Given the Sphere closes with 4001 or 4003, when the socket ends, then the phone is suspended and opens no socket until the owner acts or the app returns to the front.
- RECONNECT-4 (proposed): Given the hello step hangs, when its deadline passes, then that try counts as failed and the machine moves on.
