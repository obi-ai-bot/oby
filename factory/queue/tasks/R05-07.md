# feat(obid): steer a running Claude Code or Codex job, with fallback to its next turn

<!-- Roadmap R05-07 · Sprint 5 · R05 Durable words · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

Words told to a running job with `steer: true` reach its agent inside the turn it works on, when its CLI accepts it; otherwise they wait for its next turn as today.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R05-07, milestone R05 Durable words).

**Why:** A job's turn can last hours (up to 200 model turns a round); words wait until it ends (#925). `Jobs::message` (jobs.rs:4044) delivers only at the next turn.

**In:**
- Add-only `steer: true` on POST /v1/jobs/{id}/messages.
- Codex jobs: `turn/steer` on the job's app-server connection (codex.rs `run_turn`).
- Claude Code jobs: a further `user` line on stdin, only if R05-01 proved it.
- Cursor, a parked job, a job not at work, or a refusal: the delivery stays `queued` with `why`, heard next turn as today (TASKCONV-14).

**Out:**
- Any app change: the Electron task card's 'Tell it what to do' (`STEER` in apps/electron/src/renderer/src/mock.ts:79) keeps sending without `steer` until Sangam decides.
- Another Oby's review (it takes no words).

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Whether a job's words steer or wait, and the fallback.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- Nothing changes for clients.

### Interfaces and dependencies

- Add-only: `steer` (boolean, default false) on POST /v1/jobs/{id}/messages.

### Acceptance scenarios

- TASKCONV-25 (proposed): Given a Cursor job or a parked job, when words come with `steer: true`, then they stay `queued` with a `why` and the job's next turn hears them, as TASKCONV-14 says.
