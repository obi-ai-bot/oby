# feat(electron): show a message's delivery status in the conversation

<!-- Roadmap R05-09 · Sprint 6 · R05 Durable words · effort S · risk low -->

### Sangam's screen

needs Sangam's design first

### Outcome

In the Electron app the owner sees which of their messages wait, which were not answered because Oby restarted, and can take a waiting one back, as Sangam designs it.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R05-09, milestone R05 Durable words).

**Why:** The renderer keeps its own `unanswered` set and `waiting` question (apps/electron/src/renderer/src/live.ts:50, :69) and nothing shows words that wait or were cut. #929 is open.

**In:**
- Read `delivery` on turns (R05-02) and show `queued` and `interrupted` as Sangam's screen shows them.
- His take-back control calls POST /v1/plans/cancel with `turn` (R05-08), gated on obid sending `delivery` (an older obid shows nothing new).

**Out:**
- Any steer control: only where Sangam's design shows one.
- iPhone: later, with its own design.

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Nothing new: the status comes from obid.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- Waiting and not-answered marks on the owner's messages; a take-back control.

### Steps

- Get Sangam's screen and words for waiting, not answered and take back (Monday review).

- Write delivery.test.tsx named after CHATFIRST-34 to 36.

### Acceptance scenarios

- CHATFIRST-34 (proposed): Given a turn under way, when the owner sends more words, then they show as waiting, as Sangam's screen shows, until a turn takes them.
- CHATFIRST-35 (proposed): Given a message whose delivery is `interrupted`, when the conversation opens, then it shows as not answered, as Sangam's screen shows.
- CHATFIRST-36 (proposed): Given a waiting message, when the owner takes it back, then it leaves the conversation's waiting list and its words go back into the message box.
