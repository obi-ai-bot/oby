# feat(electron): compare a member's key and forget remembered decisions in Settings › Obysphere

<!-- Roadmap R10-10 · Sprint 8 · R10 Finish the Obysphere client · effort M · risk low -->

### Sangam's screen

needs Sangam's design first (SphereSettings.tsx has 'What your Oby tells' but no members' keys or decisions; design/app has no such screen). Engine-only parts: R10-08, R10-09.

### Outcome

The owner can verify a teammate by comparing a fingerprint with them, see the look-alike mark, and see and forget what their Oby does for teammates without asking, including standing GitHub approvals.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R10-10, milestone R10 Finish the Obysphere client).

**Why:** Every post and thread says `Not verified` (RoomView.tsx:49) because no app calls GET/POST /v1/obid/sphere/pins. Remembered decisions, including `host_approval`, cannot be listed or forgotten in any app (#1394); R10-04 makes 'Accept, and remember' one click, so its undo must be one click too.

**In:**
- In Settings › Obysphere: each member with its fingerprint and Verify (POST pins/{owner} with the fingerprint, after 'Did <owner id> read you this?').
- The `lookalike` mark from R10-08 beside a member and in the thread header.
- 'What your Oby does without asking': GET /v1/obid/decisions as words, each with Forget (DELETE /v1/obid/decisions/{id}).

**Out:**
- Phones.
- The audit chain view (#1230).

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Nothing new: pins, decisions and their routes exist.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- Fingerprints, Verify, the look-alike mark, the decision list and Forget.

### Acceptance scenarios

- SPHERE-11 (proposed): Given a member trusted on first sight, when the owner confirms the fingerprint they were read, then `verified` is true and the thread and room drop `Not verified`.
- SPHERE-12 (proposed): Given a member with a `lookalike`, when Settings or the thread shows them, then the mark names the other id.
- SPHERE-13 (proposed): Given a remembered `host_approval`, when the owner clicks Forget, then DELETE /v1/obid/decisions/{id} runs and the next approval asks again.
