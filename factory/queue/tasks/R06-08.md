# refactor(electron): follow obid's events once, in main, and feed the window and the alerts from it

<!-- Roadmap R06-08 · Sprint 5 · R06 Events that clients only apply · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

uses an existing screen: apps/electron/src/renderer/src/Splash.tsx, ChatView.tsx, AskCard.tsx (no visual change)

### Outcome

The Mac app has one connection to obid's events and one copy of obid's state, in main. The window and the Mac's alerts can no longer disagree, and obid sees one follower instead of two.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R06-08, milestone R06 Events that clients only apply).

**Why:** The renderer (live.ts:686 connect, through main/obid.ts:378 'obid-follow') and main (notify.ts:371, `?alerts=1`) each follow GET /v1/events, each keep the inbox, limit decisions and conversation titles (notify.ts:85-102), and both poll /v1/limit-decisions every 15 s (live.ts:674, notify.ts:457). #1642 item 1 is one disagreement.

**In:**
- new: apps/electron/src/main/live.ts: follows `/v1/events?alerts=1` from launch to quit, runs live-state.ts `apply`, does the snapshot reads, and holds the connection state (connecting, live, down with obid's reason).
- Two channels through R06-04's helper: the renderer asks for the snapshot on open, and gets each applied event after it (plus `step`, `delta`, `transcript`, `roomPost`, `peerThread` forwarded as they are).
- A 'load' channel (a conversation's turns, a job by id) that main reads and applies, replacing the renderer's own state-filling reads (live.ts:838 openConversation, :94 lookUpJobOf).
- notify.ts reads main's state for inbox items, limit decisions, titles and turns; its own maps and its stream go. Its /v1/alerts list (obid's decision, ALERTS-1) stays.
- One limit-decisions refresh in main: on `limitDecision`, `job` and `accounts` events, and one 15 s poll, not two.

**Out:**
- Resuming with `after` (R06-09).
- Removing the 15 s poll: only once obid sends `limitDecision` for every change of options (not confirmed; see unknown).

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Nothing new; ALERTS-3 still uses `alerts=1` to know that the Mac app follows.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- The same screens, cards and alerts; the renderer never sees obid's token and keeps its sandbox.

### Steps

- Write main/live.ts from notify.ts's `follow` and live.ts's `connect`/`sync`/`down`, using live-state.ts.
- Add the snapshot, event and load channels with decoders; replace preload's `obid.follow` with `obid.live(onSnapshot, onEvent)`; delete 'obid-follow' from main/obid.ts.
- Point renderer live.ts at the snapshot and events; delete its connect, sync, down and its 15 s limit poll.
- Point notify.ts at main's state; delete its `inbox`, `limits`, `titles` maps, its sync and its stream.
- Write ALERTS-9 to 11 and LIVE-10, 11; prove ALERTS-9 red with two followers.
- Run `npm test`, then on a real Mac `npm run test:core`, `test:chat-first`, `test:live-task` and an alert with the window closed.

### Acceptance scenarios

- ALERTS-9 (proposed): Given the Mac app runs, when the test counts obid's followers of GET /v1/events from this app, then there is one, with `alerts=1`, whether or not the window is open.
- ALERTS-10 (proposed): Given an inbox item answered on the iPhone, when its event arrives, then the window's card and the Mac's alert both drop it from the same state.
- ALERTS-11 (proposed): Given the window closes and opens again, when it opens, then it shows main's state at once with no new stream to obid.
- LIVE-10 (proposed): Given the renderer asks main to load a conversation's turns, when obid answers, then the turns enter the state through `apply` and every open window gets the events.
- LIVE-11 (proposed): Given obid stops, when main's stream ends, then the window shows 'down' with obid's reason as today, and main reconnects with the same back-off.
