# test(obid): the phone relay keeps its slots and writes across drops, rotations and reconnects

<!-- Roadmap R10-12 · Sprint 8 · R10 Finish the Obysphere client · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

The three relay bugs of one week become tests against a fake Sphere relay, and slot and session state has one owner, so the next drop is caught before a phone hangs.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R10-12, milestone R10 Finish the Obysphere client).

**Why:** relay/mod.rs (1,251 lines) has 4 unit tests and no integration test with a WebSocket. Three 'unwedge' fixes in one week: #1949 (a dropped session cancelled a write), #1992 (32-request cap across sessions), #2001 (a dropped phone's slots leaked); 4 of 6 relay commits are fixes. Slot counts live in `Live`, `Lanes` and `Stream` (mod.rs:89-182).

**In:**
- A fake Sphere relay socket in a new test file that drives obid's relay with real envelopes (relay/envelope.rs).
- Scenarios: drop mid-write, drop with 32 open requests, token rotation 45 s before expiry, a phone kicked (4003) and back, a counter gap (`reset`).
- Put slot accounting in one small type with its invariant checked in debug builds; delete the duplicated counters.

**Out:**
- Phone clients and their recovery (R07).
- The envelope format and the Sphere's relay route.

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Relay session, lane and slot state.

### Steps

- Add PAIRING-8 to 11 to the contract.

### Acceptance scenarios

- PAIRING-8 (proposed): Given a phone's write whose session drops after its last frame, when the session ends, then the write runs once and its answer is not sent to a later session.
- PAIRING-9 (proposed): Given a phone with 32 open requests whose session drops, when it reconnects, then its slots are free within one second and a new request runs.
- PAIRING-10 (proposed): Given the relay token rotates while requests are open, when the new socket opens, then no open request is lost or answered twice.
- PAIRING-11 (proposed): Given a counter gap, when obid sees it, then it sends `reset`, frees that session's slots and accepts a new hello.
