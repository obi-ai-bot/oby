# feat(obid): steer a running conversation turn, with fallback to the queue

<!-- Roadmap R05-05 · Sprint 4 · R05 Durable words · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

A client may send `steer: true` with words; obid puts them into the turn that runs when the agent CLI accepts it, and otherwise queues them as today. The default stays the queue.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R05-05, milestone R05 Durable words).

**Why:** Words sent while a turn runs wait up to the 10-minute budget (map problem 'No steering', medium; brain/turn.rs `wait_turn`). Codex `turn/steer` is already sent for declines (codex.rs:812). Open asks: #926, #924.

**In:**
- Add-only `steer: true` on POST /v1/plans.
- obid writes the delivery `queued` first. If a turn runs in that conversation and its CLI steers (R05-01's table): `start`, send, then `confirm` with `by` = the running turn id when the CLI acknowledges.
- Codex: `turn/steer` with `expectedTurnId`, its reply read.
- Claude Code: a further `user` line on the open stdin, only if R05-01 proved it.
- Cursor, no turn running, a refusal, a changed turn id, or no acknowledgement: `restore` to `queued` with `why` `provider-unsupported` or `steer-failed`; the drain answers them after the turn as today.
- The running turn's `Control.signed` becomes false when steered words are not signed.

**Out:**
- Any app change: no client sends `steer` until Sangam's design shows it.
- Jobs (R05-07).

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Whether to steer or queue, the send, the acknowledgement and the fallback.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- Nothing changes for clients; a client that sends `steer` sees the `delivery` status change.

### Interfaces and dependencies

- Add-only: `steer` (boolean, default false) on POST /v1/plans.

### Acceptance scenarios

- DELIVERY-14 (proposed): Given a Codex conversation turn under way, when the owner's words come with `steer: true`, then obid sends `turn/steer` with that turn's id, the delivery reads `running` with `by` the running turn, it is `answered` when that turn ends, and no new turn starts for it.
- DELIVERY-15 (proposed): Given a Cursor turn under way, or Codex refusing the steer, when words come with `steer: true`, then the delivery goes back to `queued` with `why: provider-unsupported` or `steer-failed`, and the next turn answers it as without `steer`.
- DELIVERY-16 (proposed): Given a turn under way, when words come without `steer`, then they wait and are answered by the next turn, exactly as today.
- DELIVERY-17 (proposed): Given a turn that is the owner's own word, when unsigned words steer it, then from then on what that turn asks for waits for a Confirm (TASKCONV-6).
- DELIVERY-18 (proposed, only if R05-01 proves it): Given a Claude Code turn under way, when words come with `steer: true`, then they go on its stdin and the delivery is `running` once Claude Code acknowledges them.
