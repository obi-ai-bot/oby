## What
- The owner paired their MacBook Pro as another computer (0.2.26, link and code), switched the window to it, and clicked **Settings › Accounts › Add account › Claude Code › Sign in**. The sheet said "Couldn't connect Claude Code. Try again." (2026-11-23).
- Nothing reached the other computer: this Mac's forwarding layer (`core/crates/obid/src/remote/forward.rs`, `named`) refuses any route that `gateway::allows_computer` doesn't list with `404 not_for_a_computer`. `POST /v1/accounts`, `GET /v1/accounts/{id}/sign-in` (and its `?with=chatgpt`), the sign-in's code and finish steps, `PATCH`/`DELETE /v1/accounts/{id}` and `POST /v1/accounts/{id}/sign-out` are on neither `ROUTES` nor `COMPUTER_ROUTES` (`core/crates/obid/src/gateway.rs`).
- The window turns that refusal into its generic words: `signInWhy` (`apps/electron/src/renderer/src/signInWhy.ts`) has no words for `not_for_a_computer`, so the person never learns why.

## Why
Using another computer means its agents run the work with that computer's own logins. Today a person who wants another account there has to walk to that Mac. With testers pairing their second Macs, the first thing they try fails with a message that says nothing true.

## How
1. **Words first (small, can land alone):** every window refusal with reason `not_for_a_computer` says "Do this on that computer itself: it isn't available from here yet." (Sangam's words when he has them); `signInSaid` gains it, and other sheets that call obid while a computer is in use show the same.
2. **Accounts through the computer:** add the account routes above to `COMPUTER_ROUTES`, each with the screen that needs it (review each one on its own, as the list's comment asks; nothing that hands a login to this Mac).
3. **The sign-in runs on the computer that keeps the login:** the agent's own login (`claude auth login`, `codex login`, `cursor-agent login`) runs on the other computer, as it does for its own window. What the window gets is what the sign-in already gives it: the provider's URL to open in **this** Mac's browser and, where the provider sends the person back to a loopback callback, the code-paste step instead (Claude Code's and Codex's device/paste flows). No token, refresh token or login file ever crosses to this Mac (AGENTS.md, "Logins and secrets"); obid on the other computer reads, stores and forwards nothing it doesn't today.
4. Signed words: account changes through another computer are signed by this Mac's device key as the other computer's paired device, as the other forwarded writes are (docs/contracts/signed-words.md).
