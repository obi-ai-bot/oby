# chore(ios): the iPhone stops calling routes obid does not serve

<!-- Roadmap R03-04 · Sprint 1 · R03 Delete what has no caller · effort S · risk low -->

### Sangam's screen

uses an existing screen: apps/ios/app/JobsActivity.swift (no visible change)

### Outcome

The iPhone app has no code for Live Activity push tokens, model settings writes or Mac sharing that only ever got a 404. The Live Activity still updates while the app runs.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R03-04, milestone R03 Delete what has no caller).

**Why:** apps/ios/app/JobsActivity.swift:41 asks for `pushType: .token` and hands tokens to POST /v1/activities (Runner.swift:1644-1648), which obid does not route (api.rs). Its comment points at the deleted Runner/Push.swift (:7). Runner.swift also calls PUT /v1/settings/models (:1264; only Phone.swift:1408 calls it, and nothing calls that) and /v1/obid/sharing/mac, macs, grants and shared (:1296-1361, with types at :2054-2170), which have no caller outside Runner.swift and no route in obid. apps/shared/SlackStatus.swift (172 lines) and SentryStatus.swift (76) declare types no file uses; only core/crates/obid/tests/swift_client.rs:36-37 compiles them. Searched apps/ios, apps/shared, apps/watch and core/crates/obid/tests.

**In:**
- JobsActivity.swift: request the activity with no push type; remove the push-to-start and push-token loops and `register`; fix the comment.
- Runner.swift: remove registerActivity, setModels, sharingMac, setSharingMac, stopGuests, macs, place, sharingGrants, setGrant, sharedWithMe and their types; remove Phone.setModels.
- Delete apps/shared/SlackStatus.swift and SentryStatus.swift and their lines in swift_client.rs APP_SOURCES and in apps/Oby.xcodeproj.
- apps/ios/app/README.md: the Live Activity bullet says it updates while the app is open only.
- Extend the Brain's call scan (tools/brain/brain.config.ts, today apps/electron/src only) to `call("METHOD", "v1/...")` in apps/shared and apps/ios.

**Out:**
- The notification categories `permission`, `permissionRule` and `plan` (Notifications.swift:36-38): obid still sends them (push.rs:383-392), so they stay.
- The Watch's calls (/v1/watches, WatchPairing.swift): R03-07.
- Background Live Activity updates through an obid APNs path: new work, not in R03.
- Android's Runner.kt: frozen by R03-08.
