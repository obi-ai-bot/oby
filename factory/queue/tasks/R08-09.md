# feat(obid): pace usage reads per account with backoff, and forget them on a login change

<!-- Roadmap R08-09 · Sprint 6 · R08 One interface for every agent CLI · effort S · risk low -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

An account whose usage read fails is asked less and less often (5 minutes, doubling to an hour) while the last figure stays shown, and a new login never shows the old login's numbers.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R08-09, milestone R08 One interface for every agent CLI).

**Why:** `want_usage` / `read_due_usages` (jobs.rs:2291, 2310) read every signed-in account every 10 minutes (`usage_every`) and retry a failed read after the same 10 minutes (USAGE-4), whatever failed; `UsageReads` keeps `asked`, `never` and `limit_refreshed` per account but no failure count. Reads hit Claude Code's shared usage endpoint during 429 periods.

**In:**
- A per-account failure count and next-read time in `UsageReads`.
- A refusal's own read (R08-10) may skip the normal interval but not the failure wait.
- Sign-in, sign-out, removal or a login change forgets that account's reading, its hold-free state and its backoff.

**Out:**
- What a read parses.
- The Usage panel's words.

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- When each account's usage is read, and forgetting it on a login change.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- Nothing changes for clients: the panel already shows what obid gives.

### Steps

- Add USAGE-14 to 16 and change USAGE-4's '10 minutes' to the backoff, marked proposed.
- Add the failure count and next-read time to `UsageReads`; use them in `read_due_usages`.
- Clear an account's entry on sign-in, sign-out, removal and login change (accounts.rs).
- Write the three tests with `OBID_USAGE_EVERY_SECONDS` and a stand-in that fails.

### Acceptance scenarios

- USAGE-14 (proposed): Given an account whose usage read failed 3 times in a row, when the next app looks, then obid does not read it before 20 minutes after the last failure, and the panel keeps the last figure.
- USAGE-15 (proposed): Given an account in backoff, when a read succeeds, then the next read is due after the normal 10 minutes.
- USAGE-16 (proposed): Given an account with a reading, when the owner signs it in again with another login, then `GET /v1/status` shows no figure for it until a read of the new login answers.
