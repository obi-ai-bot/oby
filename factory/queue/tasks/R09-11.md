# feat(obid): stay awake for any pending work, and catch up at once after a sleep

<!-- Roadmap R09-11 · Sprint 7 · R09 Ship and debug safely · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

The Mac does not idle-sleep under a conversation turn, a job waiting for a slot or a routine due soon. After a lid close, obid notices the sleep at the next tick and catches up at once: routines, the Sphere link and stalled streams do not wait out their timers.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R09-11, milestone R09 Ship and debug safely).

**Why:** power.rs holds caffeinate only while a job worker runs (RECOVERY-10, jobs.rs:3751, 3842). recovery.md's 'The Mac sleeps' row says 'no sleep detection': a sleep-cut stream is noticed only by the stall window (`stallAfter`, 15 minutes by default, recovery.md row 105), and the Sphere follower waits out its back-off.

**In:**
- power.rs: `keep` takes a list of reasons computed each tick from obid's state (worker, queued job, conversation turn, routine due in 10 minutes); an unknown kind counts as a reason.
- GET /v1/status: optional `awake: {held, reasons}` (add-only).
- One tick compares wall clock and monotonic time; a gap over two ticks is a sleep.
- On wake, one function: run the routine check now (a missed run records the sleep as its reason), restart the Sphere link's poll without back-off, re-read account usage, and treat streams silent across the gap as cut so RECOVERY-1 continues them.

**Out:**
- Holding sleep with the lid closed (macOS does not allow it).
- Changing `stallAfter`.

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- The reason list, the sleep detection, and the wake actions.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- Nothing required; status.awake lets an app say why the Mac stays on later.

### Interfaces and dependencies

- GET /v1/status: optional `awake` object (add-only).

### Steps

- Change `Awake::keep(bool)` to take reasons; build them in one place from existing state; unknown counts.
- Add `awake` to status.
- Add the clock-gap check to an existing one-minute tick, with an injected clock for tests.
- Write the wake function; call the routine check, the Sphere link's reconnect and the usage read; mark silent streams cut.
- Replace the 'no sleep detection' row in recovery.md.
- Write RECOVERY-22 to 26 with the injected clock (no real sleeps).

### Acceptance scenarios

- RECOVERY-22 (proposed): Given no job runs and a conversation turn runs, when obid ticks, then the sleep assertion is held and status.awake.reasons names the turn.
- RECOVERY-23 (proposed): Given a routine due in 5 minutes and nothing else, when obid ticks, then the assertion is held; with `keepAwake: false` it is not.
- RECOVERY-24 (proposed): Given the wall clock jumps 30 minutes past the monotonic clock, when obid ticks, then it records one sleep and runs the wake path once.
- RECOVERY-25 (proposed): Given a job's stream was silent across a detected sleep, when the wake path runs, then the turn is continued under RECOVERY-1 at once, not after `stallAfter`.
- RECOVERY-26 (proposed): Given a routine time passed during the sleep within `graceMinutes`, when the wake path runs, then it runs once and its run says the Mac was asleep.
