# refactor(obid): move the job lifecycle rules into an obid-rules crate with no I/O

<!-- Roadmap R04-14 · Sprint 9 · R04 One owner for job state · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

The lifecycle and chain rules compile in a crate that cannot reach tokio, the HTTP layer, the store or Jobs, so Cargo refuses a rule that does I/O or takes the lock.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R04-14, milestone R04 One owner for job state).

**Why:** Even after R04-08 the rules sit in the obid crate beside Jobs and can call into it. The map shows decisions reaching into Jobs and its lock (jobs.rs:7150-7260, done_with at jobs.rs:14422 reads State).

**In:**
- new: core/crates/obid-rules with serde only; it holds Status, Command, transition and the chain carrier over a slim `Facts` view of Job (the fields the rules read).
- obid builds Facts from Job and applies the Change; job/lifecycle.rs and job/chain.rs become thin adapters.

**Out:**
- The recovery table, trust mark and merge eligibility (each a later PR, after #1943 and #1940 settle).
- Moving Job itself.

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Same rules, new home.
