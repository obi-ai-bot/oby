# feat(ios, mcp): the iPhone and oby-mcp send a request key

<!-- Roadmap R02-08 · Sprint 1 · R02 Commands that are safe to repeat · effort S · risk low -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

A relay retry from the iPhone or a repeated oby-mcp call makes one job or follow-up.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R02-08, milestone R02 Commands that are safe to repeat).

**Why:** apps/shared/Runner.swift and mcp/server/Server.swift post jobs, messages and answers with no key. Relay retries are the main source of duplicates from the phone.

**In:**
- Runner.swift: one key per action, kept across retries.
- Server.swift: one key per MCP call.

**Out:**
- Android.

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Nothing new.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- Nothing visible.

### Files

- apps/shared/Runner.swift
- apps/shared/DeviceKey.swift
- mcp/server/Server.swift
- core/crates/obid/tests/swift_client.rs

### Steps

- Add the key to Runner.swift's create, message, stop and answer calls, behind the status `receipts` flag.
- Add it to oby-mcp's job and message tools.
- Extend tests/swift_client.rs to send a request twice.

### Acceptance scenarios

- RECEIPTS-16 (proposed): Given the iPhone answers Try again and the relay retries the request, then the job runs one more round.

Contract: new: docs/contracts/receipts.md (prefix RECEIPTS)

### Done when
