Step 2 of the Agents plan (docs/decisions/2026-11-16-agents-follow-openbot.md).

## What
"Make me an agent that triages my inbox every morning" can't be done in words: an agent is made only in the New agent sheet, and no `oby_*` tool makes or changes one. In OpenBot any agent makes a teammate in chat (`create_agent`), and the new agent copies its maker's provider and model.

## Why
- **The owner, 2026-11-16:** Agents follow OpenBot's agents.
- **Lane: dispatcher.**

## How
1. **The tools.** `oby_make_agent(name, color, instructions, tool, connectors)` and `oby_change_agent(id, …)`, from Oby's conversation and from any agent, as in OpenBot. Without a tool, the new agent takes its maker's.
2. **Never wider than its maker.** A made agent's level (B00-61) is at most its maker's; only the owner's signed click widens it later. `oby_change_agent` can narrow an agent's level, never widen it.
3. **Limits.** The existing 50-agent cap; at most 5 agents made per hour by agents.
4. **What shows.** The new agent appears in Agents at once, marked "Made by <maker>"; the owner can edit or delete it (B00-65).
