# refactor(obid): Stop, Try again and Another round as lifecycle commands; a never-started job reruns

<!-- Roadmap R04-06 · Sprint 5 · R04 One owner for job state · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

uses an existing screen: apps/electron/src/renderer/src/TaskCard.tsx

### Outcome

The owner's three lifecycle buttons go through Jobs::apply with one rule each, and Try again on a task that never started reruns it instead of piling up follow-ups.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R04-06, milestone R04 One owner for job state).

**Why:** stop (jobs.rs:3982) writes status and five side fields in its own closure; Try again in message_as (around jobs.rs:4340-4405) locks, calls next_round and saves outside update(); review_round_again (jobs.rs:11855) and the change-request round (jobs.rs:12156) repeat the carry check. #1851: Try again on a never-started task makes a new follow-up each time (six for one review).

**In:**
- Commands Stop{by}, TryAgain{source}, AnotherRound, each deciding status, cleared fields, refusals (branch busy, follow-up carries the branch, account-limit choice pending) in lifecycle.rs.
- message_as's Try-again path calls Jobs::apply; its own save and event go.
- TryAgain on a job with no session and no moves requeues it from its own task (new round 1 in its worktree) instead of refusing (JOBSTATE-6 changes to JOBSTATE-8).

**Out:**
- Replacing the Try-again magic words with a typed route (R02 or R07 own wire changes).
- Recovery counter resets (owner_spoke stays as it is, #1943).
- Collapsing a failing chain into one Needs-you card (#1851's second point): a client change, left on #1851.

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Stop, Try again and Another round rules and refusals.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- Nothing changes for clients; Try again on a never-started task shows the same task running instead of a new follow-up card.

### Steps

- Move stop's status write into Stop; keep drop_asks, the Signal::Stop send and the review hook in Jobs::stop.
- Move the Try-again refusals and next_round call from message_as into TryAgain; Jobs::apply saves and sends the event.
- Move review_round_again's and the change-request round's checks into AnotherRound.
- Change JOBSTATE-6's test into JOBSTATE-8 and update runner-api.md's Try-again sentence.
- Lower state_owner counts and jobs_rs_limit.

### Acceptance scenarios

- JOBSTATE-8 (proposed): Given a failed job that never started (no session, no moves), when the owner sends the Try-again words, then the same job is queued again from its own task, and no follow-up is made.
- JOBSTATE-9 (proposed): Given a done job, when the owner sends Try again and Stop within one second, then exactly one wins by lock order, the job file shows its stoppedBy or its new round, and no worker of the older run ends it.
