Step 1 of the Agents plan (docs/decisions/2026-11-16-agents-follow-openbot.md).

## What
obid can already change or delete an agent (`PATCH` and `DELETE /v1/helpers/{id}`, HELPERS-4), but the app has no way to: once made, an agent can't be renamed, recoloured, given new instructions or removed. Its model and account come from the conversation's picker, so its pill reads "Auto" where screen 08 shows the agent's own tool and model.

## Why
- **The owner, 2026-11-16:** Agents follow OpenBot's, where each agent has its own model, reasoning and account.
- **Lane: dispatcher.**

## How
1. **obid.** A helper gains add-only `account` and `model` (null: the tool's default, as today). A turn uses them when set.
2. **The app.** An agent's menu offers Edit (the New agent sheet, filled in) and Delete (with a confirmation in the page). Edit sends `PATCH`; Delete sends `DELETE`.
3. **The pill.** An agent's conversation shows its tool and model, as screen 08 does, instead of "Auto".
