# fix(ios): the phone's outbox sends in order and never drops words silently

<!-- Roadmap R07-13 · Sprint 6 · R07 One wire contract · effort S · risk low -->

### Sangam's screen

needs Sangam's design first

### Outcome

Words typed while offline go out in order, once, with the same id; words that cannot be confirmed wait for the owner with Edit and Don't send instead of disappearing.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R07-13, milestone R07 One wire contract).

**Why:** apps/ios/app/Offline.swift keeps the queue in UserDefaults (line 89) and prunes past maxEntries = 50 and maxAge = 7 days (lines 78-79) with no notice. obid dedupes words sent again with the same `id` (`repeated`, runner-api.md), but a resend outside that window can send twice.

**In:**
- One entry in flight per conversation, always the same turn `id`
- Past obid's repeated window: state Not confirmed with Edit and Don't send
- No silent prune: an entry past the limit shows as not sent
- Store as a file in the app container with write-then-rename instead of UserDefaults, migrating the existing queue

**Out:**
- Android
- obid changes

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- The repeated window (documented in runner-api.md).

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- Not confirmed, Edit and Don't send on the queued message.

### Files

### Steps

- Confirm the repeated window length in runner-api.md and the code.
- Move storage to a file with write-then-rename; migrate UserDefaults once.
- Serialise sends per conversation.
- Add the Not confirmed state.
- Write IOSNEW-16 to 18 tests.

### Acceptance scenarios

- IOSNEW-16 (proposed): Given two queued messages for one conversation, when the phone comes online, then the second is sent only after obid answered the first.
- IOSNEW-17 (proposed): Given a queued message older than obid's repeated window, when the phone comes online, then it is not sent and shows Not confirmed with Edit and Don't send.
- IOSNEW-18 (proposed): Given 51 queued messages, when the 51st is added, then none is dropped and the oldest shows as not sent.
