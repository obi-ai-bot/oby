# feat(obid): serve the owner actions a job's state allows

<!-- Roadmap R04-11 · Sprint 8 · R04 One owner for job state · effort M · risk low -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

obid tells every app which of Stop, Try again, Another round, Move, Dismiss, Publish and Delete a job allows now, from the same lifecycle rules that run the action, so no app has to guess.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R04-11, milestone R04 One owner for job state).

**Why:** The Electron app derives its buttons in live.ts askOf (line 266) and taskOf (line 470) from Job fields, the inbox and its own state, and decides Try-again words itself (againWords, live.ts:260); the phone apps copy the derivation. 7 of 35 Electron fixes were about the task card after Stop, failure or retry (input row). obid already refuses with words, but only after the click.

**In:**
- `actions` (array of strings: stop, tryAgain, anotherRound, move, dismiss, publish, delete) on the job object, computed by one fn in job/lifecycle.rs that dry-runs each Command through transition (and chain.rs for publish/delete).
- A job event to a parent when its follow-up's change flips the parent's actions (the #1779 gap).
- Refusals keep their 409 and words; add a `state` key naming the status the refusal saw.

**Out:**
- Any client change (R04-12, R10 for the phones).
- New actions or new statuses.

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Which owner actions each job allows now.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- Nothing changes until R04-12: the key is ignored by today's apps.

### Interfaces and dependencies

- Add `actions` (array of strings) to the job object in GET /v1/jobs, GET /v1/jobs/{id} and the job event.
- Add `state` to the 409 body of the job action routes.

### Steps

- Write the allowed-actions fn as dry runs of transition; no second rule.
- Add the key in to_json; to_json sees one job, so compute it where the jobs map is at hand (Jobs::update_after and the list route) and pass it in.
- Send the parent's job event from chain.rs when a carrier appears or leaves.
- Test every status row; document the key and the 409 `state` in runner-api.md.

### Acceptance scenarios

- JOBSTATE-12 (proposed): Given a job in each status (queued, running, done with and without unpublished commits, failed with and without a session, stopped, interrupted), when GET /v1/jobs/{id} answers, then `actions` lists exactly the commands lifecycle would accept, and each listed action, sent, is not refused for its state.
- JOBSTATE-13 (proposed): Given a done job whose follow-up starts, when the follow-up is created, then a job event for the first job arrives with tryAgain and publish gone from its actions.
