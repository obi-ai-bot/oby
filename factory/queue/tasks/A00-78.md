What the owner sees. Under the profile's name, a two-way switch: Projects and Agents. Agents lists the profile's agents, "Needs you" first: each row is an Oby face in the agent's colour, its name, what it is doing or last said, and one mark (working, needs you) or a time. "New agent" opens a sheet: "Its Oby" (six colours), "Name", "What should this agent help with?", "Runs on" (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor) and "Can use" (Gmail, Slack, GitHub, The web), with "Cancel" and "Create agent". An agent's conversation shows its face beside its words, its steps, its decisions with their options, and the message box says "Message <name>…". The message box shows what it talks through, as Sangam's pill does ("Auto" until chosen).
