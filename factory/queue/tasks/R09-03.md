# feat(obid): status.restart says when a restart loses nothing, and the app restarts obid only then

<!-- Roadmap R09-03 · Sprint 6 · R09 Ship and debug safely · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

uses an existing screen: apps/electron/src/renderer/src/SettingsView.tsx (About's update row); words to be checked with Sangam

### Outcome

After an update, the app restarts obid only when no job, conversation turn, publish, merge, check or open question would be cut. obid holds new admissions and routine runs while a restart waits, and the owner can still say Restart now.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R09-03, milestone R09 Ship and debug safely).

**Why:** restartIfStale (apps/electron/src/main/obid.ts:260-276) waits only for `status.running` to be 0, 20 times 30 s, then kickstarts obid anyway ('ponytail: waits for its jobs at most 10 minutes'). `running` counts jobs only: conversation turns, publishes, merges and checks are not counted, and nothing stops new work starting meanwhile.

**In:**
- obid: `restart: {ready, waitingFor: [{kind, id?}]}` in GET /v1/status, computed from obid's own state: running workers, a conversation turn in flight or queued words, a publish or merge claim, a check run, a permission or question waiting on a live process, a routine due in the next 10 minutes. An unknown kind of work counts as not ready.
- obid: POST /v1/restart/hold (loopback, runner token) that holds new job admissions and new routine runs until obid restarts or 30 minutes pass; routines due meanwhile run once after the restart.
- Electron: restartIfStale polls status every 5 s, sends the hold, and kickstarts only when `ready`; no 10-minute fallback.
- Electron: an owner's 'Restart now' path that kickstarts at once (Oby forbids nothing).

**Out:**
- Setting `autoInstallOnAppQuit` to false: quitting the app does not stop obid (it is a launch agent with KeepAlive), so install-at-quit cuts no obid work; UPDATES-1 stays.
- Sangam's restart island; until it exists the waiting reasons are one text line in About's update row.

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Which work a restart would cut (a list it computes each status from its own state).
- The hold, its expiry, and the routines it deferred.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- About's update row adds one line naming what obid waits for, and a Restart now button, until Sangam's island exists.

### Interfaces and dependencies

- GET /v1/status: optional `restart` object (add-only).
- POST /v1/restart/hold (new, loopback only, not on the gateway allowlist).

### Steps

- List every kind of in-flight work obid can lose at a restart, from jobs.rs, conversation.rs, publish.rs, merge.rs and routines.rs; write the list in engine.md.
- Compute `restart` in api.rs `status`; one function, unit-tested per kind; an unknown kind counts as busy.
- Add the hold: admission and the routine tick read it; it expires after 30 minutes.
- Replace restartIfStale's loop with a 5 s poll of `restart.ready`, sending the hold first.
- Add the Restart now path and the one-line reason in About.
- Write ENGINE-13 to 16 (obid side in core/crates/obid/tests/status.rs, app side in engine.test.ts).

### Acceptance scenarios

- ENGINE-13 (proposed): Given a conversation turn is running and no job is, when obid's binary was replaced, then status.restart.ready is false with waitingFor [{kind: 'turn'}] and the app does not kickstart obid.
- ENGINE-14 (proposed): Given a restart hold is set, when a job is dispatched, then it stays queued until obid restarts, and a routine due during the hold runs once after the restart.
- ENGINE-15 (proposed): Given a hold older than 30 minutes and no restart, when obid ticks, then the hold ends and admissions resume.
- ENGINE-16 (proposed): Given status.restart.ready is false, when the owner chooses Restart now, then the app kickstarts obid at once and the cut work is continued by restart recovery.
