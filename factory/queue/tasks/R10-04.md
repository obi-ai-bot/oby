# feat(electron): accept or decline a teammate's review request on Home and in their thread

<!-- Roadmap R10-04 · Sprint 7 · R10 Finish the Obysphere client · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

needs Sangam's design first (no review-request card exists in apps/electron/src/renderer/src or design/app; the nearest parts are QuestionCard.tsx and AskStack.tsx). Engine-only part: R10-03.

### Outcome

A teammate's review request is one card on Home's stack and in that member's thread, with what they ask quoted and Accept, Decline and the two remembered choices. Teammate approval and Obi reviews no longer stall on the receiving Mac.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R10-04, milestone R10 Finish the Obysphere client).

**Why:** live.ts keeps only `question` obiRequests on Home (questionOf, line ~360; ON_HOME has no obiRequest), and personCard.test.tsx:120 asserts 'peer_30_a_review_request_stays_off_the_stack_as_before'. obid already offers the four options (runner-api.md 'Obi-to-Obi requests in the inbox'). The phones show it (apps/shared/ChatRuns.swift:118, Logic.kt:340); the Mac does not. trim.md core 4 is reviews between teammates' Obys first.

**In:**
- A `ReviewRequest` card built from the item's typed fields (owner, repo, base and head, commit, conditions) and its quoted goal and summary as text nodes.
- Accept, Decline, Accept and remember, Decline and remember, Later, through POST /v1/inbox/{id}/answer (token alone, PEER-33).
- The same card over the member's thread box (PeerThread.tsx), as the question card is.
- Change PEER-30's last sentence and its test.

**Out:**
- Approving on GitHub (its own signed Confirm card already shows).
- Listing remembered decisions (R10-10).
- Phones.

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

### Acceptance scenarios

- PEER-30 (amended, proposed): '... A review request stays off the stack.' becomes 'A review request is its own card (PEER-44).'
- PEER-44 (proposed): Given a teammate's open review request, when Home shows, then one card names the owner id (codes for a non-ASCII id), the repository, the commit and the conditions, with their goal as a quote of text nodes, and Accept, Decline and Later.
- PEER-45 (proposed): Given that card, when the owner clicks Accept, then obid gets `Accept`, the card closes on every device with the `inbox` event, and the thread shows the review under way.
- PEER-46 (proposed): Given that card, when the owner picks 'Accept, and remember for this repo', then the answer goes once and the next request from that owner and repository makes no card.
- PEER-47 (proposed): Given a request that expires or is called off while the card shows, when the `inbox` event comes, then the card closes and says why, and a click sends nothing.
