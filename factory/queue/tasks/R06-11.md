# refactor(electron): answers to cards and task actions as pending entries

<!-- Roadmap R06-11 · Sprint 6 · R06 Events that clients only apply · effort S · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

uses an existing screen: apps/electron/src/renderer/src/TaskCard.tsx, AskCard.tsx and TaskMenu.tsx (no visual change)

### Outcome

A card the owner answered, or a task they stopped, shows as it does today until obid's event confirms it, and then shows exactly what obid says. The task-card state comes from obid plus one overlay, not from three maps.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R06-11, milestone R06 Events that clients only apply).

**Why:** `answered`, `answering` and `gone` (live.ts:57-62) and `askOf`/`taskOf` (live.ts:266, :470) build the task card from Job, inbox and local maps; seven of 35 fix(electron) commits re-fix that card after Stop, failure or retry (map: 'Task-card state re-fixed again and again').

**In:**
- Entries for inbox answers (answerJob, answerQuestion, answerOffer, live.ts:1107-1206) and job actions (stopJob, archiveJob, deleteJob, renameJob) keyed by the item or job id and the revision they act on.
- An entry leaves when the state holds the object at a newer revision, or the call fails.
- Delete `answered`, `answering` and the rest of `gone`.

**Out:**
- Showing a spinner instead of today's look: needs Sangam's design.

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- The job and inbox states and their revisions.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- The same cards and task menu as today.

### Steps

- Add action entries to pending.ts with LIVE-19 to 21; prove LIVE-21 red with answers written into state.
- Rewrite the answer and action functions to add an entry, call, and clear or fail it.
- Make askOf and taskOf read reduced state plus entries; delete answered, answering, gone.
- Run `npm test`; on a real Mac `npm run test:live-task` with Stop, failure and Try again.

### Acceptance scenarios

- LIVE-19 (proposed): Given the owner clicks Allow on a job's permission card, when obid's `inbox` event at a newer revision arrives, then the card shows obid's state and the entry goes.
- LIVE-20 (proposed): Given the owner clicks Stop, when the call fails, then the card shows the job as obid has it and the reason, as today.
- LIVE-21 (proposed): Given the owner answers a card and obid's event arrives before the call's answer, when the answer arrives, then nothing changes on the card.
