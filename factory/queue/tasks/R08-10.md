# feat(obid): after a refusal with no reset, read the account's usage before guessing an hour

<!-- Roadmap R08-10 · Sprint 6 · R08 One interface for every agent CLI · effort S · risk low -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

A limit with no stated reset waits until the reset the account's own usage shows, not a fixed hour; while it waits obid reads again every 15 minutes, so an early reset frees the work.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R08-10, milestone R08 One interface for every agent CLI).

**Why:** With no structured reset, `limit_reached` and `mark_full` hold for `UNKNOWN_RESET` (3,600 s, usage.rs:26 and jobs/account_limits.rs:26); limits.rs parses vendor words ('try again in 2 hours') as a source. Map: 'Limit and usage parsing depends on undocumented vendor shapes' (USAGE-7, USAGE-13).

**In:**
- On a refusal with no structured reset: one usage read at once (through the driver), taking the latest reset of a full window.
- `UNKNOWN_RESET` only when that read fails; limits.rs word parsing only as the last source.
- A re-read every 15 minutes while a hold lasts, within R08-09's backoff.

**Out:**
- New vendor parsers.
- ChatGPT logins (no usage to read; CHATGPT rules unchanged).

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Where a hold's end comes from: the tool's event, then a usage read, then the tool's words, then an hour.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- Nothing changes for clients: the job's line already says when at the latest.

### Acceptance scenarios

- USAGE-17 (proposed): Given a turn refused for a limit with no reset in its event, when the account's usage read shows its week full until Friday 09:00, then the hold lasts until Friday 09:00, not an hour.
- LIMITS-27 (proposed): Given a job waiting on a hold, when a re-read 15 minutes later shows every window with room, then the hold ends and the job resumes on that account within one tick.
