Design: docs/design/oby-home/ (screen 07: the profile menu's orange dot on a profile where something waits). Contract: docs/contracts/alerts.md, docs/contracts/profiles.md.

What. The Mac's alerts are obid's one list, `GET /v1/alerts` (ALERTS-1), read by main's one follower (`apps/electron/src/main/notify.ts`, `live.ts`). A00-79 makes every route answer for one profile, so main reads the current profile's list: something that waits on the owner in another profile (a permission card, a question, a plan to approve) shows only as the orange dot on that profile in the panel's menu (A00-76), and never as a Mac notification.

Why. The owner works in one company's profile at a time but wants every company's work to keep moving. A job in the other profile that stops on a question would sit unseen until the owner happens to open the profile menu, which is what alerts exist to prevent.

How. obid serves every profile's alerts on one add-only form of the list (for example `GET /v1/alerts?profiles=all`), each alert carrying its `profile` id; the list without it is unchanged, so the phones and older apps read what they read today. Main's follower reads that form. An alert from a profile other than the current one says the profile's name in its subtitle (Sangam's words when his screen has them), and clicking it, or answering it with a button, acts in that profile; a click that opens the window switches to that profile first. The phone stays on the default profile's alerts until the owner decides otherwise (a question in the report). The owner's rule of 2026-10-28 (nothing while the window is in front, ALERTS-8) is unchanged.
