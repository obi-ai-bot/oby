### Outcome

Conversations and answers to other Obys start their agent's turn through the same driver as jobs, so a protocol fix lands once.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R08-04, milestone R08 One interface for every agent CLI).

**Why:** brain/harness.rs ~2410-2425 calls `crate::codex::run_turn` and `crate::acp::run_turn` in its own branches, with launch builders split into harness_codex.rs and harness_cursor.rs; sphere/answering/providers.rs has its own `Tool::Codex`/`Cursor` paths and `Tool::Claude => unreachable!()` at line 152.

**In:**
- brain/harness.rs `session` turn start, harness_codex.rs `launch`, harness_cursor.rs `launch` behind `AgentDriver::run_turn` with conversation options.
- sphere/answering/providers.rs `run` through the driver.

**Out:**
- The conversation's prompt text (`first_prompt`): R08-14.
- The Sphere protocol and its server (Dmytro's).
- Conversation lanes (#2045).
