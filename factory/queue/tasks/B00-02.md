## What
Oby has no single place that says what can be called from the message box. Typing `/` (B00-04) needs one list: the conversation's project's factory, the profile's agents (helpers, A00-77), its routines, Oby's own commands, and each connected agent tool's commands and skills (reported by B00-03). obid answers that list; the app only filters and shows it.

## Why
- **The menu stays true.** Without one answer from obid, each client would hard-code its own list and drift from obid, as the mock already does.
- **Dispatcher, not harness.** obid lists what exists; it runs nothing new. Agents, routines and the factory are obid's; skills stay the agent CLI's (AGENTS.md, "Dispatcher, not harness").

## How
1. A read-only route, `GET /v1/commands?conversation=<id>`, built from what obid already holds: the conversation's project (A00-70, A00-73), the profile's helpers (A00-77), its routines (`docs/api/routines.md`), a fixed list of Oby's own commands (`plan`, `review`, `every`, `model`, `project`, `new`, `usage`, `settings`), and the skill reports of B00-03.
2. Each entry: `id`, `group` (`factory`, `agents`, `tool`, `routines`, `oby`: an open set, an app shows one it doesn't know under "More"), `name`, `about`, and as it applies `badge`, `project`, `children`, `color`, `tool`, `account`, `kind` (`skill` or `command`), `scope`, `argumentHint`, `seenAt`, `next`. A `revision` comes with the answer. The shape is in docs/design/oby-slash-and-factory-settings/README.md, §2.4.
3. Entries a conversation can't use are left out, not marked: no `factory` without a project; no `tool` entries for a conversation boxed for another Oby or marked untrusted (they get no account files that start programs: core/AGENTS.md, "Logins and secrets").
4. Loopback with the API token only; not on the gateway's allowlist. Add-only: the shape goes in `docs/api/commands.md` and `apps/electron/src/shared/obid-wire.ts`.

1. COMMANDS-1 (`commands_1_lists_factory_agents_routines_and_oby`): a conversation in project P, with 2 helpers and 1 routine in its profile and no skill reports, lists one `factory` entry for P, 2 `agents`, 1 `routines` and Oby's fixed commands, and no `tool` entries.
2. COMMANDS-2 (`commands_2_no_project_no_factory`): a conversation with no project lists no `factory` entry.
3. COMMANDS-3 (`commands_3_boxed_conversation_lists_no_tool_commands`): a conversation boxed for another Oby, or marked untrusted, lists no `tool` entries even when reports exist.
4. COMMANDS-4 (`commands_4_unknown_conversation_and_gateway`): an unknown conversation answers `404`; the route answers `403` through the gateway.
