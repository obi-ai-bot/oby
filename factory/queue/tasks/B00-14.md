## What
A profile is meant to be a place of its own (A00-79: its own conversations, projects, accounts and vault). The New profile form asks only a name and a colour. Someone running several companies needs to pick, when making a profile, which agent accounts it may use and its code-host login.

## Why
- **The several-companies persona's blocker** (the plan, §4): accounts and the GitHub login are the install's today.

## How
1. The sheet: name, colour, "Accounts it may use" (each signed-in account with a switch, its tool, and where else it's used), "Sign in to another account…", and Code host (GitHub, signed in or Sign in).
2. Account assignment goes through A00-79's profile accounts. A per-profile gh login needs its own folder per profile: if A00-79 doesn't give one, this task adds it, as `home/github` is today for the install.
3. Words are Sangam's: screen 18 is a proposal.
