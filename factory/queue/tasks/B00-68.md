Step 2 of the Agents plan (docs/decisions/2026-11-16-agents-follow-openbot.md).

## What
An agent's turn stops at 10 minutes and 50 model turns. Longer work, such as an afternoon of research, has nowhere to run: `oby_dispatch` starts only coding jobs in a repository. OpenBot's agents start background tasks, each its own thread with its own session, up to 4 running at once.

## Why
- **The owner, 2026-11-16:** "The agents part is more fore general tasks". General work needs long tasks without a repository.
- **Lane: dispatcher.** Starting, stopping and showing a task is the dispatcher's.

## How
1. **A job kind.** `agent` jobs (add-only kind): no repository, no check, no pull request. The job runs in its own folder under the agent's room, on the agent's tool, account, model and level, with the stall, ceiling and token-budget rules jobs already have (RECOVERY).
2. **The tool.** `oby_start_task(title, task)` from the agent's own conversation. At most 4 running and 8 open per agent.
3. **The result.** When it ends, its summary goes back to the agent's conversation as a message from the task, and the files it made stay in its folder, readable with `oby_read`.
4. **What shows.** Its card in the agent's conversation, with Stop, as a coding task's card (03-chat).
5. **jobs.rs may only shrink.** The new code goes in `jobs/agent_task.rs`.
