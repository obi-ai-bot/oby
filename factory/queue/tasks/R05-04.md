# refactor(obid): one event-driven drain per conversation in place of Turns.owners and Sessions.busy

<!-- Roadmap R05-04 · Sprint 3 · R05 Durable words · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

One place decides when a conversation's next words start and why they wait, from the stored deliveries. Two lock maps and the in-memory queue go.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R05-04, milestone R05 Durable words).

**Why:** Two per-conversation locks serialize turns: `Turns.owners` (brain/turn.rs:91) and `Sessions.busy` (brain/harness.rs:219, taken at :518 for prestart and :1335 for N9). `Turns.waiting` (turn.rs:88) duplicates the queued deliveries of R05-02. Map problem 'Conversation state is kept in many places' (medium) names this split as a source of repeated fixes.

**In:**
- `drain(conversation)` called on: new words, a turn's end, cancel, a run that exited, the prestart's end. Never on a timer.
- It starts the next batch (every `queued` delivery, oldest first, joined as today) only when no hold applies.
- Holds as a small enum: `turn-running`, `run-exiting` (N9: a cancelled run still exiting in the room), `prestarting` (start_next owns the room).
- Add-only `hold` on the conversation summary while words wait.
- Delete `Turns.waiting`, `Turns.owners`, `Turns::wait_turn`, `Next`, `Sessions.busy` and `Sessions::busy`; replace R05-03's `resume_queued` with `drain`.

**Out:**
- Account-limit and sign-in holds: today a conversation turn fails over or fails, it does not wait; add them only with a decision.
- Steer (R05-05).

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- When the next turn of a conversation starts, and the hold that says why it waits.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- Nothing changes for clients; `hold` is there for a later screen.

### Interfaces and dependencies

- Add-only: `hold` (open string) on a conversation in GET /v1/conversations and `conversation` events, left out when nothing waits.

### Steps

- List every caller of `wait_turn`, `Turns::begin`, `Sessions::busy` and `start_next`; write which event frees each.
- Add `drain` beside `Turns` (it owns the per-conversation `Control` list that Stop and cancel use).
- Move brain/mod.rs `plan` to: write queued, call drain, wait for its own delivery's answer (the request still answers as today: `none` for earlier words).
- Make the harness report 'run exited' to drain instead of holding `busy`; make `start_next` take the `prestarting` hold.
- Delete the old fields and their test `words_wait_for_the_turn_under_way_and_are_answered_together` once DELIVERY-2, 11, 12 and 13 cover it.
- Lower `obid_lines_limit` in scripts/oby-check.sh by what was deleted net.

### Acceptance scenarios

- DELIVERY-11 (proposed): Given a turn that was cancelled and whose agent still exits in the room, when the owner sends words, then they stay `queued` with `hold: run-exiting` and start only once the run exited.
- DELIVERY-12 (proposed): Given turns under way in two conversations, when words arrive in each, then neither waits for the other, and in each the oldest queued words go first.
- DELIVERY-13 (proposed): Given words queued behind a turn, when that turn ends, then the next turn starts from that event with no polling interval (the test drives a paused clock and sees it start).
