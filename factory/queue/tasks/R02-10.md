# fix(obid): a worker ends only the round it started

<!-- Roadmap R02-10 · Sprint 2 · R02 Commands that are safe to repeat · effort S · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

Stop, then Try again during the old worker's wrap-up, runs the new round. The old worker can no longer mark it Done.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R02-10, milestone R02 Commands that are safe to repeat).

**Why:** #1649: `end_done` checks only that the job is active; Try again's next_round sets a Stopped job back to Queued while the old worker finishes, and that worker marks it Done. A repeated command loses its effect.

**In:**
- The worker keeps the round it started (job.round); end_done and the other end paths change nothing when the job's round moved on.

**Out:**
- The owner's words taken too early (#1649 part 2: R05).
- A general run token for every transition (R04).

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Which worker may end a job.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send
