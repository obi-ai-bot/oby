# test(obid): merging_39 and merging_40 wait for the card of the round's new head

<!-- Roadmap R01-15 · Sprint 2 · R01 Safety net · effort S · risk low · new 2026-11-13 from R01-13's flaky list -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

Two merge tests stop failing when the Mac is busy, and come off the flaky list.

### Status and scope

Proposed (new, R01-13's flaky list).

**Why:** both tests are on the flaky list in `core/.config/nextest.toml` (R01-13). Each takes the first open merge card that matches, and under load that can still be the card of the pull request's earlier head, before the round's new push replaced it:
- `merge_api` `merging_39_a_second_red_head_asks_the_owner`: `red_card` (merge_api.rs:2019) takes the first card saying "Its checks failed on GitHub."; the test then asserts it names the new head (merge_api.rs:2108).
- `merge_api` `merging_40_a_members_change_request_starts_one_round`: `changes_card` (merge_api.rs:2243) takes the first change-request card; the test then asserts it names the pushed head (merge_api.rs:2310).

Evidence (factory job logs on the factory's Mac, `factory/jobs/`):
- merging_39 failed at merge_api.rs:2105 in the gates of R02-06 (job 008, round-1/checks.log) and R01-07 (job 009, round-2/merge-checks.log), and in R03-01's (job 010, build.md), none of which touched it; R02-06's builder showed it fails the same way on `origin/dev` under load (job 008, build.md).
- merging_40 failed under load on R02-06's branch and on a clean `origin/dev` (job 008, build.md), and in R03-01's run of the plan, passing 6 of 7 runs alone (job 010, build.md).

**In:**
- Wait for the card whose id names the round's new head (`within` on that id), not for the first card of its kind.
- Remove both entries from `core/.config/nextest.toml` in the same pull request.

**Out:**
- Other merge tests, and any change to obid's merge code.
