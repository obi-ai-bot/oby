## What
W01-13 (#2854) gave each artifact card a picture of its latest version, taken in main in a never-shown view of the preview's locked session (`main/artifactPreview.ts`). Its round-1 reviews (factory/jobs/012-w01-13-an-artifact-s-card/round-1/review-security.md, review-ux.md, Notes) found three things:

1. **A card can get another artifact's picture.** The 10-second limit answers `{ none: true }` but doesn't stop the slow capture; the next capture then starts on the same view, and the slow one can finish into the wrong answer.
2. **Slow.** Each picture waits a fixed 4 s, one at a time: the last of 25 cards waits ~100 s. Pictures are still taken for versions no card shows any more.
3. **Can't be read.** The page is drawn 1280 px wide and shrunk to the 360 px card, so body text is ~4 px.

## How
1. Every capture carries its key (`<id>/<version>`). After a timeout the view is closed and a fresh one made (same `makeView`, same locked session and relay) before the next capture; a capture whose key isn't the one being answered is dropped. Test: a slow first capture never answers the second ask.
2. Wait for the preview's own "drawn" signal if `preview.html` has one (Sandpack's done), with the 4 s as the ceiling, not the floor. Drop a queued ask whose card asked for a newer version since.
3. Draw at 640x185 CSS px (the card's ratio, half the width) and capture at device scale 2 (`setZoomFactor`/`webContents` emulation or a 1280x370 capture of a 2x zoomed page), so the picture is 1280x370 pixels and its text is twice today's size. Keep `object-fit: cover; object-position: top left`.
