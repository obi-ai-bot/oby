## What
Now and then, a call to one of Oby's tools from a conversation waits for obid's whole 5-second reader cap instead of answering at once. `a_call_waits_for_the_reader_and_sees_the_read_before_it` (`core/crates/obid/tests/conversation_harness.rs:1476-1492`, #1686) checks that the call finishes in under 4 s. On `origin/dev` it usually takes about 0.43 s, but on 2026-11-15 one run on the gate VM took 6.13 s with no load and failed. The same wait failed A00-70's gate twice: 6.35 s and 4.39 s, on Linux shard 3/4. On this Mac it took 0.4 to 0.5 s in 12 runs. So the reader sometimes doesn't record the agent's line before the call is judged, and the call only goes ahead when the cap runs out.

## Why
- **The owner waits.** A tool call that waits out the cap holds the owner's conversation for 5 seconds for no reason.
- **Merges stop.** The test fails at random and blocks merges for every pull request. Under CI-26 to 28 it goes on the flaky list (`core/.config/nextest.toml`) in the same change as this task, and this fix takes it off again.

## How
1. Find why the call's line isn't seen in time. Look at the order between obid's reader of the agent's output and the tool call that arrives on its own channel: a line read but not yet recorded, a notification sent before the record, a wake-up that can be missed, or a Linux-only timing difference. T3 Code orders this by serialising writes through one commit queue and awaiting the previous event (`apps/server/src/orchestration-v2/ProviderCommitBatch.ts` `consume` and `barrier`, `EventSink.ts` `commitThenPublish`), never by waiting on a timer.
2. Fix it so a call waits only until the reader has handled the call's own line, and is woken the moment that happens. The 5 s cap stays only as a guard for a line that never comes.
3. Take the test off the flaky list in the same change.

Acceptance:
- **No slow calls on the gate VM.** `a_call_waits_for_the_reader_and_sees_the_read_before_it` passes in under 1 s in 30 runs in a row there, through `factory/bin/test-run`, and on this Mac.
- **A new test reproduces the race.** It holds the reader between reading and recording the line, and shows the call waits for the record and then goes ahead, not waiting out the cap. It fails on the code before the fix.
- **The flaky entry is gone** from `core/.config/nextest.toml`.
