Contract: docs/contracts/obysphere.md ("Out of scope: … inviting people …: Sangam's Create and Invite screens stay on the mock").

What. On a real Obysphere (one joined by an invite link), two controls in the window pretend:
- **Invite member** (the Obysphere panel, `Sidebar.tsx`, the button that opens `InviteModal`) shows for every Obysphere but the sample. Its link is a made-up code (`InviteModal.tsx`: `Math.random().toString(36)`), and "Send invitation" only adds the emails to the window's memory (`spheres.ts`, `invite`) and says they were sent. Nothing reaches the Obysphere: invitations are its admin's, on its server.
- **Leave <name>** in the Obysphere's ⋮ menu only closes the menu (`Sidebar.tsx`, its own comment: "Leave does nothing yet here, Settings' does").

Why. The window tells the owner an invitation went out, or that a menu item will leave, when nothing happened. A teammate waits for an invitation that was never sent.

How. Keep Sangam's controls and words. Invite member shows only on the mock (with `?everything`) and on the sample, as the contract says, until obid can invite (a question for the owner: Dmytro's Obysphere server makes invitations today). Leave in the ⋮ menu opens the same confirmation and runs the same leave as Settings › Obysphere's Leave (`SphereSettings.tsx`, main's `leave`), not a copy of it.
