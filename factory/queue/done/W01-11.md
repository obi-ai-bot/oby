## What
An artifact shows in its conversation, but not where the boards draw it, and not with their icon, their words or their side-panel row. Read from the code on `dev` (verify each before relying on it):

- **Where the card goes.** `ChatView.tsx` puts a task's artifact under the task's card (`art-cards`, in the `jobs` map of each message), and the conversation's own artifacts (`oby_artifact`), and any whose task isn't shown, in a row of their own after the last turn (`ownArtifacts`). Board 1a puts the card inside Oby's message that tells of it: in its `msg-end`, after its `msg-bubble` ("Codex made it: a canvas of eight boards…"), before its `msg-time`. `docs/contracts/artifacts-app.md` says "under the task's card" (Outcome, "The card" under Triggers and states, and ARTIFACTSAPP-4's first bullet): the contract disagrees with the board, and the board wins.
- **What the window knows.** obid already says which turn made what. Oby's turn carries `artifacts: [{id, version}]` for what its `oby_artifact` published (`Turn::artifacts` in `core/crates/obid/src/conversation.rs`; `docs/api/conversations.md`, the `oby_artifact` line). A job's report turn has `ended: true` and `jobs: [<job>]` (`job_ended`, same file), and an artifact a task made has that job in `job` (`GET /v1/artifacts`). The app's `Turn` (`shared/obid-wire.ts`) has no `artifacts`, and `chatOf` (`renderer/src/live.ts`) carries neither it nor which job a report is about onto the `Message` (`renderer/src/view.ts`).
- **The card's face.** `artifacts/Card.tsx` draws lucide `Shapes` at 16 and `ChevronRight` at 16. Board 1a's `art-card-row` draws the path `M22 6H2M22 18H2M6 2v20M18 2v20` at 16, which is lucide `Frame`, and the chevron `m9 18 6-6-6-6` at 14. The meta line already matches the board's form: `Oby artifact · Canvas · v3 · made by Codex`.
- **The kind's word.** `kindName` in `artifacts/store.ts` says `Picture` for `image`. The boards say `Image` (Main.dc.html's kinds: `Canvas`, `Page`, `Document`, `Image · PDF`); `Picture` is on no board.
- **The side panel.** `Sidebar.tsx` nests only a conversation's tasks (`convo-kids`, `kidsOf` in `nested.ts`). Board 1a nests, after the task's row, a row for the artifact made there: `<button class="convo-kid art-kid {{kidArtOn}}" title="Oby Artifacts, made in this conversation">`, an `art-kid-icon` holding Frame at 14, `<span class="convo-kid-text"><b>Oby Artifacts</b> · Canvas</span>`, and a New dot (`<span class="status" role="img" aria-label="New"><span class="status-dot is-new"></span></span>`). `kidArtOn` is `is-on` while the artifact is open (board 1b). Its click opens the artifact (`goArtifact`). The board's own `artifacts.css` styles it with `.art-kid-icon` and `.convo-kid.art-kid.is-on`, which the app's `artifacts/artifacts.css` doesn't have; the `.convo-kid` rules in the app's `app.css` already match the board's.

## Why
The card is how the owner finds what an agent made, and the boards draw it as part of what Oby says, not as an attachment to a task. Today the words and icon differ, and the side panel has no row at all, so an artifact is lost once its conversation scrolls. This is the board-matching half of the Artifacts loop: the pane's own chrome (board 1b's tab strip, full width) is a later task and stays out.

2. **Place each card once**, in this order:

5. **The row under its conversation** (`Sidebar.tsx`, `artifacts/store.ts`, `artifacts/artifacts.css`):

   - It has `is-on` while that artifact is open beside the conversation.
   - A conversation with an artifact and no task gets the twist and the group too.
   - A click opens the conversation and the artifact beside it, on its latest version, as the card's click does. Full width (board 1b's `goArtifact` sets `full`) is the pane task's, not this one.
   - The New dot, exactly as the board's markup, shows for an artifact made or given a new version while the window runs, until the owner opens it. It is the window's own state, as `open` is in `store.ts`: nothing is kept in obid, and after a restart nothing is New. On the board the dot follows a reply to a share, and sharing is parked; say so in the contract.
   - Read the artifacts once with `GET /v1/artifacts` (no filter: every artifact, `docs/api/artifacts.md`), grouped by `conversation`, and again on each `artifact` event (`useArtifactsSeen`). Never one request per row. None on the mock.
   - Copy the board's `.art-kid-icon` and `.convo-kid.art-kid.is-on` rules into `artifacts/artifacts.css` as the board's `artifacts.css` has them.
