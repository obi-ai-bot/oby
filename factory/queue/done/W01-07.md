## What
Today the only check of the real window against the browser boards is obid-spec's `app.*` workflows (`tools/obid-spec/workflows/app/browser-01..17.json`, written by `apps/electron/scripts/browser-workflows.py`). Their Rust nodes (`tools/obid-spec/src/app.rs`) pipe JSON lines into `apps/electron/scripts/drive-app.mjs`, which launches the built app hidden on its demo data and compares it with a board through `apps/electron/scripts/board-compare.mjs`. Running that needs obid-spec's cargo build.

This task adds a plain node script, `apps/electron/scripts/boards.mjs`, that does the same for all 17 browser boards with no Blok and no Rust:

- it launches the built app hidden, once per board, at the board's demo query, size and storage;
- it compares the window with the board, every held piece within tolerance and word for word;
- it writes a result JSON and a side-by-side PNG per board;
- it exits 1 naming the board, the piece, the pixels and both sets of words.

Run it as `node scripts/boards.mjs <out-folder> [board numbers…]` from `apps/electron`.

Read on `dev` (each checked):

- `drive-app.mjs` can't be imported. Its read loop runs at module top level (`for await (const line of lines)`, lines 264 to 278) and ends in `process.exit(0)` (line 279). So `boards.mjs` spawns it unchanged as a child and speaks its JSON lines, one request in and one answer out, as `tools/obid-spec/lab-ui/browser/scripts/check.mjs` spawns `drive-lab.mjs` (its `spawn` and `ask`, lines 25 to 27).
- One helper launches one app (`launch` refuses a second: "this helper already launched its app"), so `boards.mjs` spawns one helper per board and ends each with `close`.
- `launch` needs absolute `scratch` and `userData`, and `obid.port` and `obid.home`; it refuses 7777. With `mock`, it opens `?everything&chat&<query>`.
- `board-compare.mjs` fixes the design folder: `DESIGN` (line 12) is `tools/obid-spec/lab-ui/browser/design`. It is also the board page's `<base href>` (line 36), from which `oby.css` and `blobs/` load.
- `judge()` passes when nothing was compared. If no held piece exists on either side, nothing is pushed to `off`, `counts` or `words`, so `within` and `sameWords` are both true with `compared` 0 (lines 86 to 106).
- 13 boards have a compare today, and 8 of those carry a skip list (`BOARD_SKIPS` in `browser-workflows.py`, line 666: boards 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 11, 12 and 14). Boards 9, 10, 13 and 15 have no compare step at all, only a side-by-side picture. Every compare uses the default tolerance of 4 px and is asked again for up to 10 s (`within=10`).
- Four boards need a step before their picture. Board 1 hovers the globe ("Show the browser, 2 tabs, Codex is using one": drive-app's `click` with `hover: true`) before it compares Closed. Board 6 clicks "Give back" before it compares Hold's `gaveBack` state. Board 10's picture is of the crashed page: it clicks the sixth tab (`{"css": ".br-tab", "nth": 5}`) and its picture waits for `[data-browser=page-crashed]`. Board 13 presses `Meta+Comma`, then clicks the `.sidebar-item` with "General", and its picture waits for `[data-browser=settings] .set-head` (the workflow's `read` with `within=10`).
- The workflows' extra compares after a click (board 1's "now-board-2" against Main, board 7's "now-board-8" against Secret) repeat boards 2 and 8, so `browser.json` leaves them out.

## Why
The boards are the source of truth for what a person sees. A window task needs a check it can run on a Mac, or on a macOS runner later, without building obid-spec: a later task's macOS CI job runs this script. Today's compare can also pass while checking nothing, and the skip lists sit in a generator script nobody reads.

Browser boards only. The Artifacts boards are a later task: they draw their screens from the board's own script (sc-for lists and screen flags set by its logic), which `boardFile()` doesn't run, and the app has no Artifacts demo data to launch at.

## How
1. **`apps/electron/scripts/boards/browser.json`.** One entry per board, 17 in all, in board order:
   - `n`, `board` (the file's name), `query` (`browser=<n>`), `size`, `storage` (`oby.chatPanelOpen`, "1" on boards 3 and 13, "0" elsewhere, as `launch()` in `browser-workflows.py` sets it);
   - `checks`: each with an optional `state`, `skip` list, `tolerance` (4) and `before` steps. `before` holds only drive-app's `click` (with `hover` where board 1 needs it), `press` and `type` requests, sent as they are written, plus a `wait`: a drive-app `read` target that `boards.mjs` asks again for up to 10 s until its `count` is at least 1;
   - board 6 has two checks, `held`, then `gaveBack` after its click;
   - boards 9, 10, 13 and 15 have no check: a `gap` line saying why (for example board 9 is a sheet of four cards), and for boards 10 and 13 the `before` steps their picture needs (above). They get only their picture.

   Copy the skip lists from `BOARD_SKIPS` as they are today, so the first run is green. Copy the launch values from `browser-workflows.py`'s `launch()` calls. Every skipped piece, and every board with no check, shows in the result and in the summary as a known gap, never hidden.
2. **`board-compare.mjs`.** Two changes:
   - `DESIGN` honours `OBY_BOARDS_DIR`, read when the module loads. It names the whole design folder (`boards/`, `oby.css`, `blobs/`), not only the boards.
   - `judge()` fails when nothing was compared: with `compared` 0, `within` is false, and the result says nothing was compared. Nothing else in `judge()`, `PIECES` or `TODAY` changes. obid-spec's `app.compare` (through drive-app.mjs) gets the same `judge()`: say so in `build.md`. If a board of today compares nothing on the real run, that is a finding: stop and report it to the orchestrator; never add a skip or drop its check to turn it green.
3. **`boards.mjs`.**
   - **Before launching:** it checks that `out/main/index.js`, `out/renderer/index.html` and drive-lib's `electronBinary` exist and that the build is newer than every file under `src/`. If not, it exits 1 saying to run `npm ci` and `npm run build` in `apps/electron`.
   - **Port and home:** it takes a free port on 127.0.0.1 with nothing listening, as `idle_port()` in `app.rs` does. `OBY_BOARDS_PORT` may name one. That port must not be 7777 and nothing may listen on it; otherwise it exits 1 saying so and what to do. The scratch home is a new folder under the system's temporary folder: `userData`, `no-obid` (obid's home), and drive-app's `boards/`.
   - **Per board:** spawn `drive-app.mjs`, then `launch` with `mock: true`, the board's query, size and storage. Then each check's `before` steps, then `compare` (asked again for up to 10 s until it holds, as the workflows do), then `sidebyside`, then `close`.
   - **Writes:** `<out>/<nn>-<Board>[-<state>].json` (the judge's result, or the board's `gap` where it has no check, the skips and the page errors `close` returned) and `<out>/<nn>-<Board>[-<state>]-beside.png`, plus `<out>/result.json` for the run.
   - **Exits 0** when every check holds. **Exits 1** printing one line per miss: the board's number and name; the piece (`.br-control-text[0]`); how many pixels off, with both boxes; or both sets of words; or both counts; or "nothing compared".
   - **Cleans up:** the scratch home is removed when the run passes and kept, with its path printed, when it fails.
   - **Per board, every request:** `spawn` inherits `process.env`, so `OBY_BOARDS_DIR` reaches the child's `board-compare.mjs`. The `compare` retry asks again on a miss (`within` or `sameWords` false) and keeps the last answer.
   - **Pure parts are exported:** reading `browser.json`, the readiness check, and the lines a miss prints. The readiness check takes the app's folder and the port as arguments (it defaults to `appRoot` from drive-lib), so its test builds a scratch folder with its own `out/` and `src/` and never touches the real build. The script runs only when it is the program node started (`import.meta.url` against `process.argv[1]`), so `boards.test.mjs` can import it.
4. **`apps/electron/scripts/boards.test.mjs`** (node's own `node:test`, no new dependency). vitest doesn't collect it: its root is `src/renderer` (`vite.config.ts`, line 58). Run it as `node --test scripts/boards.test.mjs` from `apps/electron`. It sets `OBY_BOARDS_DIR` before importing `board-compare.mjs`, through a dynamic `import()`.
5. **`docs/contracts/boards.md`**, from `docs/business-contract-template.md`: what the script runs, what it reads, what it writes, its known gaps (the skip lists and the four boards with no check), and BOARDS-1 to 5 as `- **BOARDS-n**` lines, each a given/when/then:
   - **BOARDS-1:** given a built app, when `boards.mjs` runs, then every one of the 17 boards in `browser.json` is launched and pictured, every check is judged, and it exits 0 with today's skips listed.
   - **BOARDS-2:** given a board whose word differs from the window's in a held piece, when it runs, then it exits 1 naming the board, the piece and both words.
   - **BOARDS-3:** given a held piece more than its tolerance off, when it runs, then it exits 1 naming the board, the piece, the pixels and both boxes.
   - **BOARDS-4:** given a check where no held piece is on either side, when it is judged, then it fails as "nothing compared" instead of passing.
   - **BOARDS-5:** given no build, a build older than `src/`, port 7777 or a port something listens on, when it starts, then it exits 1 before launching anything, saying what to do.

   It needs no line in `tools/obid-spec/scope.json`. The coverage gate reads only the contracts `scope.json` lists, and counts the others as unread (`tools/obid-spec/src/coverage.rs`: the `contracts_unread` count at the end of `report`). `gate_ok` fails only on unclassified scenarios of listed contracts.
