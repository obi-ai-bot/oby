# feat(electron): who may ask whom in Settings › Obysphere, with one click to let members ask

<!-- Roadmap R10-07 · Sprint 7 · R10 Finish the Obysphere client · effort M · risk low -->

### Sangam's screen

needs Sangam's design first (no policy screen in apps/electron/src/renderer/src/SphereSettings.tsx or design/app/src/renderer/src/SphereSettings.tsx). Engine-only part: R10-06.

### Outcome

An admin sees the Sphere's rules in plain words and turns on 'members may ask each other's Obys' with one click; a member sees them read-only. The ask-an-oby promise works on a fresh Sphere.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R10-07, milestone R10 Finish the Obysphere client).

**Why:** SphereSettings.tsx has 'What your Oby tells' (line ~181) but nothing about the Sphere's policy; the mock's Create and Invite screens never write one. Every first question on a new Sphere fails with 'This Obysphere's rules don't allow questions yet' (ASK-8).

**In:**
- A rules section in SphereSettings.tsx: each rule as words, Remove and the starter rule for an admin.
- Members read the same list, read-only.
- When a question is refused `policy_denied` and the owner is the admin, the refusal offers the same one click.

**Out:**
- A full rule editor per person and repository (later, #1220).
- Creating a Sphere or inviting (server routes missing).

### Acceptance scenarios

- POLICY-5 (proposed): Given an admin with policy `[]`, when they open Settings › Obysphere, then the rules say nobody may ask yet and offer 'Let members ask', which writes the starter rule.
- POLICY-6 (proposed): Given a member who is not an admin, when they open it, then they read the rules and have no control.
- POLICY-7 (proposed): Given an admin whose question was refused `policy_denied`, when the refusal shows, then it offers the same one click, and after it the owner can ask again.
