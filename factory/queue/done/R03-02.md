# chore(cloud): delete the control plane's sign-in, installation, tunnel and hook routes; keep the push relay

<!-- Roadmap R03-02 · Sprint 1 · R03 Delete what has no caller · effort S · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

The deployed Worker serves only the iPhone push relay and its health check. A public webhook endpoint, a sealed store with no reader, the tunnel sweep and the sign-in flow leave the code and, after the owner deploys, the internet.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R03-02, milestone R03 Delete what has no caller).

**Why:** Only two callers exist. obid posts to `/v1/push` (core/crates/obid/src/push.rs:1318) and the iPhone registers at `/v1/push/devices` (apps/ios/app/Notifications.swift:232, 252). Searched core/crates/obid/src, apps/electron/src, apps/ios, apps/shared, apps/watch, apps/android (pushes go by FCM through obid, apps/android/README.md:27), apps/web/src, mcp/ and install/ for `/v1/auth`, `/v1/installations`, `queue-key`, `/h/`: no caller. The Swift Mac app that signed in and registered installations was deleted (#1860); obid's tunnel and hooks went in #1510 and #1446. app.ts:236-262 still routes sign-in, installations, endpoints, hooks and the hook queue, and index.ts runs a minute sweep that asks Cloudflare about tunnels. docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:85 still lists webhook senders as an input.

**In:**
- cloud/control-plane/src/app.ts: remove startSignIn, finishSignIn, register, showSelf, forget, createEndpoint, removeEndpoint, endpointToken, createHook, listHooks, removeHook, deliver, keep, seal, setQueueKey, keptItems, removeKept, signed, spend and their constants (PER_ACCOUNT, PER_DOMAIN, HOOK_*, QUEUE*, SIGNED_PREFIX, sweep timings). Keep /v1/health, registerPhone, forgetPhone, push and their limits.
- Make `sweep` clear only ended rate-limit windows (the push relay's and the day-old ones); index.ts keeps the cron for it and loses the Cloudflare tunnel and Apple/Google key dependencies.
- Delete src/signin.ts, src/cloudflare.ts, src/suffixes.ts and scripts/suffixes.mjs; trim src/tokens.ts and src/env.ts to what push uses (addressBucket, networkBucket, base64, randomBytes, newId).
- Delete test/endpoint.test.ts, hooks.test.ts, hook-queue.test.ts, installations.test.ts, signin.test.ts, members.test.ts, signatures.test.ts and the unused parts of test/helpers.ts; keep test/push.test.ts.
- new: migrations/0009_drop_retired_tables.sql: DROP TABLE hook_queue, hooks, endpoints, nonces, installations, sign_ins, users (in that order, for the foreign keys). Keep rate_limits, push_devices, push_device_count, apns_provider_token.
- wrangler.jsonc: remove the sign-in, domain and tunnel vars that no code reads.
- docs/remote-access.md: keep 'Pushes' and its setup; reduce the instance, sign-in and tunnel sections to one note that they were removed (git history keeps them). cloud/control-plane/README.md likewise. docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:85: remove the webhook row.
- The deploy runbook in the PR body (the owner's steps below).

**Out:**
- Deploying, migrating the remote D1 and removing Worker secrets: the owner's step (AGENTS.md 'Build and run').
- Moving the push relay off Deskree's default instance (push.rs:78, config.rs:291): a separate decision.
- apps/shared/RemoteRoute.swift and the Android hostname test, which still know tunnel hostnames: R03-04 or a follow-up, after checking that nothing routes by them.
- Sangam's mock Settings › Remote access (apps/electron/src/renderer/src/SettingsView.tsx MockRemote, shown only with ?everything): his design, unchanged.
- A signed webhook later is built on an outbound socket, not on this tunnel forward; #1315's sealed-queue design stays in git history and the issue.
