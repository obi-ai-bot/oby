## What
`merge_api::merging_40_rounds_stay_within_reviews` (core/crates/obid/tests/merge_api.rs) has the same wrong-card race R01-15 (#2206) fixed in merging_39 and merging_40_a_members_change_request_starts_one_round: it can read the card for the pull request's earlier head instead of the new round's. R01-15's builder measured it under load (four copies at once): it failed 15 of 20 runs on the branch and 3 of 4 on origin/dev. It isn't on the flaky list (core/.config/nextest.toml), so a busy gate can fail on it.

## Why
A test that fails for the timing, not the change, costs a whole gate and reads as the change's fault.

## How
- Apply R01-15's fix: wait until the remote's branch has moved past the old head, read the new head from there, then wait for the open card of its kind whose id names that head, through the existing `red_card` / `changes_card` helpers. No sleep added, no assertion removed.
- Prove it under the same load: 20 runs in batches of four copies at once, 20 of 20 passing; and show it failing on origin/dev under that load first.
- Change no obid production code. Depends on R01-15 (its helper changes).
