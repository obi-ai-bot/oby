Found by A00-58 (PR #2328, factory/jobs/153-a00-58-receipts-15-and-recovery/build.md): obid's `start_review` can start a job's second review twice. Under forced delays both publish_api recovery_15 second-review tests fail with `left: 3` / `right: 2` (three review passes where two are expected), and on a loaded Mac (load 61 to 85) the fixed tests still failed once in 20 runs on the same double start, at publish_api.rs:3740. A00-58 fixed only the tests' waits; this is the product race.

Why it matters: a second review that starts twice runs a second reviewer on the owner's quota and can record a duplicate verdict on the same head, which the merge rules (two approving reviews by different tools, MERGING-13 to 16) count.

What to build: find the race in start_review (two callers seeing "no second review yet" before either records it) and make starting a second review once per head atomic, with a test that forces the interleaving and fails without the fix, and the two recovery_15 tests passing 20 of 20 on a loaded Mac.
