## What
Board 1 draws the globe pointed at: under it, the tip **Show the browser** with **⇧⌘B**. The board check from W01-07 (`apps/electron/scripts/boards.mjs`) found the app's tip has the board's words but sits 20 px left of the board's. The app's tip ends 28 px from the window's right edge, and the board's ends 8 px from it. On W01-07's run, at 1280x800, the app's tip was at x 1094 and the board's at x 1114, both 158 px wide. W01-07 holds `.tip-under` on board 1 alone (`HELD` in `board-compare.mjs`) and leaves it red with no skip. obid-spec's `tools/obid-spec/workflows/app/browser-01-closed.json` fails its `matches-board` step the same way.

Read from the code on `dev` (verify each before relying on it):

- `BrowserGlobe` in `renderer/src/browser/BrowserPane.tsx` wraps the closed globe and its tip in a `div` with `position: relative`. `.tip-under` in `renderer/src/browser/browser.css` is `position: absolute; top: 44px; right: 8px`. So the app measures the tip's 8 px from the globe's own right edge.
- The board puts the same `.tip-under` (the same rule, in the board's `<style>`) inside `<div class="bar-end" style="position: relative">`. So the board measures 8 px from the bar's end, which is the window's right edge.
- The app's `.bar-end` (`renderer/src/app.css`) has `padding-right: var(--gutter)` (20 px). That puts the globe's wrapper, and so the tip's anchor, 20 px in from the edge. That's the 20 px.
- The globe's blue dot (`.bar-end .icon-button .badge`, `position: absolute` in `browser.css`) is placed against the same wrapper. The app's `.icon-button` isn't positioned (`app.css`), unlike the board's `oby.css`, which sets `.bar-end .icon-button { position: relative; }`. So a fix that only takes `position: relative` off the wrapper moves the dot too.

## Why
Board 1 is the first thing the owner sees of the browser. W01-07 holds the window to the boards with no skip lists, and this tip is the one piece on board 1 it finds off. Until it moves, a full `boards.mjs` run exits 1, and so does obid-spec's board 1 workflow, on a known red.

## How
Wait for W01-07 to merge into `dev`: `boards.mjs`, `HELD` and `docs/contracts/boards.md` arrive with it. Run `node scripts/boards.mjs <out> 1` in `apps/electron` on `dev` first, and keep the failing line (`board 1 Closed: .tip-under[0] 20px off: …`).

Then anchor the tip as the board does, 8 px from the bar's end, inside `renderer/src/browser/**`:

- One way: give the closed globe's `.icon-button` its own `position: relative` (as the board's `oby.css` does, so the dot stays on the button). Then the tip's containing block is the bar's end, not the globe's wrapper.
- Another way: offset the tip by the bar's padding, `right: calc(8px - var(--gutter))`.

Choose the one that keeps the dot and the open globe where they are, and say which in `build.md`.

The tip shows only on board 1 (the panel closed, the globe pointed at). Every other board must stay exactly as it is.

Update `docs/contracts/boards.md`'s board 1 line in the same change. It says the tip's place doesn't match yet and that a full run exits 1 on it. After this task, both are false: board 1 holds the tip, and it matches.
