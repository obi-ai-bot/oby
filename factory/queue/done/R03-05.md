### Outcome

The owner decides whether oby-mcp goes. On yes, a stale MCP server that reads a deleted app's settings and calls a route obid does not serve leaves the repository, with its Swift package and its check branch.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R03-05, milestone R03 Delete what has no caller).

**Why:** mcp/server/RunnerAPI.swift:27-41 reads the deleted Swift Mac app's `runnerURL` default (com.deskree.oby) and talks of Tailscale (:11); Server.swift:279 calls POST /v1/jobs/{id}/start-when, which obid does not route (api.rs). Only 6 commits ever touched mcp/. Searched apps/electron (scripts and src), install/, README.md, docs/ and scripts/: nothing starts or ships oby-mcp; only Package.swift builds it, scripts/oby-check.sh:30, 335 and 457 test it, docs/mcp.md describes it, and tools/brain/brain.config.ts:129 scans it. obid's own MCP server for conversations and agents (brain, /v1/agent/mcp) is separate and stays.

**In:**
- Step 1: ask the owner on the issue: retire oby-mcp now, or keep it and fix its discovery. Record the answer in docs/trim.md.
- On yes: delete mcp/, Package.swift and docs/mcp.md; remove the `mcp/* | Package.swift` branch and its swift test step from scripts/oby-check.sh and scripts/oby-check-test.sh; remove the swift-tests node's mcp part from tools/oby-check-blok/workflows/oby-check.json; remove 'mcp' from the Brain's hostScan; fix README.md:60, docs/ci.md:11 and docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:23, 49.
- On no: replace RunnerAPI.url's `runnerURL` read with obid's home token file and 127.0.0.1:7777 only, drop the Tailscale text and the start-when call, and fix docs/mcp.md's merge wording (#1140).

**Out:**
- An `oby mcp` subcommand or the `oby` CLI itself: R12.
