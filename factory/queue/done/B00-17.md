## What
`factory/bin/job reject <task>` only works on a job in `needs-info` or `needs-human`, and `job advance <id> needs-human` refuses a job whose last step went well (a READY build, a passed gate, an APPROVE). So a job the owner decides to drop while it's in `intake`, `spec`, `build`, `gate`, `prove`, `review` or `approve` can't be dropped at all. On 2026-11-16 the owner closed six such jobs in a triage of open PRs (R12-01, R12-10, R09-03, R04-10, A00-48, A00-62); the script refused every one.

## Why
The owner decides what stays on the roadmap, at any time ("Defaults: Oby forbids nothing" in AGENTS.md). Today the only way around the refusal is closing the PR by hand, which leaves the job looking in progress, so a later `/factory run` would resume it.

## How
- `job reject <task>` accepts a job in any stage but `merged` and `rejected`, with the owner's reason on stdin as today.
- It does what it does today: saves `rejection.md`, closes the PR with the reason, moves the job to Done, and leaves the task file in `factory/queue/tasks`.
- A job in `queued` (GitHub's merge queue holds its PR) is refused with a message saying to take the PR out of the queue first.
- `job queue` never lists a rejected job as `resume`.
- The factory skill and README say a job can be rejected at any stage.

Acceptance:
- A job in each of `build`, `gate`, `prove`, `review` and `approve` is rejected: `rejection.md` is written, the job is Done, its task file stays, and the PR close is asked of GitHub once.
- A `queued` job is refused, and nothing changes.
- A `merged` or `rejected` job is refused, as today.
- Tests use a stand-in `gh`, with no network.
