# feat(obid): an owner id that looks like another member's waits for a Confirm that names both

<!-- Roadmap R10-08 · Sprint 7 · R10 Finish the Obysphere client · effort S · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

uses an existing screen: the Confirm card on Home (apps/electron/src/renderer/src/AskStack.tsx)

### Outcome

A member who registered `dirna` beside `dima` cannot receive the owner's question or review by a near-miss: obid shows both ids before anything goes, and the apps can mark the look-alike.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R10-08, milestone R10 Finish the Obysphere client).

**Why:** Member identity is self-claimed (#1613): the first to claim an owner id owns it, and an ASCII look-alike passes (peer-thread.md 'Who': `dirna` for `dima` 'still passes'). ASK-17 lets an exact owner id go with no Confirm when no other card claims the same id, so a look-alike is never checked. A full fix (invites that vouch) needs the server and is out of R10.

**In:**
- A confusable check in the sphere client: same skeleton after folding common look-alikes (rn/m, l/1/I, 0/o, case, separators) or edit distance 1, against every other member and pinned owner.
- Add-only `lookalike: [<owner id>]` on GET /v1/obid/sphere/members and on a peer thread.
- oby_ask and POST /v1/obid/sphere/requests to a look-alike: the Confirm names both ids, as ASK-17 does for a shared claim.

**Out:**
- Server-side vouching, named invites only, admin confirmation (server work, Dmytro's).
- The peer thread ask: it is the owner's own signed act on an exact id; the app shows the mark (R10-10).

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- The confusable rule, written once and used by asking.rs and the members route.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- The Confirm card already shows its title. The `lookalike` key is new data for R10-09.

### Interfaces and dependencies

- Add `lookalike` (list of owner ids) to GET /v1/obid/sphere/members items and to GET /v1/obid/sphere/peers/{owner}.
