# fix(obid,docs): the phone allowlist and runner-api.md name only the routes obid serves

<!-- Roadmap R03-03 · Sprint 1 · R03 Delete what has no caller · effort S · risk low -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

A phone call to a route obid does not have is refused at the gateway, and runner-api.md no longer promises a speech service, a Watch API or Live Activity pushes that obid does not have. A Brain test keeps it that way.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R03-03, milestone R03 Delete what has no caller).

**Why:** The gateway allowlist (core/crates/obid/src/gateway.rs:68-152) lets through 7 entries that api.rs does not route: POST /v1/jobs/*/proposals/*/file (:103), POST /v1/jobs/*/start-when (:118), PUT /v1/settings/models (:137; api.rs:225 has GET only), POST /v1/activities (:147), GET and POST /v1/watches and DELETE /v1/watches/* (:149-151), checked against api.rs on origin/dev 56c6083f. runner-api.md still documents POST /v1/activities (:1204), POST /v1/speech (:1383), /v1/watches (:1433), 'The Watch API' (:1451-1573) and start-when. No Swift file calls /v1/speech any more (searched apps/shared, apps/ios, apps/watch); speech runs on the iPhone (apps/ios/app/PhoneSpeech.swift). #1653 lists this drift and its fix.

**In:**
- Remove the 7 allowlist entries from gateway.rs and the `/v1/speech` mention in its refused-routes comment (:66).
- runner-api.md: mark each unserved route as gone, with the date and one line (as #1653 asks, not a silent drop): POST /v1/speech (both sections), POST /v1/activities, /v1/watches, The Watch API, POST /v1/jobs/{id}/start-when, POST /v1/jobs/{id}/proposals/{id}/file, PUT /v1/settings/models. Remove the long bodies of those sections.
- Document the served routes #1653 lists as undocumented (GET /callback, POST /v1/agent/mcp/{job}, GET /v1/artifacts/{id}/{version}/{*path}) if they are still missing.
- Fix the Brain matcher misses #1653 names (conversation-<id> routes) in tools/brain/generator.
- A Brain test that fails on any `doc-without-route` or `phone-without-route` check.
- Lower `obid_lines_limit` by the production lines removed.

**Out:**
- The iPhone's own calls to these routes: R03-04 and R03-07.
- Serving any of these routes again: new work, with its own contract.
