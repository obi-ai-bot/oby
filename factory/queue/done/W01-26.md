## What
Recommendations 1 and 2 of reports/w01/research-artifacts-oss.md (problems 1, 2 and 3), checked on `dev`:

1. **Nothing says when a preview has drawn.** Sandpack's static client fires `done` as it sets the frame's `src`, before the page loads, and nothing else (`sandpack-client` 2.19.8, `compile()`). `host.ts` resolves `window.obyDrawn` on the frame's first `load`, or, for a React artifact, on W01-23's `RENDERED` post, sent one `requestAnimationFrame` after React's commit. Tailwind's browser compile and the page's fonts aren't waited for. rAF is suspended in a hidden page, and the picture's view is never shown.
2. **Card pictures come out blank, and the pane flashes white.** `artifactPreview.ts` `take()` waits for `obyDrawn` (at most 4 s), then a fixed 250 ms (`ponytail: a frame just loaded isn't painted yet`), and captures once. `show()` makes the pane's view visible as soon as the host page has the files. `host.ts` removes the last frame at once.
3. **A broken page says nothing.** A plain page that throws, or whose script or stylesheet is refused, shows blank or half-drawn, and the pane says nothing. Main keeps the refused URLs (`refused[]`) and only logs them. A React artifact's `MOUNT` (`react.ts`) replaces the whole body with a `<pre>` on any `window.onerror` or `onUncaughtError`, Recharts' harmless `ResizeObserver loop` error included.
4. **No way to ask for a fix.** The parts exist: words said with the pane open carry `artifact: {id, version}` (ARTIFACTSAPP-7, WORDS-22), and since #2845 the answer is the artifact's next version.

## Why
The owner sees a blank page and can't tell why, nor ask the agent that made it in one click. The blank card pictures are the same missing signal. LlamaCoder solved all three with one bridge script in the preview page (its `architecture.md`: "Errors come back through our own bridge, so 'Try to fix' can send build/runtime/import errors back to the LLM.").

## How
1. **`bridge.js`, after LlamaCoder's** (`Nutlope/llamacoder` at `5d57b70`, `lib/preview/html.ts`: `ERROR_BRIDGE` and `waitForTailwindReady`; MIT, its notice kept at the top: "Copyright (c) 2024 Hassan El Mghari" and the licence text). A new `apps/electron/resources/preview-bridge/bridge.js`, a classic script of about 60 lines. It isn't in `resources/sandpack-vendor/`, whose files the vendor test requires the script to make.
   - **Served:** the relay's `files()` gets `/__csb_oby/bridge.js` (`?raw`, `text/javascript`). The service worker already sends `/__csb…` paths of the preview's origin to the network, and so to the relay.
   - **Put in every page by Sandpack:** `host.ts` passes `externalResources: ['/__csb_oby/bridge.js']` to `SandpackStatic`. The path ends in `.js`: Sandpack picks the tag from the extension (`injectExternalResources`). The tag lands in `<head>` after the console hook and before the page's own scripts.
   - **Errors:**
     - `error` in the capture phase, so a `<script src>`, `<link>` or `<img>` that fails to load is caught too (LlamaCoder listens to bubbling only);
     - `unhandledrejection`;
     - `console.error`, wrapped as LlamaCoder wraps it and kept as context.
     - LibreChat's regex drops `ResizeObserver loop …` (`client/src/lib/rum/redact.ts`: `/^ResizeObserver loop (?:limit exceeded|completed with undelivered notifications)\.?$/`).
     - Each is posted to `parent` as `{ oby: 'error' | 'console-error', message }`, the message cut to 4,000 characters.
   - **Drawn:** on `load`, it awaits `document.fonts.ready`. Then, when the page loads the vendored Tailwind (`script[src$="tailwindcss-browser/index.global.js"]`, the React page's and, since W01-25, a rewritten CDN tag), it waits for LlamaCoder's probe: a `grid rounded-md bg-zinc-50 p-6` element kept at `left: -9999px` until `display` is `grid` and `paddingTop` is `24px`, or 5 s pass. Then, when the page set `window.__obyReact`, it waits for React's commit (`window.__obyRendered()`). Then it posts `{ oby: 'drawn' }`. It polls with `setTimeout` only, never `requestAnimationFrame`: "rAF is fully suspended in hidden tabs".
2. **`react.ts` `MOUNT`:**
   - It sets `window.__obyReact = true` first.
   - After `flushSync(render)` it calls `__obyRendered` in a `setTimeout(0)` (LlamaCoder's), replacing W01-23's rAF and `RENDERED` post.
   - A compile error (sucrase) and a failed `import('/App.js')` still show their `<pre>` (ARTIFACTSAPP-14), now marked `data-oby-error`. The bridge posts a marked `<pre>`'s text as the error on `load`, so a page made of that `<pre>` alone, which runs no script, still reports it.
   - `window.onerror` and `onUncaughtError` no longer replace the body. The bridge reports them.
   - `RENDERED` and `host.ts`'s listener for it go.
3. **`host.ts`'s status:**
   - `window.obyDrawn` becomes `window.obyStatus`: a promise of `{ drawn: true }`, `{ error, console }` or `{ timeout: true }`, whichever comes first, from messages whose `source` is this show's own `frame.contentWindow`, or `timeout` after 4 s.
   - `error` is the first fatal error, and `console` the last ten console errors.
   - The previous frame stays on view until the new one's status settles, then goes, as LlamaCoder keeps the last preview while rebuilding, so a new version doesn't flash white.
   - What the frame posts is the artifact's own code and can be forged. It may only time a picture and give words to show as text and to quote. Main trusts it for nothing else.
4. **Main** (`artifactPreview.ts`, `previewRules.ts`):
   - `previewStatus` decodes the status strictly, as `previewAsk` does: anything else is `{ timeout: true }`.
   - `show()` makes the pane's view visible only once the status settles (drawn, an error, or the 4 s ceiling).
   - Its answer gains `pageError` (add-only: `{ shown: true, pageError?: string }`). That is the error, the console errors after it, and one line per host the preview session refused since this show began ("The page asked for `<host>`: previews have no network."). It comes when any of the three is there.
   - `take()` awaits `obyStatus` in place of `obyDrawn` and the fixed 250 ms. It captures, and when `image.isEmpty()` or a coarse sample of `toBitmap()` is a single colour, it nudges the view and captures again, at most 3 times, 150 ms apart. The nudge is LlamaCoder's `nudgePreviewIframeGeometry` (`lib/preview/repaint.ts`), applied to the view: `setBounds` 1 px narrower, then back. A page that really is one colour keeps its last capture.
   - The picture's view gets `backgroundThrottling: false`, as a never-shown view is a background page.
   - `preload/index.ts` and `Oby.tsx` carry `pageError` in `artifactPreview`'s answer type.
5. **The pane** (`Pane.tsx`, `artifacts.css`):
   - With `pageError`, a strip shows above the view, as `art-refused` does. Its lead line, then the error in monospace (`textContent`, never HTML), at most 10 lines (`line-clamp`, as LlamaCoder's `ErrorMessage`).
   - One button, **Ask to fix**. A click uploads the error as `preview-error.txt` (`uploadFiles`, `POST /v1/attachments`). It then sends the button's own words with `ask()` to the artifact's conversation, which carries `artifact: {id, version}` by itself (ARTIFACTSAPP-7), and the file in `attachments`.
   - The error reaches the agent only as that quoted file, never in the prompt: "From outside" in AGENTS.md, as an imported or Sphere artifact's error is someone else's text. obid already counts a turn with an attachment as having read something untrusted, so the work it dispatches is marked: accepted.
   - While Oby's question waits in that conversation, the button is disabled: `ask()` would send its words as the answer.
   - Nothing is sent without the click: no auto-fix. LlamaCoder's once-per-generation auto-fix (`lib/chat-auto-fix.ts`) is Oby acting on its own, a default the owner hasn't turned on.
6. **Signed words** (`shared/words.ts`, docs/contracts/signed-words.md):
   - The button's message is the owner's, so it goes through the existing signed path. Main signs it only right after the click it saw (`main/words.ts`).
   - **WORDS-24:** `ASK_TO_FIX` is the label and `FIX_WORDS` the `said` it stands for, held by that label alone, as `TRY_AGAIN`/`TRY_AGAIN_LABEL` are. `wordsOf` takes `POST /v1/plans` `{said: FIX_WORDS, conversation, artifact, attachments}` as it does any words. `owners` lets the held label stand for `FIX_WORDS` on `/v1/plans` only.
   - The upload and the send come within `WITHIN_MS` (2 s) of the click.
