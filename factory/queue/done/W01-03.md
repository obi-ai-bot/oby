## Why
Today such a job gets no browser at all (`jobs/reach.rs`), and nothing in obid stops a URL at the box's edge. The owner's agents keep sign-ins in their partitions: work from outside must never share them. The window side of the edge (a box page's own redirects and links) is a later task and depends on this one.
