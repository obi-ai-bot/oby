## What
`obid_lines_limit` in `scripts/oby-check.sh` fails a change when obid's production lines outside the Sphere pass it, and also when they sit more than 1,000 under it. It began at 90,500 on 2026-11-05 and has been raised about 15 times since, each raise a pull request of its own with a `# payback:` line (CI-39 to 41). It stops features rather than shaping them: on 2026-11-16 A00-70 (projects) waited on 83 lines, and the Home tasks would each have waited on a raise.

## Why
The owner, 2026-11-16: "this ceiling makes no sense at all". A count of lines can't tell whether obid is doing the agents' work instead of the dispatcher's, which is what the "Dispatcher, not harness" rule protects. B00-26 replaces it with checks that can.

## How
- On every run of its limits block (the pull request's check, the merge queue's `limits` job (CI-35) and the nightly), the check prints obid's production line count and the change's difference from its base, and never fails on either.
- `obid_lines_limit`, its `# payback:` line and the rule that fails a count far under it go.
- `jobs_rs_limit` stays as it is: `jobs.rs` may only shrink, and a raise of it still needs its own pull request and a payback line. `tests_sleep_limit` and `agents_md_bytes_limit` stay as they are.
- In the same pull request: the AGENTS.md line about `obid_lines_limit` in "Dispatcher, not harness" and its entry in `docs/agents-rules-manifest.md` (CI-42 to 44), `docs/contracts/ci.md` (CI-35, CI-39 to 41), and `scripts/oby-check-test.sh`.

Acceptance:
- A change that takes obid past 102,450 production lines passes the check, and the check's output names obid's count and the change's difference.
- A change that deletes 2,000 lines from obid passes and isn't told to lower anything.
- `jobs.rs` past `jobs_rs_limit` still fails, and raising `jobs_rs_limit` still needs its payback line and a pull request of its own.
- The merge queue's `limits` job no longer fails on obid's total.
- Each rule that stays still has a case in `scripts/oby-check-test.sh` that fails without it; the cases for the rules that go are replaced by cases for the report.
