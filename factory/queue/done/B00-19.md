## What
Of 66 jobs on Oby's factory engine (#2401), the 48 review jobs and most follow-up builds have no `conversation`, so the side panel has nothing to nest them under; only a task's first build and its intake and spec jobs belong to a conversation.

## Why
The owner expects each task's work (its build, follow-ups and reviews) together under that task's thread. Without it, the side panel shows almost nothing of what Oby is doing.

## How
- A follow-up and a review of a job carry the conversation of the job they follow or review.
- The side panel shows a conversation's jobs nested under it, in Sangam's look, and nothing new beyond that.
- Jobs made before this keep what they have.

Acceptance:
- A follow-up of a job in a conversation is in that conversation.
- A review of a job in a conversation is in that conversation.
- A job with no conversation stays as it is.
- The side panel shows a task's follow-ups and reviews under it.
- Tests for the API and the Electron view.
