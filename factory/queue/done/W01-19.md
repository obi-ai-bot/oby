## What
Board 6 is how the owner takes a page back from an agent: Take control, use the page, Give back. Once W01-08 merges, obid holds the tab on the signed take and refuses the agent's acts `human_control`. The window already signs both clicks (`live.ts` posts `…/take` and `…/giveBack`, main signs them by their labels), and the strip already says board 6's words from obid's `heldBy` (`Panel.tsx` `Control`). Three things are still wrong in the real window on obid. Verify each on `dev` before relying on it:

- **The keyboard stays where it was.** Take control only posts the take (`store.tsx`, live `take`). The page doesn't get the keyboard, so the owner has to click the page before typing. T3 Code's Take control on its streamed surface puts the keyboard on the page, and its release takes it away again (pingdotgg/t3code@304cf149:apps/web/src/browser/ServerBrowserSurface.tsx:225-245 `clearInput` blurs the input, and :750-775 the Take control / Release control button). On a native webview T3 focuses the guest itself (apps/desktop/src/preview/Manager.ts:2482, `if (!wc.isFocused()) wc.focus()`).
- **The ring is under the page.** Board 6 rings the held page (`.br-view.is-held`, `browser.css:95`, an inset `box-shadow`). On obid the page is the tab's `<webview>` in a `position: fixed` `.br-hosted` (`Webviews.tsx`, `browser.css:269`, `z-index: 1`) laid exactly over the slot, so the inset ring is drawn beneath it. The brief calls this an inference: the real-window step below settles it.
- **The agent's cursor still shows while you hold the tab.** `live.ts` drops obid's `pointer` from the tab while `heldBy` is set, but `Hosted` (`Webviews.tsx`) draws main's relay `pointer` whatever the hold.

## Why
The owner takes control to sign in or to stop a wrong click. With the keyboard elsewhere, the first keys they type go to the message box, not the page. With the ring hidden, nothing on the page says it is theirs. With the agent's cursor still moving, it looks as if the agent still drives. Board 6 draws a held page ringed with no agent cursor, and T3 puts the keyboard on the page when control is taken.

## How
Window only, renderer only. Read `factory/jobs/015-w01-08-the-owner-takes-a/build.md` for what obid answers (`{"tabId", "heldBy", "holdGeneration"}`, the refusals) and W01-12's `factory/jobs/011-w01-12-the-browser-panel-s/build.md` for how the panel reaches a tab's webview.

1. **Take control focuses the page, once obid answers.** `live.ts`'s `take` resolves whether obid took it (a 2xx), and `store.tsx`'s live `take` then puts the keyboard on that tab's `<webview>`: a small `focusPage(job, tab)` exported from `Webviews.tsx`, which focuses the tab's mounted webview element only while its slot is on screen (`rect`). A refused take (another device holds it, `held_by_other`; unsigned) moves no focus. Take control to answer and I'll choose in the page take the same path.
   - Spike it first in the real window: if focusing the `<webview>` element doesn't give the guest the keyboard, stop and say so in `build.md`. Main's own `wc.focus()` (T3's way) needs a channel this task doesn't hold.
2. **Give back returns it.** After obid answers the give back, the keyboard goes back to the window's message box: the one a conversation shows (the selector `ChatView.tsx:279` focuses), else Home's (`Home.tsx:408`), read, not changed. The click on Give back is the window's own, so the guest no longer has the keyboard. A refused give back moves nothing.
3. **The ring over the page.** `Hosted` gets `is-held` while its tab is held by you (`tab.holder === 'you'` on an agent's tab). `browser.css` draws board 6's ring on it, above the webview: an `::after` with `inset: 0`, `box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 2px var(--unread)`, `pointer-events: none`, above the webview. The demo's `.br-view.is-held` stays as it is.
4. **No agent cursor while held.** `Hosted` draws no `Pointer` while its tab is held, and drops one that comes in meanwhile.
5. **The contract** (`docs/contracts/browser-app.md`): BROWSERAPP-9's sentence says Take control also puts the keyboard on the page and Give back returns it to the message box, and that on obid the ring is drawn over the page. Add BROWSERAPP-40 and 41 under Acceptance scenarios, implemented, each naming its tests. Classify both in `tools/obid-spec/scope.json`'s `browser-app.md` block (the brief's one exception: only these two ids, in that block).

No word a person sees changes: the strip's sentences, Take control and Give back are on the board and already in `Panel.tsx`.
