The owner's words, 2026-11-15, on seeing the card "Failed · It failed · Give it another round / Stop here / Later" on a job in the Electron app: "I hate this card asking for another round. Claude Code does not have this type of card, it loops and continues. Why do we have this?"

What is wrong. When a job ends `failed`, Oby shows a decision card that offers "Give it another round", "Stop here" and "Later". The agent's own CLI never asks this: it keeps going. The card also shows for failures another round can't fix. The one seen: obid refused to start Claude Code because the account's settings.json sets an env key, and "Give it another round" would fail the same way.

Why it matters. Oby is a dispatcher, not a gatekeeper: the owner asked for the work once and shouldn't be asked again to let it continue. Every such card stops work until someone clicks, which is what made the factory's builds through Oby stall.

How (the owner decides the design; this is the behaviour asked for):
- A job that fails in a way a retry can fix (a passing error, a crashed turn, a failed check with rounds left) goes on by itself, as its rounds and obid's existing retry rules allow, with no card. This holds for a job no app signed too (`POST /v1/jobs`), not only the owner's own work (A00-47 took other cards away for own work only).
- A job that fails in a way a retry can't fix, or that is out of rounds, ends `failed` with its reason as plain words in the task's conversation and on its row, saying what to change. It raises no decision card and no "needs you". The owner starts it again by telling the task something (the follow-up that already exists) or with Try again in the task's menu.
- The safety ceiling and token budget (jobCeilingHours, jobTokenBudget) stay as they are: they end the job with their reason, without a card.
- The Electron app shows no "Give it another round" card; where Sangam's screens have one, the issue for his screen is linked and his words are used for the plain line.

Acceptance:
- A contract scenario (docs/contracts, the area that owns a job's end: recovery or tasks) says a failed job raises no decision, with a test named after it that fails on today's code.
- A test for each: a retryable failure with rounds left starts the next round with no inbox item; a non-retryable failure ends failed with its reason and no inbox item; an unsigned job behaves the same.
- docs/api for the job's `ask`/inbox says the failed card is gone (add-only: no key removed from the API; the value simply stops being sent).
