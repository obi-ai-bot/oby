# docs: rewrite AGENTS.md as non-negotiables, defaults and links, keeping every rule in force

<!-- Roadmap R13-03 · Sprint 3 · R13 How we work · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

engine only

### Outcome

AGENTS.md states each rule in force once, in one or two sentences with a link to its decision. A short 'Non-negotiable' list is apart from defaults that the owner's word changes. The owner reviews the result before merge.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R13-03, milestone R13 How we work).

**Why:** Agents read 46 KB of dated, stacked exceptions. 'Oby forbids nothing' already says most holds are defaults, but the file does not mark which rules are non-negotiable. It also points to `process/working-system.md` in obi-ai-bot/oby-plan, which does not exist on oby-plan main.

**In:**
- A 'Non-negotiable' section proposed for the owner (for example: secret scan before anything leaves; approvals only from the owner's own apps; add-only API; Apache-2.0; agents get no logins or vault secrets) and a 'Defaults' section.
- Every other rule kept, its history moved to docs/decisions/ links.
- The stale oby-plan link fixed or removed.
- A one-line 'Do not argue by citing this file' rule in Oby's words.

**Out:**
- Changing what any rule requires.
- Per-directory files (R13-05, R13-06).
- The 'Proven on 2026-10-09' facts: keep them, they are facts, not history.

### Actors and ownership: what obid owns

- Nothing changes in obid.

### Actors and ownership: what the apps show and send

- Nothing changes for clients.

### Files

- AGENTS.md
- docs/decisions/README.md

### Steps

- Make a table: each rule sentence in AGENTS.md on origin/dev -> where it goes (non-negotiable, default, fact, or history -> decision file).
- Open a `decision` issue with the proposed non-negotiable list; wait for the owner's confirm.
- Rewrite AGENTS.md from the table: the current rule only, each with its decision link.
- Check by diff that no rule left: attach the table to the PR, every row marked kept or moved.
- Ask the owner to review the PR.

### Acceptance scenarios

- None: this task changes no product behaviour.

Contract: none

### Done when

- The owner confirmed the non-negotiable list and approved the PR.
- The PR body carries the rule table; no row is 'dropped'.
- AGENTS.md has no 'replacing', 'until' or 'since the owner's decision of' clause outside a link.
