`core/crates/obid/tests/vault_api.rs`, test `the_apis_json_has_the_vaults_secrets_taken_out`, failed on the factory's gate VM on 2026-11-14 (R03-06's round-2 gate, commit 666ebf4, a branch that only edits a comment in vault.rs). The test waits for a job to end (`ended`), reads `GET /v1/jobs/{id}` as `before`, adds a vault secret, reads the job again as `after` and asserts the two texts are equal. The two answers differed only in `revision` (22 before, 23 after): obid saved the job once more after `ended` returned, so the test compared a job mid-settle with its settled self.

Fix the test, not the product: wait until the job's revision stops changing (or until whatever obid does after a job ends has happened, using `support::within`) before reading `before`, or compare the two answers with `revision` left out, whichever keeps the test's point: that adding a vault secret changes nothing in a clean job's answer. Find what the last save after the end is and say so in the test's comment. Prove it under load (the way R01-15 and R01-16 did) and that the old test fails under the same load. If the extra save turns out to be a real bug (a save that shouldn't happen), say so and fix it in obid instead.

A second vault_api test failed on the same branch's rerun, also on timing: `obid_answers_while_the_vault_scan_reads_a_big_file` asserts every `GET /v1/jobs/{id}` answers in under 5 seconds, and one took 5.95 s while the gate VM ran another job's check in a second slot. Look at whether that test measures obid or the machine's load, and make it robust the same way (or show the slow answer is a real obid stall and fix that).
