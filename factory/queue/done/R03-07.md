# chore(ios): drop the Apple Watch target from the iPhone build, on the owner's yes

<!-- Roadmap R03-07 · Sprint 2 · R03 Delete what has no caller · effort M · risk medium -->

### Sangam's screen

needs Sangam's design first: only to confirm that the iPhone Settings screen without its Watch rows is his (a removal, no new screen)

### Outcome

The owner decides whether the parked Watch app leaves the iPhone build. On yes, the iPhone app no longer carries a Watch app, its relay or its pairing code, and git history keeps them for when a Watch comes back.

### Status and scope

Proposed (roadmap R03-07, milestone R03 Delete what has no caller).

**Why:** docs/trim.md:26 parks the Watch ('not cut and not worked on'). Its without-iPhone path needs /v1/watches and the Watch API that obid does not serve (#454, closed not planned; apps/watch/WatchLink.swift). It was not updated for chat-first. apps/watch has 2,291 Swift lines plus image frames; the iPhone carries WatchRelay.swift (356), WatchPairing.swift (97), PairingApproval.swift (24), apps/shared/WatchWire.swift (121), WatchRelayTests.swift (70) and Watch parts of PhoneSpeech.swift, SettingsScreen.swift and Notifications.swift. Searched apps/ios, apps/shared, apps/watch, core/crates/obid/tests (swift_client.rs does not compile the Watch files) and apps/Oby.xcodeproj (55 lines name the Watch).

**In:**
- Step 1: ask the owner on an issue, with docs/trim.md:26 quoted: remove the Watch target now? Record the answer in docs/trim.md.
- On yes: remove the ObyWatch target and its widget from apps/Oby.xcodeproj; delete apps/watch, WatchRelay.swift, WatchPairing.swift, PairingApproval.swift, WatchWire.swift and WatchRelayTests.swift; remove Runner.swift's watches, pairWatch and unpair calls and PairedWatch; remove the Watch parts of PhoneSpeech.swift and Notifications.swift (WatchRelay.shared.start).
- Remove the Watch rows from apps/ios/app/SettingsScreen.swift (:390-400).
- AGENTS.md 'Build and run' (:113, :121): remove the ObyWatch scheme and apps/watch from the build lines. Keep the rule at :39 that the iPhone and Watch apps stay in Swift on obid's API and that the API stays add-only.
- docs/ARCHITECTURE.md and apps/ios/app/README.md: the Watch is not built.

**Out:**
- Android and Wear OS: R03-08.
- obid: nothing to change; R03-03 removes the /v1/watches allowlist entries and docs whatever the answer is.
- tools/brain/brain.config.ts:122 rule text: it names the Watch with the iPhone, as AGENTS.md:39 does; keep it.

### Steps

- Ask the owner and wait for the answer; write it in docs/trim.md. Ask Sangam to confirm the Settings screen without Watch rows.
- On yes, re-run the caller search on dev (WatchRelay, WatchWire, PairedWatch, WCSession, ObyWatch) and put it in the PR body.
- Remove the target in Xcode (not by hand in the pbxproj), then delete the files.
- Build ObyPhone and run iOSTests; run `cargo test --test swift_client` in core/crates/obid.
- On a real iPhone: install over the previous build, open Home and Settings, talk with the mic, and receive a push.
