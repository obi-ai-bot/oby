## What
Someone who mostly reviews gets everything that waits for them mixed into Home's stack (`AskStack.tsx`). The side panel should have a "Waiting for you" row with a count, opening one list across the profile's projects: pull requests to merge (with Oby's summary, checks, approvals by tool, lines changed), permissions, and questions, each with its action (Merge, Allow once or Deny, Answer).

## Why
- **A manager or CTO needs one queue** (the plan, §4), not cards mixed with job questions.
- **No new state:** it is a view over what obid already sends.

## How
1. A row under New conversation, "Waiting for you" and its count, shown only when the count is above zero.
2. The list is `GET /v1/inbox` items with `triage: needs_you` and the merge cards (MERGING-21), longest waiting first, with an "All projects" chip to narrow to one.
3. Merge sends the signed Merge (docs/contracts/signed-words.md), Allow once and Deny answer the permission, Answer opens the conversation.
