### Status and scope

Proposed (new, R01-13's flaky list).

**Why:** `long_jobs` `recovery_12_an_idle_jobs_first_cut_comes_after_one_window` is on the flaky list in `core/.config/nextest.toml` (R01-13). Under load the agent's start takes longer, and the gap passes 12.

Evidence (factory job logs on the factory's Mac, `factory/jobs/`):
- It failed in R01-08's run of the planned test binaries, a change that didn't touch recovery, and failed the same way on a clean `origin/dev` checkout (f3ad6e17) under the same load (job 020, build.md).

**In:**
- Measure what RECOVERY-12 promises (the first cut comes after one window, not two) from where the window starts, the moment the agent went quiet, rather than from the round's start; or bound it so the agent's start-up doesn't count.
- Remove its entry from `core/.config/nextest.toml` in the same pull request.
