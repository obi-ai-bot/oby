## What
`rooms_11_a_post_read_back_before_its_answer_counts_once` (core/crates/obid/tests/rooms.rs, around line 1078) fails now and then at its assertion near line 1099 with `left: 1, right: 2`. The test reads the job first and the room's posts second. obid's `room_post_sent` (core/crates/obid/src/room_link.rs) marks a post `sent` before it records it on the job through `jobs.room_posted`, so the test can read both posts as `sent` while the job it read a moment earlier lists only one. B00-37 put it on the flaky list until this lands.

## Why
It failed the gate of A00-70 (round 7, on this Mac's shard) and of R12-05, at the same line with the same values, on branches that don't touch rooms. A test that fails at random holds real work back from merging and teaches people to ignore a red check.

## How
Make the test observe one consistent moment: either obid records the post on the job before it marks it `sent` (if the order is something an owner could see through the API), or the test waits with `support::within` until the job and the posts agree before it compares them. Decide from what an owner sees. Then remove its entry from the flaky list in `core/.config/nextest.toml` in the same change (docs/contracts/ci.md, CI-26 to 28).

Acceptance: the test passes 50 runs in a row on this Mac while another Rust build runs, and its flaky-list entry is gone.
